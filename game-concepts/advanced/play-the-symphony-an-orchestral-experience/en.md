# Play The Symphony: An Orchestral Experience

## Concept

A rhythm and music simulation game inspired by Sound! Euphonium (Hibike! Euphonium), in which the player experiences what it is like to be part of an orchestra. They can choose from a variety of instruments and perform their respective sheet music while following the conductor and other musicians in real time.

Each instrument offers its own gameplay experience, with specific sheet music, playing techniques, difficulty levels, and musical responsibilities. The player can experiment with different instruments, switching between strings, woodwinds, brass, and percussion, learning their unique characteristics and contributing to the performance of complete musical works.

The game also features a career mode in which the player begins their musical journey and develops as a member of an orchestra. Throughout their journey, they participate in rehearsals, auditions, performances, and competitions, improving their skills, expanding their repertoire, and earning opportunities to join more prestigious musical ensembles.

Each performance involves playing an entire musical piece, requiring rhythmic precision, music reading, dynamic control, and perfectly timed entries. The player must follow the conductor and stay in sync with the other musicians, respecting their role within the ensemble.

Rather than relying exclusively on an individual score, performance is reflected in the overall quality of the ensemble and the audience's reaction. A technically correct performance without proper integration with the orchestra does not necessarily constitute a great performance.

The goal is not to be the protagonist, but to develop one's own musical abilities and contribute to an impeccable ensemble performance.

## Objective

Build a musical career, master different instruments, and earn recognition as an orchestra member by participating in increasingly demanding performances and contributing to the excellence of the ensemble.

## Core Mechanics

- Instrument selection across different orchestral families, including strings, woodwinds, brass, and percussion.
- Instrument-specific sheet music, featuring unique playing challenges and techniques.
- Rhythm and music-reading system requiring precision, synchronization, and dynamic control.
- Real-time coordination with the conductor and other musicians, following entrances, pauses, tempo, and dynamics throughout each piece.
- Performance of complete musical works with varying difficulty levels and repertoires.
- Career mode featuring musical progression, auditions, rehearsals, performances, competitions, and opportunities to join more prestigious orchestras.
- Ability to experiment with and develop proficiency in multiple instruments throughout the career.
- Rehearsal mode for practicing specific passages, studying sheet music, and improving performance.
- Ensemble performance evaluation based on execution quality and audience reaction.
- Online cooperation, allowing different players to take on different instruments and form a virtual orchestra.

## References

- Sound! Euphonium (Hibike! Euphonium): musicians' everyday lives, rehearsals, performances, and relationships within a band.
- Rock Band: rhythm-based musical performance and synchronization.
- Guitar Hero: reading musical sequences and execution precision.
- Maestro: musical conducting and interpretation experiences.
