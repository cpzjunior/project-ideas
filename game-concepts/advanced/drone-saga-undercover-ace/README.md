# Drone Saga: Undercover Ace

## Concept

An action-adventure game in which the player takes on the role of a drone enthusiast who starts competing in local tournaments for fun. His exceptional drone piloting skills attract attention after a series of tournament victories, sparking the interest of police and military organizations.

Over time, the protagonist is recruited, or practically forced, to carry out secret infiltration, reconnaissance, and intelligence-gathering missions using his piloting skills. While maintaining his public career in the competitive circuit, he begins living a double life, alternating between tournaments, sponsorships, and sporting events and high-risk clandestine operations.

Throughout his career, the player improves their skills, develops new piloting techniques, and modifies their drones to meet the increasingly demanding requirements of competitions and secret missions. The challenge lies in balancing growing fame as a professional pilot with the dangers of an activity that must remain completely unknown to the public.

## Objective

Build a successful career in the competitive drone racing circuit while carrying out secret missions for government organizations, developing skills and equipment to become the best pilot in both fields.

## Main Mechanics

- Drone competitions: participate in local and professional tournaments, facing pilots with different styles and skill levels.
- Piloting: control drones through courses featuring obstacles, maneuvers, and precision challenges.
- Secret missions: carry out infiltration, reconnaissance, and intelligence-gathering operations.
- Double life: balance a public competitive career with clandestine activities.
- Drone customization: modify components, cameras, sensors, motors, and control systems.
- Professional progression: earn victories, reputation, sponsorships, and access to more prestigious competitions.
- Operational progression: receive increasingly complex and risky missions as the organizations' trust grows.
- Reputation management: maintain a positive public image while keeping secret activities away from the spotlight.

## References

- FPV Drone Racing competitions.
- Drone piloting simulators.
- Espionage and infiltration games.
