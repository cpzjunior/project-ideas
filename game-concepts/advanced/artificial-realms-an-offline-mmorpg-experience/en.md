# Artificial Realms: An Offline MMORPG Experience

## Concept

An RPG inspired by Erenshor and Ultima Online mods such as Ruins & Riches, aiming to deliver the experience of an MMORPG without requiring real human interaction. The concept caters to players who prefer solo experiences, as well as casual players or those with limited time, allowing them to enjoy the social dynamics of an MMO without depending on other players.

The world is inhabited by NPCs who believe they are real players. Each has their own personality, memories, reputation, and goals, forming a living and unpredictable virtual society. These characters interact with one another through chaotic conversations, rumors, disputes, and an emergent economy, reacting to world events as if they were participating in a genuine online server.

This simulation also creates humorous situations: since NPCs believe they are real players, they may suspect that the protagonist is actually a bot controlled by the server. When they observe the protagonist repeatedly farming, following predictable routes, or performing tasks in an automated manner, they begin reporting them to the Game Master (GM). Complaints spread through the chats, with NPCs demanding action, accusing server administrators of negligence, and complaining that nobody is doing anything about the bots, unaware that they are reporting the only human player in that world.

The player can form parties with these NPCs to explore dungeons, complete quests, and engage in cooperative combat or simulated PvP against other groups. The result is an entirely offline MMORPG in which the entire community is artificial but socially convincing and dynamic, allowing the player to participate at their own pace and disconnect at any time.

## Objective

Explore a persistent and dynamic world, develop the character, and participate in typical MMORPG activities by forming groups with NPCs, exploring dungeons, completing quests, and facing other groups in cooperative combat or simulated PvP.

## Main Mechanics

- **Player-like NPCs:** Artificial characters with individual personalities, memories, reputations, and goals.
- **Dynamic virtual society:** NPCs interact with one another through chat, rumors, alliances, rivalries, and disputes.
- **Emergent economy:** Trade and economic activity influenced by NPC decisions and behavior.
- **Party formation:** Ability to recruit NPCs into exploration and combat groups.
- **Dungeons and quests:** Traditional MMORPG activities performed with entirely artificial groups.
- **Cooperative combat:** Group encounters in which NPCs act according to their individual traits and behaviors.
- **Simulated PvP:** Battles against NPC-controlled groups, reproducing the competitive dynamics of online servers.
- **Individual progression:** Character development without depending on the availability or cooperation of human players.
- **Flexible, asynchronous interaction:** Ability to enter and leave the world at any time, without social commitments or shared schedules.

## References
