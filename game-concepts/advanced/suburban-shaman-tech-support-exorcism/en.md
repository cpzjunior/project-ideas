# Suburban Shaman: Tech Support Exorcism

## Concept

A supernatural adventure, simulation, and comedy game in which the player takes on the role of a freelance shaman handling paranormal incidents in an apparently ordinary suburb. Instead of battling powerful demons or saving the world, their job is to solve the neighborhood's small and absurd spiritual problems.

Modern-day spirits are everywhere: poltergeists interfere with Wi-Fi router signals, curses affect household appliances, and elementals get trapped in plumbing, causing leaks and other domestic inconveniences. Each residence presents a unique supernatural problem, requiring investigation, diagnosis, and an appropriate combination of traditional rituals and adapted technology.

The protagonist uses sacred herbs, amulets, offerings, and ancestral ceremonies, but also relies on spiritual detection apps, improvised electronic equipment, and digital tools to locate and identify supernatural manifestations.

Between jobs, the shaman must run their small business, earn the residents' trust, deal with eccentric clients, and make enough money to pay the rent on their studio. After all, ghost removal is a profession like any other, and the bills keep coming even when the afterlife decides to cause trouble.

## Objective

Resolve supernatural incidents throughout the neighborhood, build a solid reputation as a freelance shaman, and keep the business running while balancing customer satisfaction, available resources, and personal expenses.

## Core Mechanics

- Investigation of paranormal incidents in suburban homes and businesses.
- Identification of supernatural entities and manifestations through clues, observation, and specialized equipment.
- Exorcisms and rituals adapted to modern problems, such as poltergeists in routers, curses affecting household appliances, and elementals in plumbing.
- Combination of traditional spiritual practices with paranormal detection apps and devices.
- Preparation of rituals using herbs, amulets, offerings, and other resources.
- Diagnosis and resolution of problems through different approaches, depending on the entity and environment.
- Customer service for clients with different personalities, expectations, and levels of tolerance for the supernatural.
- Business management, including payments, expenses, equipment purchases, and studio maintenance.
- Reputation system based on service quality and resident satisfaction.
- Unexpected incidents that can turn seemingly simple service calls into absurd situations.

## References

- Ghostbusters: investigation and containment of supernatural manifestations using technological equipment.
- Luigi's Mansion: exploration of haunted environments and interaction with ghostly entities.
- The Sims: suburban life, residences, and domestic situations.
- Phasmophobia: paranormal investigation and the use of equipment to identify entities.
