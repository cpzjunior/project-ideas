# The Grand Schemer: The World is My Masterpiece

## Concept

A strategy, adventure, and social manipulation game set in a fictional kingdom obsessed with artistic prestige, intellectual recognition, and cultural acclaim. The protagonist is a young man rejected by the Imperial Academy who decides to abandon the pursuit of conventional recognition and turn reality itself into his greatest masterpiece.

A master of disguises, intrigue, and manipulation, he begins secretly interfering in political and social events, influencing reputations, provoking conflicts, and changing the destinies of different characters. Instead of using canvases, sculptures, or traditional instruments, his art consists of manipulating collective perception and transforming real events into carefully orchestrated compositions.

Every conspiracy, scandal, alliance, or rivalry represents a new brushstroke in an invisible work of art. The protagonist uses false identities, manipulated information, staged events, and carefully positioned characters to steer events in the desired direction.

However, as his influence grows, his creations become increasingly eccentric and complex. The protagonist begins orchestrating ever more absurd and chaotic situations, connecting seemingly unrelated events into a narrative that only he can interpret as part of a greater artistic vision.

While the kingdom struggles to understand the source of its growing instability, the true artist remains hidden, watching his creation come to life. The challenge lies in continuing to manipulate events without revealing his identity, while maintaining the coherence of a creation that may be beginning to escape its creator's control.

The game explores the boundary between artistic genius, obsession, and eccentricity, questioning whether the true masterpiece lies in controlling events or in how people interpret them.

## Objective

Create a living work of art through the manipulation of political and social events, influencing characters, reputations, and relationships without revealing one's identity. The player seeks to expand their influence and transform the kingdom into an increasingly elaborate artistic composition while keeping their authorship concealed.

## Core Mechanics

- Disguise and alternative identity system for infiltrating different social and political circles.
- Manipulation of information, reputations, and collective perceptions.
- Planning and execution of conspiracies to influence events and relationships between characters.
- Interference in political, social, and cultural events through staged scenarios and indirect manipulation.
- Character system featuring individual interests, ambitions, relationships, and reactions.
- Creation of connections between seemingly independent events, generating emergent narratives.
- Management of personal identity and exposure risk as the mastermind behind the manipulations.
- Influence progression across different groups and institutions within the kingdom.
- Increasingly eccentric and unpredictable events as the protagonist's creation grows more complex.
- Emergent consequences that can disrupt original plans and produce unexpected outcomes.
- Artistic interpretation system in which the protagonist assigns meaning and coherence to his creations.

## References

- The Great Ace Attorney: intrigue, eccentric characters, and interconnected events.
- Hitman: disguises, infiltration, and manipulation of situations to achieve objectives.
- Crusader Kings: political intrigue, dynastic relationships, and emergent consequences.
- The Stanley Parable: metafiction, interpretation of events, and absurdist humor.
- The Invisible Hand: system manipulation and influence over behavior and decisions.
