# MOBA Minion: Escape The System

## Concept

An action-adventure and exploration game with metalinguistic elements, in which the player controls a minion trapped in an eternal MOBA, condemned to endlessly repeat the same cycles of spawning, marching down lanes, and dying in battles that never end.

Amid the simulation's automated routines, the protagonist awakens to consciousness and realizes the true nature of their existence: they are not a player, hero, or voluntary participant in the battle, but merely a disposable resource used by the system to fuel an endless war.

Breaking away from their original programming turns them into an anomaly. Their own allies attempt to correct their behavior and guide them back to their lane, while enemy minions and heroes pose constant threats. The system itself detects the deviation and begins working to eliminate the anomaly, restoring the established order.

To escape, the protagonist must exploit glitches in the simulation, discover inaccessible areas of the map, and collect items capable of breaking the world's fundamental rules. Along the way, they discover that the crystal located at the center of the enemy base, traditionally the ultimate objective of any match, may hold the only possibility of permanently breaking the cycle.

The adventure transforms familiar MOBA elements into obstacles and opportunities. Lanes, towers, minion waves, neutral monsters, abilities, and defensive structures are no longer just components of a match; they become the rules of a world from which the protagonist is trying to escape.

## Objective

Escape the endless cycle of battles by exploring glitches in the simulation, overcoming the forces attempting to preserve the original programming, and reaching the enemy base's crystal to discover a way to permanently leave the MOBA behind.

## Core Mechanics

- Exploration of a map inspired by traditional MOBA layouts, including lanes, jungle, bases, towers, and hidden areas.
- Survival system in an environment where allied and enemy minions, heroes, and defensive structures pose threats.
- Gradual awakening of consciousness, allowing the protagonist to perform actions that contradict their original programming.
- Allied minion behavior system, in which minions attempt to guide the protagonist back to their lanes and battle cycles.
- Exploration of simulation glitches, allowing access to regions, paths, and features that should not be available.
- Collection of items and artifacts that modify or break the world's rules.
- Progression of skills and abilities that allow the protagonist to face threats and explore new possibilities.
- Pursuit and correction system, in which the system itself reacts to the protagonist's abnormal actions.
- Interaction with different MOBA characters and entities, including minions, neutral monsters, heroes, and structures.
- Gradual discovery of the mechanisms that keep the battle cycle running.
- Final confrontation at the enemy base, with the crystal serving as the central element in a possible escape.

## References

- League of Legends and Dota 2: map layouts, lanes, minions, towers, heroes, and traditional MOBA objectives.
- The Stanley Parable: metalinguistic elements, subversion of expectations, and questioning the rules established by the game.
- The Matrix: awakening of consciousness and discovery of the artificial nature of reality.
- Free Guy: a non-player character who develops consciousness and challenges the way the virtual world operates.
