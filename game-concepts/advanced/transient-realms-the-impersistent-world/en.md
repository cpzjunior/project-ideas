# Transient Realms: The Impermanent World

## Concept

A fantasy RPG with elements of drama and procedural exploration, set in a world where nothing is permanent. Places appear and disappear without warning, cities change their layouts, people assume different identities, and events are replaced as though they had never existed. Only the protagonist remains aware that reality was once different.

Since childhood, he has noticed these disruptions. A house he once knew may have a completely different architecture the next day. Familiar people take on different faces, histories, and relationships. Places disappear, while others emerge in exactly the same locations. His memories are often vague and fragmented, but one unsettling certainty remains: the world around him is no longer the same.

For years, he tries to understand these inconsistencies, until one day he returns home to find an unrecognizable reality. There are no signs of his parents, his family, or anyone who should have been part of his life. The house may still exist, but its inhabitants and history have been replaced. He retains fragments of memories, such as faces, names, and small everyday gestures, but cannot find anyone who can confirm that those people ever existed.

The protagonist is not simply forgetting his past: the world itself seems to be erasing and replacing its history. While everyone accepts the current reality as though it had always been that way, he is the only witness to the existence of other versions.

From that moment on, he sets out in search of answers across territories that never remain the same. Along his journey, he meets people, builds friendships, forms bonds, and finds his place in the world, knowing that all of it could disappear at any moment. Family, companions, places, and accomplishments are all subject to the same impermanence.

The protagonist is the only constant in a universe incapable of preserving its own history. As he tries to understand the origin of this phenomenon, he must confront the possibility that nothing he builds will survive, except for fragments of his memories.

## Objective

Explore a constantly changing world, investigate the nature of its impermanence, and discover why the protagonist is the only being capable of perceiving changes in reality. Throughout the journey, build relationships, achieve goals, and search for meaning in a life where everything can disappear.

## Core Mechanics

- Procedural generation of territories, cities, dungeons, and regions, with constant changes throughout the world.
- Dynamic changes to locations, characters, cultures, and events as exploration progresses.
- Persistent memories belonging to the protagonist, allowing him to recognize remnants of previous versions of the world.
- Characters whose identities, histories, and relationships can change or disappear between different versions of reality.
- Development of relationships and family and social bonds that remain subject to the world's impermanence.
- Progression of skills, equipment, and achievements in a universe where even the results of the journey can be erased.
- Investigation of anomalies and fragments of reality to understand the rules behind these transformations.
- Narrative decisions that explore the value of relationships, memories, and experiences in the face of impermanence.

## References

- Daggerfall: procedural generation of a vast world and exploration of dynamic regions.
- Kenshi: a world that evolves independently of the protagonist, with factions, characters, and events that develop over time.
- Outward: exploration, survival, and progression in a hostile fantasy world.
- Planescape: Torment: philosophical narrative exploring identity, memory, and existence.
