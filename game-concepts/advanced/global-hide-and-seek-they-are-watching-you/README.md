# Global Hide and Seek: They Are Watching You

## Concept

An adventure, survival, and strategy game inspired by GeoGuessr. A professional thief breaks into a military base and steals an experimental teleportation device. During his escape, however, the equipment malfunctions and begins transporting him to random locations around the world, with no precise control over his destination.

Now being hunted by military forces using satellites and advanced surveillance systems to track him, the protagonist must learn to survive in this completely unpredictable situation.

Each jump takes him to a new location, which could be a bustling city, an isolated road, a desert region, or even an extremely hostile environment. To escape, he must observe his surroundings, identify his location using signs, languages, architecture, vegetation, and other geographical clues, and find an opportunity to activate the device again.

The problem is that staying in one place for too long increases the chances of being detected. Every decision involves balancing the need to gather enough information to plan the next escape against the urgency of disappearing before the pursuers arrive.

## Objective

Escape the military pursuit and find a way to stabilize the teleportation device, using geographical knowledge, observational skills, and strategic decisions to survive unpredictable jumps.

## Core Mechanics

- Teleport to random locations around the world.
- Identify geographical locations through landscapes, signs, languages, architecture, and vegetation.
- Explore and observe the environment to determine the current location.
- Monitor approaching military forces through satellite surveillance.
- Manage the time available before being detected.
- Strategically choose when to activate the next teleportation.
- Deal with an unstable device with limitations and potential malfunctions.
- Face region-specific situations and challenges depending on where the player appears.
- Narrative progression involving the investigation of the device and the attempt to escape for good.
