# Royal Snatch: Rebel Rush

## Concept

An action-adventure comedy set in the fictional kingdom of Valdoria, an apparently prosperous monarchy plagued by inequality, economic exploitation, and the greed of its royal family.

Following an excellent harvest, the population continues to starve due to the exorbitant taxes imposed by King Edmund IV, who amasses wealth while his subjects can barely afford bread. Extremely stingy, the monarch decides to trade his own daughter, Princess Isadora, in marriage to Prince Gaston III, an arrogant, unpleasant suitor known for his unbearable cheese-like smell. In exchange, the king will receive a mountain of gold.

Desperate to escape both the marriage and the responsibility of inheriting a miserable kingdom, Isadora finds an unexpected opportunity when a revolutionary storms the wedding ceremony and kidnaps her. To the kidnapper's surprise, the princess accepts the situation and actively helps with the escape. While the protagonist believes he is leading a great revolution, Isadora reveals that she has interests of her own and a particular talent for taking advantage of chaos for personal gain.

Upon discovering what happened, Edmund summons his knights to rescue his daughter, offering a generous reward. However, unable to bear the thought of giving away any of his gold, he secretly begins sabotaging his own rescue teams. Meanwhile, Gaston hires mercenaries to recover his future wife and ensure that the marriage agreement is fulfilled.

The escape turns into a chaotic chase through starving villages, black markets, and roads filled with absurd tolls. Incompetent knights, greedy mercenaries, corrupt officials, and an increasingly outraged population clash as events spiral completely out of the protagonists' control.

Gradually, the individual pursuit evolves into a popular uprising against the monarchy. Royal granaries are looted, secrets of the royal family come to light, and Valdoria descends into a revolution as unexpected as it is disastrous.

In the end, the protagonist may even succeed in overthrowing the monarchy, but Isadora reveals that she always had plans of her own. The princess disappears with part of the royal treasury, leaving behind only a farewell letter and one final piece of advice: never marry someone who smells like cheese.

## Objective

Lead Princess Isadora's escape, evade her pursuers, and survive the political and social chaos unfolding throughout the kingdom, while the protagonist attempts to turn a simple kidnapping into a popular revolution.

## Core Mechanics

- Action-adventure gameplay featuring exploration of villages, black markets, roads, and noble estates.
- Chase system, with knights, mercenaries, and other forces attempting to capture the protagonists.
- Two protagonists with distinct personalities and interests, enabling interactions and comedic situations during the escape.
- Skills and resources used to evade pursuers, overcome obstacles, and improvise solutions.
- Interaction with the kingdom's inhabitants, including peasants, merchants, criminals, revolutionaries, and members of the nobility.
- Reputation and popular influence system, affecting how different groups react to the protagonists.
- Missions and events involving abusive taxation, corruption, food shortages, and the exploitation of the population.
- Pursuers with conflicting goals and motivations, including knights seeking the reward, mercenaries interested in payment, and a king secretly sabotaging his own teams.
- Dynamic events that intensify the popular uprising and transform the escape into a conflict of greater proportions.
- Narrative decisions and events that influence the revolution's development and its outcome.

## References

- Shrek: medieval fantasy humor, eccentric characters, and subversion of fairy tales.
- The Princess Bride: adventure, romance, chases, and comedy in a fantasy kingdom.
- Donkey Kong Country: platforming action, obstacles, and challenges throughout level progression.
- Monty Python and the Holy Grail: satire of medieval society, incompetent authorities, and absurd humor.
