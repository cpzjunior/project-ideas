# Heroic Bloodline: Beyond a Single Life

## Concept

A fantasy RPG with dynastic strategy and management elements, set in a doomed kingdom that must be saved by a lineage of heroes.

In this world, every action directly consumes the character's lifespan. Exploring regions, fighting creatures, building structures, training, and developing skills all accelerate aging. Lifespan is therefore the game's most valuable and limited resource. Every decision requires evaluating whether the benefits gained justify the years sacrificed.

The player takes on the role of the founder of a bloodline tasked with saving the kingdom. A mission too great to accomplish within a single lifetime, but possible to complete across multiple generations. To achieve this, the player must build a family, establish alliances through strategic marriages, and prepare heirs capable of continuing the work of their predecessors.

Each generation inherits part of the family's legacy, including resources, knowledge, properties, equipment, and relationships. However, descendants also have their own characteristics, talents, and limitations, requiring new strategies to make the most of their potential.

The player must balance dangerous expeditions, family development, resource management, and successor preparation. Some adventures may offer extraordinary rewards but consume a significant portion of the character's lifespan. Others may be less profitable but allow the character to live long enough to establish and prepare a new generation.

The kingdom's salvation does not depend on an immortal hero, but on an entire family passing its legacy down from generation to generation. Each character contributes to the mission, even knowing that their time will eventually run out.

## Objective

Save the kingdom across multiple generations by developing a bloodline capable of accumulating enough resources, knowledge, alliances, and power to overcome the threat that endangers its very existence.

## Core Mechanics

- Aging and Life Expectancy System: every action consumes a portion of the character's remaining lifespan.
- Exploration, Expeditions, and Adventures: venture into different regions with varying levels of risk, reward, and lifespan cost.
- Combat: fight creatures and enemies while accounting for the time consumed by battles.
- Skill and Attribute Development: improve abilities through training that requires sacrificing years of life.
- Family Formation: establish strategic marriages and alliances between bloodlines.
- Dynastic Succession System: continue the journey through successive descendants.
- Intergenerational Inheritance: pass down resources, knowledge, properties, equipment, and relationships across generations.
- Individual Heir Traits: descendants have unique characteristics, talents, and limitations that influence their potential.
- Successor Preparation: pass on knowledge and develop heirs' abilities before succession.
- Wealth and Resource Management: manage the assets and resources accumulated by the family over generations.
- Narrative Progression: advance the story through the achievements and decisions of different members of the bloodline.
- Evolving Events and Threats: face challenges that develop over time, pressuring the family to make progress before it is too late.

## References

- Fantasy RPGs featuring character progression and exploration.
- Crusader Kings: dynasties, strategic marriages, succession, and family relationships.
- Rogue Legacy: progression through successive generations of characters.
- Resource management and long-term planning games.
