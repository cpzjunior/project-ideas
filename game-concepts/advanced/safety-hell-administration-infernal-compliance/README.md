# Safety Hell Administration: Infernal Compliance

## Concept

A simulation and management game with satirical humor, in which the player takes on the role of the Inferno's Occupational Safety Technician. Their mission is to ensure that even the most condemned souls, demons, and supernatural entities comply with occupational safety regulations.

Amid the chaos of Hell, working conditions are deplorable: demons operate machinery without protective equipment, boilers explode, torturers ignore procedures, and Satan himself believes he is above labor laws.

The player's daily routine involves inspecting facilities, identifying hazards, issuing fines, shutting down departments, and filling out endless reports. The challenges are as absurd as the environment itself: adapting helmets for horned demons, sanitizing torture forks, installing emergency exits in infinite labyrinths, and filing Work-Related Accident Reports (CATs) for immortal employees.

In addition to physical hazards, the player must deal with employees outraged by the new requirements, demonic managers disputing fines, and a finance department that considers investments in safety a waste of resources. To make matters worse, surprise audits by celestial angels can put the entire infernal administration under scrutiny.

Every decision directly affects Hell's safety, productivity, and budget. The player must balance regulatory compliance, operational efficiency, and political resistance within a millennia-old organization that has never had to worry about labor laws.

The greatest challenge will not be controlling the forces of darkness, but convincing ancient creatures that wearing safety boots is not optional. After all, suffering may be eternal, but workplace accidents don't have to be!

## Objective

Manage occupational safety throughout Hell, reducing accidents, ensuring regulatory compliance, and keeping operations running despite resistance from demons, infernal managers, and the administration itself.

## Core Mechanics

- Safety inspections across different infernal facilities, identifying hazardous conditions and regulatory violations.
- Identification and classification of occupational risks, taking into account the supernatural nature of the activities.
- Issuing fines, warnings, and shutdown orders for departments that fail to comply with regulations.
- Management of personal protective equipment (PPE), including adaptations for demonic anatomies and species.
- Workplace accident investigations and filing CATs for immortal employees.
- Preparation of technical reports, documentation, and compliance procedures.
- Upgrading facilities, machinery, and processes, including installing emergency exits in impossible environments.
- Budget and productivity management, negotiating safety investments with the infernal administration.
- Employee and conflict management, dealing with resistance to regulations and negligent behavior.
- Surprise audits by celestial angels, evaluating regulatory compliance and the quality of safety management.
- Consequence system in which accidents, productivity, costs, and compliance are affected by the player's decisions.

## References

- Two Point Hospital: simulation and management featuring absurd situations and humor.
- Good Job!: improvised problem-solving in workplace environments.
