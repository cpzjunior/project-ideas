# Vessel Conflict: The Masquerade of Centuries

## Concept

An action, strategy, and psychological thriller game starring two brothers born with an extraordinary ability: transferring their consciousness into any human body and completely assuming their hosts' physical identities.

Initially, both brothers share this ability but follow opposing paths. The older brother becomes corrupted and begins using it to manipulate entire societies, infiltrating institutions, governments, and organizations with the goal of establishing global dominance. His influence grows silently across generations, while his true identity remains concealed.

The younger brother initially tries to stop this threat without abandoning his moral principles. However, he realizes that his own longevity and ability to transfer consciousness are not enough to ensure that his successors will be able to contain his brother's growing influence. Distrusting the resilience of those who might carry on his mission, he decides to abandon all moral restraints and adopt the same cruel methods of survival and infiltration.

Consciousness transfer begins occurring at the moment of death, eliminating the original host and allowing both brothers to survive across generations without depending on a lineage of successors. Thus, the brothers become opposing yet equally powerful forces, using bodies, identities, and social positions as instruments in an invisible war spanning centuries.

As the conflict progresses, the distinction between hero and villain becomes increasingly unclear. Both manipulate people, eliminate obstacles, and sacrifice lives in pursuit of their goals. The struggle is no longer simply about controlling the world; it begins to question the very nature of human identity: if a consciousness can survive in different bodies, what truly defines a person?

## Objective

Follow the centuries-long war between the two brothers as they infiltrate different societies and institutions, assume new identities, and interfere with historical events. Players must thwart the older brother's plans while confronting the consequences of adopting methods increasingly similar to those of their enemy.

## Core Mechanics

- Consciousness transfer between human bodies, allowing players to assume different identities.
- Host system featuring distinct physical traits, abilities, professions, and social positions.
- Consciousness transfer at the moment of death, eliminating the original host.
- Infiltration of organizations, governments, institutions, and social groups.
- Political, economic, and social manipulation to influence events.
- Historical progression through different periods, societies, and cultural contexts.
- Investigation of identities, tracking of movements, and anticipation of the rival brother's strategies.
- Planning of covert operations using different hosts and their social positions.
- Moral and strategic decisions reflecting the protagonist's transformation and growing adoption of the antagonist's methods.
- Indirect confrontations between the brothers, in which information, influence, and the ability to anticipate their opponent are just as important as physical strength.

## References

- Assassin's Creed: historical conspiracies, secret societies, and manipulation of events across centuries.
- Hitman: infiltration, disguises, and the use of different identities to carry out operations.
- The Occupation: investigation, espionage, and information manipulation.
- Altered Carbon: consciousness transfer between bodies and questions surrounding personal identity.
- Metal Gear Solid: conspiracies, political manipulation, and ideological conflicts.
