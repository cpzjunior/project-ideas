# Virtù: The Necessary Evil

## Concept

An action RPG featuring elements of strategy, stealth, and political intrigue. The protagonist travels thirty years into the past with a single goal: eliminate the Great Leader before he rises to power and prevent the dictatorship responsible for destroying his family.

However, the past resists attempts to change it. Every intervention triggers unexpected consequences, unleashing conflicts, political instability, and even greater violence. Realizing that simply eliminating the tyrant will not be enough to prevent the country's collapse, the protagonist makes a radical decision: seize power for himself.

His journey shifts from a mission of revenge to a campaign for political and military ascension. Starting with virtually nothing, he must build a network of allies, recruit agents, gain influence, and confront organizations that control different sectors of society. Through covert operations, infiltration, sabotage, and armed confrontations, he secures strategic positions and eliminates his adversaries.

Inspired by the political philosophy of Niccolò Machiavelli, particularly the concepts of virtù and necessity, the protagonist believes that the stability of the State requires ruthless decisions. His adaptability, strategic intelligence, and willingness to act without moral constraints become essential tools for achieving his goals.

As he gains power, he begins to reproduce the same methods he initially intended to fight against. The line between liberator and tyrant becomes increasingly blurred, while his decisions shape the country's future and determine the kind of regime that will emerge.

The central question is no longer simply how to prevent a dictator from rising to power, but how far someone can transform into the very thing they swore to destroy in the name of a supposed greater good.

## Objective

Build an organization capable of challenging the existing regime, gain control of strategic institutions, and seize state power. Throughout the campaign, the protagonist must overcome political and military adversaries, make strategic decisions, and deal with the consequences of his own transformation.

## Core Mechanics

- Third-person combat featuring firearms, melee weapons, and military equipment.
- Infiltration of government facilities, military bases, and underground organizations.
- Sabotage, espionage, rescue, pursuit, and target elimination operations.
- Recruitment and development of a network of allies, agents, and collaborators.
- Formation and expansion of a political and military organization.
- Conquest of territories, institutions, and strategic positions.
- Information manipulation, exploitation of rivalries, and negotiations with different factions.
- Decisions involving alliances, betrayals, repression, and the use of force.
- Dynamic consequences resulting from interventions in the timeline.
- Protagonist progression in combat, leadership, infiltration, and strategic skills.
- Organizational development based on the resources, territories, and influence acquired.

## References

- Mafia: narrative of rising to power, conflicts between organizations, and morally ambiguous characters.
- Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain: infiltration, military operations, and organization building.
- Far Cry 4: political conflict, factions, and armed resistance.
- Watch Dogs: infiltration, system manipulation, and covert operations.
- The Saboteur: resistance, sabotage, and combat in occupied territory.
- Niccolò Machiavelli, The Prince: philosophical inspiration for the concepts of virtù, necessity, and the exercise of power.
