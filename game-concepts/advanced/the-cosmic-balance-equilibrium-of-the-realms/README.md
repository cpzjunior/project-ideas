# The Cosmic Balance: Equilibrium of the Realms

## Concept

A dark fantasy and surreal action RPG set in a fragmented multiverse, composed of planes of existence organized into a cosmic structure inspired by the concept of the Great Wheel. Each plane has its own characteristics, physical laws, civilizations, and creatures, forming a network of interdependent realities.

The player assumes the role of a divine avatar created by a primordial entity that governs the structure of the multiverse. From the moment of its creation, the protagonist has a single purpose: to traverse the different planes, gather fragments of power, and confront their own creator to take their place.

Each plane presents a unique reality, with fantastical environments, impossible architecture, grotesque creatures, and challenges that explore different aspects of existence. Some worlds are decaying realms dominated by colossal entities, while others challenge the very perception of reality, featuring incomprehensible physical laws and supernatural phenomena.

Exploration allows the player to discover passages between planes, forgotten civilizations, ancient ruins, and fragments of primordial essence. These fragments not only strengthen the protagonist but also allow them to alter fundamental aspects of reality, reshaping its structure and influencing the fate of its inhabitants.

The creator is not necessarily an evil entity. They genuinely believe that absolute control is essential to preserving existence. In their view, the multiverse is inherently chaotic, and the unrestricted freedom of its inhabitants represents a threat to the stability of all realities. Individual sacrifices and even the destruction of entire planes would be justified in the name of preserving the whole.

The protagonist, in turn, seeks to challenge this authority and take control of the multiverse. However, as they recover fragments of power, they gradually become more like their creator. Their journey is not merely about defeating a divine entity, but about proving that they are capable of taking its place.

Inspired by the concept of Mantling from The Elder Scrolls, the ending involves the transcendence of the protagonist's original identity and their transformation into the very entity they intend to replace. Victory may represent the beginning of a new era or simply the continuation of a cosmic cycle under a new authority.

## Objective

Traverse the fragmented planes of the multiverse, confront their guardians and primordial entities, gather fragments of power, and challenge the creator to claim their place in the cosmic structure of existence.

## Core Mechanics

- Exploration of interconnected planes, each with its own visual identity, geography, creatures, civilizations, and characteristics.
- Challenging action combat based on positioning, dodging, blocking, timing, and mastery of the character's abilities.
- Battles against bosses and colossal entities representing fundamental forces within the different planes.
- Character progression through the collection of fragments of primordial essence.
- Acquisition of weapons, abilities, and powers associated with the characteristics of each plane and the entities defeated.
- Alteration of fundamental aspects of reality through collected fragments, producing consequences for environments and their inhabitants.
- Narrative exploration through ruins, characters, objects, dialogue, and environmental events.
- Gradual transformation of the protagonist, reflecting their growing resemblance to the nature and powers of their creator.
- Discovery of different interpretations of cosmic balance, divine authority, and the responsibility of governing the multiverse.

## References

- Dungeons & Dragons: Great Wheel cosmology and the organization of the planes of existence.
- Dark Souls: challenging combat, interconnected exploration, memorable bosses, progression, and environmental storytelling.
- Alice: Madness Returns: surreal art direction, fantastical worlds, disturbing environments, and visual creativity.
- The Elder Scrolls: the concept of Mantling, transcendence of identity, and the replacement of divine entities.
