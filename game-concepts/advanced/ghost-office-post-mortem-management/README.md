# Ghost Office: Post-Mortem Management

## Concept

A supernatural simulation, management, and strategy game with a comedic tone, set in a company on the brink of bankruptcy. Following the death of its former president, the firm fell into chaos. Throughout his tenure, he centralized virtually every decision, personally handled tasks, and never prepared his children to take over the business.

Unable to accept the disappearance of the company he built, his ghost remains in the office and decides to continue running the business from beyond the grave. Since he cannot interact directly with the world of the living, he begins possessing employees to increase productivity, take on impossible tasks, and resolve internal problems. In more delicate situations, he can also possess clients and influence business negotiations.

Amid chaotic meetings, incompetent employees, unprepared heirs, and supernatural events, the former president must keep the company running and prevent bankruptcy. The problem is that, even in death, he remains a controlling boss who believes he is the only person capable of doing anything right.

## Objective

Save the company from bankruptcy, reorganize its operations, and ensure its survival by using supernatural abilities to compensate for the heirs' managerial incompetence and the employees' limitations.

## Core Mechanics

- Manage employees, departments, and business operations.
- Possess employees to perform tasks and increase productivity.
- Possess clients to influence negotiations and close deals.
- Manage meetings, projects, and corporate demands.
- Handle finances and turn the company around.
- Resolve crises and absurd corporate situations.
- Develop the ghost's supernatural abilities.
- Balance productivity, efficiency, and the consequences of possession.
