# Anhangabaú: The Devil's River

## Concept

An action-adventure game featuring dark fantasy and supernatural horror elements, set deep beneath the Anhangabaú Valley in São Paulo.

Beneath the city's concrete lies an ancient river, imprisoned for generations by a seal that prevents its influence from reaching the surface. This river is not made of water, but of a black, supernatural substance capable of manifesting nightmares, distorting environments, and transforming creatures into monsters. With the seal broken, its flow begins to invade tunnels, underground passages, and foundations, progressively distorting urban reality.

The player takes on the role of a guardian belonging to a forgotten lineage, trained to protect the city against the entities emerging from this abyss. Their mission is to explore underground structures, uncover the origin of the breach, and confront the Colossi of the Devil's River, ancient creatures that represent different manifestations of its supernatural power.

The experience combines interconnected environment exploration, combat against gigantic creatures, mystery investigation, and skill progression. As the player advances, the environment becomes increasingly hostile, and the river's influence transforms familiar places into increasingly unrecognizable areas.

## Objective

Descend into the underground depths of the Anhangabaú Valley, investigate the breach of the ancient seal, and defeat the Colossi of the Devil's River before the entity reaches the surface and consumes the city.

## Main Mechanics

- Exploration of interconnected underground environments, including tunnels, passages, foundations, and forgotten structures.
- Action combat against supernatural creatures and large-scale bosses.
- Battles against the Colossi of the Devil's River, each with unique characteristics, attack patterns, and vulnerabilities.
- Combat system featuring weapons, techniques, and abilities inherited from the guardians' lineage.
- Skill progression and enhancement of the protagonist's powers.
- Environments transformed by the influence of the black substance.
- Investigation of documents, symbols, and remnants of the ancient guardian lineage.
- Horror and survival elements, with resources and threats distributed throughout the environment.
- Narrative progression based on uncovering the river's origins and the history of the ancient seal.
