# The Swap Meet: One Man's Trash is Your Treasure

## Concept

A commercial simulation and management game set in a bustling flea market, where used goods, rare finds, antiques, and merchandise of questionable origin circulate daily among sellers and buyers.

The player starts as a small-time dealer with little money or experience, trying to find opportunities amid the chaos of the market. Each day represents a new chance to discover valuable items, negotiate with sellers, identify counterfeits, and resell merchandise at better prices.

The market is populated by charismatic characters, experienced merchants, collectors, curious shoppers, and unpredictable customers. Not everything is as it seems: an apparently worthless object may conceal a rare treasure, while an item that looks valuable may turn out to be counterfeit or simply fail to attract buyers.

As the player develops their trading skills and reputation, they gain a foothold in the market, build relationships with other dealers, and expand their operations. What begins with small trades can evolve into a true commercial empire, built on knowledge, negotiation, and the ability to recognize opportunities where others see nothing but junk.

## Objective

Thrive in the competitive flea market environment, building an increasingly profitable business through the purchase, appraisal, and resale of merchandise. The ultimate goal is to gain influence and become one of the market's leading dealers.

## Core Mechanics

- Daily exploration of the market, visiting stalls and discovering merchandise available for negotiation.
- Buying and reselling used, rare, collectible, and goods of uncertain origin.
- Merchandise appraisal to identify market value, rarity, authenticity, and potential counterfeits.
- Haggling and price negotiation with sellers and buyers of different backgrounds.
- Inventory, working capital, and profit margin management.
- Reputation building based on negotiations and business relationships.
- Interactions with merchants, collectors, and customers, including opportunities and conflicts.
- Business progression, allowing players to expand their operations and gain positions of greater influence in the market.

## References

- Supermarket Simulator: purchasing, selling, inventory, and business management.
- Dealer's Life: negotiation, item appraisal, and haggling with customers.
- Pawn Shop Simulator: buying and reselling used merchandise and assessing its value.
- Moonlighter: the gameplay loop of exploration, item acquisition, and trading.
