# Investigative Mysteries: Urban Nightmares of Brazil

## Concept

A supernatural horror and investigative adventure game in which the player takes on the role of a detective hired to investigate and document Brazilian urban legends scattered across cities and villages.

Each investigation features accounts of unexplained phenomena, disappearances, apparitions, and events that defy reason. To unravel each mystery, the detective must visit the locations involved, interview residents, gather evidence, analyze documents, photograph supernatural manifestations, and reconstruct the events.

As the investigations progress, the protagonist begins to uncover connections between different legends, realizing that seemingly unrelated accounts are part of something much larger. However, the closer they get to the truth, the more their perception of reality is affected by the supernatural presence.

The investigative work itself can have unexpected consequences. Documenting apparitions, recording evidence, or sharing information can inadvertently strengthen the entities and bring them closer to the world of the living. The detective must uncover the force connecting all the legends before their own identity and sanity are consumed by the mystery.

## Objective

Investigate and document Brazilian urban legends, solve the mysteries behind each account, and uncover the supernatural force connecting the different phenomena before the protagonist becomes part of the legends themselves.

## Core Mechanics

- Investigation of supernatural cases across different cities, villages, and regions of Brazil.
- Exploration of locations associated with urban legends and unexplained events.
- Interviews with residents, witnesses, and people involved in the accounts.
- Collection and documentation of evidence through photographs, recordings, documents, and physical traces.
- Analysis of clues and reconstruction of events to solve each investigation.
- Encounters and confrontations with supernatural manifestations and entities.
- Sanity and perception system, affecting how evidence is interpreted and the protagonist's experience.
- Discovery of connections between different legends through recurring clues and shared information.
- Supernatural consequences resulting from the investigation and documentation of phenomena.

## References

- Brazilian urban legends and folklore: inspiration for the cases, entities, and supernatural manifestations.
- Investigative adventure and narrative games: evidence collection, clue analysis, and mystery solving.
- Psychological survival horror: atmosphere, tension, and distortions in the perception of reality.
