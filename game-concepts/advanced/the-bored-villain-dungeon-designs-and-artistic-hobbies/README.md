# The Bored Villain: Dungeon Designs and Artistic Hobbies

## Concept

A traditional fantasy RPG with comedy and metafiction, inspired by the Zelda Cucui Ganon (Suavemente) meme. The game features a caricatured version of a great villain who, after conquering the world and establishing seemingly absolute dominion, discovers that his greatest challenge is not defeating heroes, but finding something interesting to do with the eternity he has secured.

While the hero embarks on a conventional fantasy adventure, exploring dungeons, fighting monsters, solving puzzles, and developing their abilities, the villain follows the entire journey through an arcane television installed in his castle. Bored, he makes absurd comments, criticisms, and observations about the player's decisions. He might complain when the hero overlooks architectural details in a dungeon, criticize their combat strategy, or express outrage at particularly questionable choices.

These interactions can unlock secret achievements, encouraging the player to perform specific actions to entertain, surprise, or annoy the viewer. The traditional adventure thus gains a second layer of humor, in which the hero's actions are constantly judged by someone who believes he has absolute authority over everything.

Periodically, the player takes control of the villain himself and experiences his daily routine of artistic pursuits and hobbies. Despite being the ruler of an empire conquered through force, he approaches his pastimes with almost obsessive discipline. His schedule includes salsa and classical dance lessons, piano, pipe organ, orchestral conducting, classical singing, opera, painting, sculpture, poetry, theater, chess, ornamental gardening, calligraphy, collecting, and musical composition. Each activity features its own challenges and minigames, requiring dedication and continuous improvement.

The villain must also manage his empire, maintaining a strict schedule to balance artistic commitments, administrative responsibilities, and monitoring the hero's adventure. Everything must run perfectly, because even a world conqueror needs to keep his routine organized.

The contrast between the hero's epic adventure, the villain's eccentric commentary, and his intense artistic life serves as the game's primary source of humor. However, as he follows the journey, the villain begins to develop a genuine interest in the hero and his accomplishments. Gradually, what initially seemed like nothing more than predictable entertainment begins to occupy an unexpectedly important place in his daily routine.

While believing he has absolute control over everything, the villain may discover that his dominion is not as unshakable as he imagines.

## Objective

Guide the hero through a traditional adventure while following the great villain's daily routine, balancing his artistic pursuits, administrative commitments, and commentary on the journey. The experience explores the contrast between epic fantasy and the eccentric everyday life of someone who has conquered the world but is still searching for something to give meaning to his days.

## Core Mechanics

- Traditional fantasy RPG featuring dungeon exploration, monster combat, puzzle-solving, and character progression.
- Arcane television that allows the villain to follow the hero's adventure and comment on his decisions in real time.
- Contextual comments and eccentric reactions from the villain to the player's actions, choices, and behavior.
- Secret achievements unlocked through specific behaviors during the adventure, including actions intended to entertain or annoy the villain.
- Alternation between the hero's perspective and the villain's personal routine.
- Artistic hobbies and cultural activities presented as independent challenges and minigames, including dance, music, painting, sculpture, poetry, theater, chess, and ornamental gardening.
- Progression in artistic activities, allowing players to improve their skills and develop different specialties.
- Strict scheduling requiring the player to balance hobbies, administrative commitments, and monitoring the adventure.
- Empire management, keeping the administration organized while the villain pursues his pastimes.
- Evolution of the indirect relationship between the villain and the hero, reflected in the villain's comments and growing interest in the adventure.
- Comedic situations arising from the contrast between the villain's absolute authority and his seemingly trivial everyday concerns.

## References

- The Legend of Zelda: traditional adventure RPG structure, dungeon exploration, and puzzle-solving.
- The Stanley Parable: metafiction, commentary on player actions, and narrative humor.
- Disgaea: exaggerated fantasy, eccentric characters, and humor involving powerful villains.
- WarioWare: varied challenges and minigames representing different activities.
- Persona: schedule management, commitments, and development through side activities.
