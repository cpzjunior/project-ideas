# Star Piracy: Return of the Scoundrel

## Concept

A space exploration and adventure game with RPG and management elements. The title is a play on words referencing Star Wars: Return of the Jedi, replacing the return of the Jedi with the return of a true space scoundrel: a pirate from the Golden Age of Piracy.

During an expedition in search of treasure in Antarctica, the protagonist becomes frozen and awakens centuries later in a futuristic civilization where humanity has already conquered space.

As he attempts to resume his former profession, he discovers that the world has become far too safe, automated, and monotonous for his adventurous spirit. Unwilling to abandon his true nature, he decides to take piracy to the stars and become the greatest intergalactic pirate of all time.

A rogue, arrogant, heavy drinker with rather flexible morals, the protagonist is, above all, an adventurer with a passion for the unknown. His journey involves exploring uncharted planets, discovering lost civilizations, finding legendary treasures, facing rivals, and assembling a crew.

Although he is a privateer who never turns down a good plunder, his life is not solely about looting. He seeks freedom, fame, wealth, and, above all, epic stories to tell. After all, the entire universe is his ocean, and he intends to become its greatest legend.

## Objective

Build a reputation as the greatest intergalactic pirate of all time by exploring the universe, amassing wealth, assembling a crew, and embarking on extraordinary adventures.

## Core Mechanics

- Space exploration and discovery of unknown planets.
- Investigation of lost civilizations and historical locations.
- Search for treasures, relics, and legendary rewards.
- Space combat and battles against rival pirates.
- Crew recruitment and management.
- Plundering, smuggling, and trading goods.
- Customization and upgrades for the pirate ship.
- Building reputation and fame across the universe.
- Moral choices and relationships with different civilizations and factions.
