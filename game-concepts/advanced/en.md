# Just Breathe: Restarting One Step at a Time

## Concept

A narrative adventure game about personal recovery, in which the player follows a young hikikomori who lives isolated in his bedroom, withdrawn from society and dependent on his family for his everyday needs. After his mother expresses her frustration with his self-destructive habits and the impact his isolation has on the family, he decides to try changing his life, driven by his love for his family and his fear of completely losing control over his own existence.

The recovery journey begins with small actions, such as organizing his room, establishing a routine, maintaining personal hygiene, and taking responsibility for basic tasks. Gradually, the protagonist must confront social isolation, abandon harmful habits, overcome his fear of leaving home, and rebuild his relationship with the outside world.

Progress happens gradually, with small achievements unlocking new possibilities and allowing him to regain independence and self-esteem. However, change is not linear: relapses, frustrations, insecurities, and personal difficulties can interrupt his progress, requiring patience and determination to start over.

The narrative follows the protagonist's transformation, showing how small steps can represent major advances in rebuilding a life.

## Objective

Help the protagonist gradually overcome his hikikomori isolation, rebuilding his independence, self-esteem, and autonomy while restoring his family and social connections.

## Core Mechanics

- Daily routine and activity system, featuring tasks that represent small steps toward personal recovery.
- Gradual development of healthy habits and abandonment of self-destructive behaviors.
- Progression based on small achievements and personal goals.
- Relapse and difficulty system that can temporarily interrupt progress.
- Restoration of autonomy through the recovery of responsibilities and independence.
- Development of family relationships and reconnection with other people.
- Gradual exploration of external environments as the protagonist regains the confidence to leave home.
- Self-esteem and confidence system influenced by the player's choices and achievements.
- Branching narrative with consequences for the protagonist's decisions and recovery journey.

## References

- Hikikomori culture: prolonged social isolation and withdrawal from society.
- Narrative adventure games: character development and choice-based progression.
- Life simulation games: routines, habits, responsibilities, and relationships.
