# Cozy Bubble: The Illusion of Perfection

## Concept

A psychological horror game set in a world carefully designed to be welcoming, comfortable, and seemingly perfect. Shopkeepers are always smiling, creatures are docile, routines run without unexpected events, and everything seems to exist solely to ensure the player's well-being.

However, small inconsistencies begin to emerge in this seemingly flawless reality. Characters repeat behaviors in an artificial manner, situations unfold predictably, and strange events are quickly corrected or ignored.

Gradually, the player discovers that this world is sustained by a control structure that forces all its inhabitants to fulfill predetermined roles. Behaviors considered inappropriate are corrected, characters who deviate from their assigned functions disappear, and even the enemies seem to follow a carefully predetermined fate.

The welcoming atmosphere begins to reveal a disturbing reality in which happiness is mandatory and any expression of individuality represents a threat to the system's equilibrium. The player must decide whether to accept this comfortable existence or question the nature of perfection and what has been sacrificed to maintain it.

## Objective

Investigate the inconsistencies in this world, uncover the mechanisms responsible for maintaining its apparent perfection, and decide whether to preserve or challenge the reality built around you.

## Main Mechanics

- Exploration of a welcoming world: visit colorful and seemingly safe environments, observing their routines and inhabitants.
- Environmental investigation: identify small flaws, contradictions, and artificial behaviors.
- Character interaction: talk to inhabitants and gradually discover their limitations and conflicts.
- Behavioral anomalies: witness characters repeating actions, avoiding questions, or displaying unexpected behaviors.
- Reality correction: observe situations and characters being altered or removed when they fail to fulfill their assigned roles.
- Narrative discovery: gather clues about the control structure and the origins of this world.
- Moral choices: decide between accepting the illusion of perfection or confronting the system that sustains it.

## References

- Psychological horror.
- Cozy aesthetics and seemingly welcoming worlds.
- Narratives about social control, conformity, and the loss of individuality.
