# Color Tones: Chromesthesia & Musical Jams

## Concept

A musical narrative game inspired by the aesthetics, personality, and artistic journey of Jimi Hendrix. The player takes on the role of a chromesthete guitarist in the 1960s, capable of seeing sounds as colors, shapes, and movements. To him, each note has its own visual identity, and playing the guitar is a way of transforming the reality around him.

Living as an independent musician, the protagonist must find ways to pay the bills while trying to make a name for himself in the music scene. Through performances at small bars, odd jobs, encounters with other artists, and unexpected opportunities, he embarks on a journey of artistic discovery and financial survival.

His performances go beyond music: each improvisation visually transforms the environment, causing colors, lights, and shapes to spread throughout the world. As he develops his musical identity, the protagonist discovers that his ability to see and express music can influence not only the audience but also the way people perceive the world.

The game combines narrative, exploration, and musical experimentation in a psychedelic journey about artistic freedom, the hardships of a musician's life, and the desire to make the world a more colorful place.

## Objective

Build an independent music career, achieve financial stability, and develop a unique artistic identity, using music to visually transform the world and impact the people around you.

## Main Mechanics

- Musical performance: play guitar and improvise using different techniques, tones, and effects.
- Chromesthesia: transform notes, chords, and melodies into visual manifestations of colors, shapes, lights, and movements.
- Musical performances: play shows at bars, clubs, and other venues, attracting audiences and unlocking opportunities.
- Financial survival: manage money, pay bills, purchase equipment, and find jobs to sustain your career.
- Sound experimentation: discover musical combinations and effects that produce different visual experiences.
- Artistic progression: develop musical skills, explore new styles, and build a unique artistic identity.
- Narrative interaction: meet musicians, producers, fans, and characters who influence the protagonist's journey.
- Environmental transformation: use performances and improvisations to alter the visual atmosphere of locations and the people around you.

## References
