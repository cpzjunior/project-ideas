# The Live House Project: From Silence to Spotlight

## Concept

A narrative simulation and management game in which the player takes on the role of a live music venue owner in a constantly changing city. More than simply running a business, their mission is to build a cultural space capable of bringing together artists, audiences, and different stories through music and nightlife.

Every night brings new situations, characters, and conflicts. Ambitious artists seek recognition, independent bands struggle to make a name for themselves, customers visit the venue carrying their own problems, and people with different interests cross paths in this environment. Each performance can represent an opportunity for discovery, a commercial failure, or the beginning of an important relationship.

The player must organize events, select artists, manage resources, and handle audience expectations. However, their decisions go beyond financial results: they can launch music careers, strengthen communities, create rivalries, bring characters closer together, or trigger conflicts that ripple throughout the city.

The nightlife scene also evolves over time. New venues emerge, musical trends shift, neighborhoods transform, and different groups compete for space and influence. The venue must establish its own identity amid these changes, balancing financial sustainability, artistic freedom, and personal relationships.

Between fame, business pressures, and human dilemmas, the player discovers that running a live music venue means dealing with the stories of everyone who walks through its doors. The establishment becomes more than just a business: it transforms into a gathering place capable of shaping the city's cultural and social destiny.

## Objective

Build and manage a successful live music venue, developing its cultural identity and influencing the city's nightlife scene. The player must balance financial sustainability, artistic opportunities, and personal relationships, building a legacy that extends beyond the venue's commercial performance.

## Core Mechanics

- Live music venue management, including budgeting, infrastructure, staff, and operating expenses.
- Event organization and selection of artists, bands, and musical performances.
- Scheduling, venue capacity, ticket sales, and audience expectations management.
- Artist system featuring unique personalities, ambitions, struggles, and career paths.
- Emergent narratives involving musicians, employees, customers, suppliers, and regular patrons.
- Relationship system featuring friendships, rivalries, conflicts, and alliances between characters.
- Business and artistic decisions that influence the venue's reputation and cultural identity.
- Dynamic evolution of the nightlife scene, with shifting trends, new competitors, and changing neighborhoods.
- Consequences of decisions affecting music careers, local communities, and personal relationships.
- Special events and unexpected situations requiring decisions under pressure.
- Venue progression, allowing the player to expand its facilities, gain recognition, and establish its presence in the city.

## References

- This Is the Police: management, difficult decisions, and narrative consequences.
- VA-11 Hall-A: personal stories unfolding in an establishment frequented by different characters.
- Coffee Talk: episodic storytelling and relationship building through everyday encounters.
- Two Point Campus: establishment management and administration of multiple interconnected systems.
- Rock Band: musical performances and performance culture.
