# Silver Sprint: One for the Road

## Concept

An adventure comedy starring a group of elderly people who decide to escape from a retirement home in a decrepit van to finally do everything they have been putting off throughout their lives.

The group consists of eccentric characters, each with a distinct personality and backstory: a paranoid former spy who still sees conspiracies everywhere, a former athlete who has gained considerable weight and lost some of her physical conditioning, a former musician who now suffers from shaky hands, and a sarcastic former teacher who has never lost her habit of lecturing everyone around her.

Despite their physical limitations and rusty skills, each member has experiences and knowledge that can prove useful throughout the journey. The former spy still knows infiltration techniques, the former athlete retains her sports experience, the musician maintains his musical knowledge, and the teacher can resolve situations through intelligence, persuasion, and her talent for argumentation.

The trip turns into a series of improvised adventures, unexpected encounters, and absurd situations. The van develops mechanical problems, the characters get themselves into trouble, and their plans frequently go wrong, requiring creativity and cooperation to keep the journey going.

Behind the physical comedy and humorous situations, each character has a personal dream they want to fulfill. As the journey progresses, the group shares memories, confronts regrets, and discovers that there are still experiences worth embarking on an adventure for.

## Objective

Join the group on their last great adventure, helping each member fulfill their personal dream before the journey comes to an end.

## Core Mechanics

- Exploration of different cities, roads, and locations during a road trip in the van.
- Group of playable characters with distinct personalities, backstories, physical limitations, and abilities.
- Use of each character's previous experience and skills to overcome challenges.
- Cooperation between group members, combining abilities to overcome obstacles and unexpected situations.
- Physical comedy based on the characters' limitations, improvisations, and absurd situations.
- Van management and maintenance, including fuel, repairs, and mechanical problems.
- Missions and activities related to each character's personal dreams.
- Narrative interactions, sarcastic dialogue, memories, and conflicts between characters.
- Unpredictable events during the trip, creating opportunities for new adventures.
- Individual and collective narrative progression, developing the characters and their relationships throughout the journey.

## References

- Thelma & Louise: road trip, escape, freedom, and personal transformation.
- Little Miss Sunshine: family road trip, eccentric characters, and humor combined with emotional moments.
- The Last of Us: character relationships and emotional development throughout a journey.
- Overcooked: cooperation, improvisation, and coordination in chaotic situations.
