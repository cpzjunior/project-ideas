# Pocket Berserker: The High-Stress Pinscher Simulator

## Concept

A comedic simulation and survival game in which the player controls a small Miniature Pinscher living in a house full of stimuli that constantly increase its stress levels.

Despite its small size, the Pinscher has an explosive personality and enormous difficulty dealing with everyday situations. Noises, unexpected movements, visitors, other animals, and changes in the environment can increase its tension, making its body tremble and its movements become increasingly unstable.

As stress levels rise, controlling the character becomes progressively more difficult. When tension reaches its limit, the Pinscher enters berserker mode, completely losing control and reacting impulsively to everything around it with barking, lunging, and uncontrolled attacks.

The player must find ways to restore calm before the situation gets out of control. Finding shelter, receiving affection from its owner, resting, and seeking quiet environments are some of the ways to reduce stress.

The experience combines humor, chaos, and tension management, turning ordinary household situations into unpredictable challenges. The little Pinscher must survive in an apparently harmless world filled with threats to its emotional stability.

## Objective

Keep stress levels under control, find safety and tranquility around the house, and prevent the Pinscher from entering berserker mode.

## Core Mechanics

- Dynamic stress system influenced by environmental stimuli and household events.
- Progressive trembling that affects movement stability and precision as tension increases.
- Berserker mode activated when stress reaches its limit, causing loss of control and impulsive reactions.
- Environmental interactions, including hiding spots, quiet areas, and objects that can provide protection.
- Relationship with the owner, allowing the Pinscher to seek affection, attention, and comfort to regain its composure.
- Different stimuli and household situations that trigger varied reactions from the Pinscher.
- Management of the balance between exploration, exposure to chaos, and stress recovery.
- Unpredictable events that can turn everyday activities into situations of extreme tension.

## References

- Goat Simulator: humor based on chaotic behavior and unpredictable situations.
- Untitled Goose Game: comedic interactions with environments and characters.
- The Sims: simulation of everyday situations and character needs.
