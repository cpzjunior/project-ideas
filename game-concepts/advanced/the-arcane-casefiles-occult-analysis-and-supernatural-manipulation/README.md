# The Arcane Casefiles: Occult Analysis and Supernatural Manipulation

## Concept

An action, investigation, and supernatural horror game inspired by Constantine, in which the player takes on the role of an arrogant and experienced field occultist who sees the supernatural as a system of rules that can be manipulated. Using rituals, symbols, artifacts, and specialized instruments, he investigates anomalous phenomena and forces reality to produce results that would normally be impossible.

The world is filled with breaches between planes of existence, manifestations threatening to cross their boundaries, and supernatural phenomena on the verge of stabilizing into something far worse. The protagonist operates in these locations, analyzing incidents, identifying patterns, and applying occult interventions to contain or exploit anomalies.

His confidence begins to be challenged when he encounters an enigmatic entity originating from an unknown plane. Cold and methodical, it demonstrates an understanding of reality that seems to surpass the occultist's knowledge. Convinced that he can manipulate it just like any other supernatural manifestation, the protagonist establishes a relationship in which every interaction appears to offer new advantages and possibilities.

However, these advantages come with consequences that are not immediately apparent. Each intervention silently alters the surrounding conditions, distorting the rules the protagonist believes he has mastered. Tools begin behaving unexpectedly, rituals produce results different from those intended, and even his own perception of reality becomes questionable.

What initially appears to be an investigation into supernatural phenomena evolves into a struggle for control between the occultist and something that may never have been subject to his rules. The entity does not need to confront him directly: it only needs to let him keep believing he is in control.

The experience combines supernatural case investigations, manipulation of occult systems, and psychological horror, exploring the tension between knowledge, arrogance, and loss of control. The central conflict is not simply about defeating the unknown, but discovering whether there is still a difference between controlling reality and merely believing that you are doing so.

## Objective

Investigate and contain supernatural phenomena, using occult knowledge to manipulate the rules of reality and survive increasingly complex manifestations. At the same time, uncover the nature of the enigmatic entity and discover the consequences of the advantages gained through this relationship.

## Core Mechanics

- Investigation of supernatural incidents and breaches between different planes of existence.
- Analysis of anomalous phenomena through observation, evidence collection, and pattern recognition.
- Ritual system based on combinations of symbols, ingredients, instruments, and occult procedures.
- Localized manipulation of the rules of reality to produce specific supernatural effects.
- Use of artifacts, tools, and specialized equipment during investigations and confrontations.
- Case resolution through different approaches, including containment, negotiation, manipulation, or confrontation with entities.
- Interaction with an enigmatic entity whose intentions and capabilities remain partially unknown.
- Hidden advantages and consequences system in which seemingly beneficial interventions can alter the conditions of future cases.
- Progressive corruption of tools, rituals, and instruments, changing their behavior and outcomes.
- Perceptual distortions that affect the interpretation of evidence and understanding of events.
- Evolution of the relationship between the protagonist and the entity, with consequences for the investigation and the player's own perception of control.

## References

- Constantine: urban occultism, supernatural investigation, and a protagonist who manipulates forces beyond his understanding.
- Control: paranormal phenomena, reality manipulation, and environments subject to inexplicable alterations.
- Alan Wake: psychological horror, reality distortions, and supernatural storytelling.
- Call of Cthulhu: occult investigation, forbidden knowledge, and psychological consequences.
- Dishonored: use of supernatural powers and consequences of the protagonist's choices.
