# Freakolympics: Chaos is the Gold Standard

## Concept

A chaotic party game inspired by Mario Party, in which countries from around the world compete in completely absurd Olympic games featuring minigames that defy logic, physics, and common sense.

Players represent different countries and compete for medals in a series of unpredictable events. Each event features its own rules, unusual controls, and unexpected situations. During matches, rules can change midway through a competition, obstacles can appear without warning, and random events can completely turn the scoreboard upside down.

The combination of skill, timing, and luck ensures that no competitor is guaranteed victory until the very last moment. The goal is to deliver multiplayer matches filled with hilarious situations, rivalries, and unexpected twists, where chaos is an essential part of the competition.

## Objective

Compete in a series of absurd Olympic events, accumulating medals and points across different minigames to claim the title of Freakolympics champion.

## Core Mechanics

- Varied Minigames: compete in events that challenge logic, coordination, reflexes, and unusual skills.
- Multiplayer Competition: play matches with friends or AI-controlled opponents.
- Unpredictable Rules: deal with changes to event conditions and objectives during matches.
- Random Events: face obstacles, interference, and occurrences that can change the outcome of a competition.
- Scoring System: accumulate points and medals across different events.
- National Representation: choose countries to represent during the Olympics.
- Unexpected Twists: use special events and unforeseen situations to change the course of the competition.
- Competitive Progression: compete in different tournaments until claiming the overall championship.
