# The 90s: Echoes of My Analog Youth

## Concept

An exploration and contemplative game set in the 1990s, designed to recapture the nostalgia and simplicity of childhood in a quiet town. The experience allows players to freely explore houses, streets, squares, and local businesses, discovering small details of everyday life in an era defined by analog technology, outdoor activities, and different forms of social interaction.

The world features various activities and situations characteristic of the period: using a payphone, connecting to dial-up internet, watching CRT televisions, listening to cassette tapes, renting movies, visiting video rental stores, and playing soccer in the street with friends. Each environment offers opportunities for interaction and small discoveries that help recreate the atmosphere of the era.

The town features a dynamic day-and-night cycle, residents with their own routines, and spontaneous events. Sudden rain showers, power outages, street vendors, neighborhood games, and unexpected encounters help create a living world where not everything happens at the player's initiative.

Exploration is encouraged through achievements representing small nostalgic experiences, without imposing rigid objectives or creating a sense of competition. By discovering places, interacting with objects, and participating in events, players unlock fragments of stories, photographs, and memories that are collected in a scrapbook gradually assembled throughout the journey.

More than completing missions or achieving high scores, the purpose is to create an emotional connection with the past. The experience celebrates seemingly insignificant moments, such as a late afternoon spent on the sidewalk, a song playing on the radio, or a soccer game that ends when night falls. The game transforms these small experiences into a contemplative journey through childhood, memory, and the passage of time.

## Objective

Explore a town set in the 1990s, discover experiences characteristic of the era, and reconstruct a scrapbook of memories through interactions with environments, objects, residents, and everyday events.

## Core Mechanics

- Free exploration of a town set in the 1990s, including homes, streets, squares, shops, and community spaces.
- Interaction with analog objects and technology, such as payphones, CRT televisions, cassette tapes, radios, VCRs, and computers with dial-up internet.
- Everyday activities and games from the era, including street soccer and hanging out with friends.
- Dynamic day-and-night cycle affecting activity availability and residents' movements.
- NPCs with their own routines, habits, and behaviors that bring the town to life.
- Spontaneous events, such as rain showers, power outages, street vendors, and group activities.
- Achievement system based on small nostalgic experiences and discoveries.
- Collection of photographs, story fragments, and memories unlocked throughout exploration.
- Scrapbook that brings together discoveries and documents the player's journey.
- Contemplative experience without rigid objectives, competition, or performance pressure.

## References

- Shenmue: urban exploration, interaction with objects, and NPC routines.
- My Summer Car: everyday life, simple activities, and environmental interaction.
- Firewatch: contemplative exploration and emotional connection with the setting.
- Lake: relaxed pacing, everyday life, and relationships with residents of a small community.
- Unpacking: environmental storytelling and the creation of memories through everyday objects.
