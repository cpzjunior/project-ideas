# Ghosted Dates: Until Death Doesn't Part Us

## Concept

A dating simulation and narrative adventure game featuring humor and supernatural elements. After spending years mourning his wife's death, a man decides to rebuild his love life and return to the dating scene, using a dating app filled with eccentric people and unpredictable personalities.

There is, however, one small problem: his late wife continues to haunt the house and shows no intention of moving on to the afterlife. Extremely attached to her husband and unable to accept the idea of seeing him with someone else, she follows every attempt at a new relationship and constantly interferes with his dates.

Convinced that ignoring the supernatural manifestations will eventually make his wife move on to paradise, the protagonist insists on acting as though nothing is happening. Naturally, this strategy doesn't work. The more he tries to ignore her, the more creative and outrageous her attempts become to get his attention and drive away his potential partners.

Between disastrous dates, possessions, supernatural manifestations, and increasingly absurd explanations for inexplicable situations, the protagonist must try to build new relationships while his former wife continues to claim her place in his life.

## Objective

Rebuild the protagonist's love life by meeting new people and developing relationships, while managing the constant interference of his late wife and surviving the supernatural chaos that follows every date.

## Core Mechanics

- Dating app featuring potential partners with different personalities, interests, and quirks.
- Dialogue and choice system that influences relationship development.
- Dates at different locations and activities, featuring unpredictable situations and events.
- Supernatural interference from the wife, including possessions, object manipulation, and ghostly manifestations.
- Jealousy and mood system for the wife, influencing the frequency and intensity of her interventions.
- Improvisation and choices to handle absurd situations without ruining the dates.
- Potential partners with varying levels of sensitivity to the supernatural, including some who can see and talk to the wife.
- Narrative events and special situations that develop the relationship between the protagonist and his late wife.
- Multiple endings determined by the choices and relationships developed throughout the story.
