# Isekai Worlds Tycoon: Divine Sabbatical

## Concept

A strategic simulation and management game with comedy elements, in which the player takes on the role of an intern angel tasked with managing the operations of isekai worlds while God is on vacation.

In charge of an enormous celestial bureaucratic structure, the protagonist must coordinate reincarnation processes, issue passports for souls, manage the logistics of isekai trucks, select heroes, and hire demon lords (maous) along with their respective armies to maintain the balance across different worlds.

Each world has its own characteristics, needs, and challenges, requiring administrative decisions that can produce unexpected consequences. Bureaucratic errors, absurd requests, conflicts between departments, and unpredictable events are all part of the daily routine. A soul sent to the wrong world, a hero given unsuitable abilities, or a demon lord hired without the proper qualifications can trigger crises that threaten the stability of multiple dimensions.

With God temporarily absent and an inexperienced intern in charge, celestial administration must keep running. The player must balance efficiency, creativity, and chaos control to prevent the worlds from collapsing, discovering that keeping the universe running can be far more complicated than creating it.

## Objective

Manage the bureaucratic structure responsible for the operation of isekai worlds, coordinating reincarnations, resources, heroes, demon lords, and dimensional events to maintain balance between worlds and prevent collapses while God is on vacation.

## Core Mechanics

- Management of celestial administration and its various departments.
- Processing of souls, passport issuance, and assignment of reincarnation worlds.
- Management of the logistics of trucks responsible for transporting souls to isekai worlds.
- Selection of heroes, distribution of special abilities, and management of their requests.
- Recruitment of demon lords, formation of armies, and management of threats across different worlds.
- Management of resources, budgets, and departmental operational efficiency.
- Coordination of interconnected systems in which administrative decisions can affect multiple worlds.
- Resolution of absurd requests, bureaucratic conflicts, and unexpected events.
- Monitoring of dimensional stability and prevention of crises and collapses between worlds.

## References

- Isekai genre: narrative conventions involving reincarnation in fantasy worlds, chosen heroes, special abilities, and demon lords.
- Simulation and management games: administration of resources, departments, and interconnected systems.
- Workplace comedies: bureaucracy, hierarchy, administrative conflicts, and absurd situations.
