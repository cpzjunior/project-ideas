# Mindjack: Borrowed Lives

## Concept

A psychological thriller and narrative survival game in which the player works for a clandestine service that provides assistance in extreme situations through encrypted calls. Their job is to remotely take control of ordinary people's minds and guide them through kidnappings, pursuits, escapes, and other seemingly impossible situations.

Each client presents a different challenge. After taking control, the player must deal with the physical and psychological limitations of that person's body: people with no physical training, individuals overwhelmed by fear, injured people, or those unable to perform actions requiring strength and endurance. Survival depends on planning, improvisation, and making intelligent use of the environment rather than extraordinary abilities.

The service operates under one absolute rule: never question what your client has done. The operator must fulfill their role, guide the person to safety, and never investigate the circumstances that led to the situation.

However, as cases progress, seemingly unrelated details begin to reveal contradictions in the clients' stories. Some victims may not be as innocent as they appear, while pursuers and kidnappers may have unexpected motivations. The player begins to question the nature of the service itself and the role they play in saving people without knowing their stories.

The narrative gradually builds a moral dilemma around responsibility for the consequences of one's actions. After all, does saving someone necessarily mean doing the right thing?

## Objective

Guide each client through extreme situations and ensure their survival while gradually uncovering the secrets behind the cases and questioning the legitimacy of the people they are helping.

## Core Mechanics

- Remote mind-control system, allowing the player to temporarily take control of different characters.
- Encrypted calls as an interface for receiving instructions, information, and updates on each operation.
- Survival scenarios involving kidnappings, pursuits, escapes, and unpredictable threats.
- Characters with their own physical and psychological attributes and limitations, such as strength, endurance, fear, and physical fitness.
- Challenge resolution based on strategy, environmental exploration, improvisation, and risk management.
- Tactical decisions that influence each client's chances of survival.
- Individual narratives featuring incomplete information and conflicting accounts of events.
- Indirect investigation through dialogue, evidence, and details revealed during operations.
- Connections between different cases that gradually reveal the structure and objectives of the clandestine service.
- Moral dilemmas and narrative consequences related to the people the player chooses to save and the information they uncover.

## References

- Psychological thrillers: narrative tension, incomplete information, and moral dilemmas.
- Survival games: management of physical limitations, resources, and risks.
- Investigative narrative games: gradual revelation of secrets and reconstruction of events.
- Techno-thrillers: clandestine services, encrypted communication, and remote control.
