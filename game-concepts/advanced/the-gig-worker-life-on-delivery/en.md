# The Gig Worker: Life on Delivery

## Concept

An urban simulation, management, and survival game in which the player takes on the role of a delivery app worker in a large, chaotic city. Starting with a basic bicycle and limited financial resources, they must work every day to pay the bills, settle debts, and try to build a better life.

Daily life involves dealing with heavy traffic, adverse weather conditions, demanding customers, slow restaurants, and increasingly tight delivery deadlines. Every delivery represents an opportunity to earn money, but also carries risks that can compromise the protagonist's vehicle, health, and financial stability.

As they accumulate resources, the player can invest in equipment, upgrade their vehicle, purchase a motorcycle, or even acquire a car to expand their work opportunities. However, they must also manage expenses, maintenance, food, housing, and accumulated debts.

The pursuit of financial advancement offers tempting but dangerous paths. The player can choose to save money, invest in upgrades, and pay off obligations, or risk part of their earnings on high-stakes bets that rarely pay off. A series of poor decisions can turn a difficult financial situation into a full-blown downward spiral.

The game explores the reality of people who depend on gig work to survive, combining everyday situations, humor, and economic challenges. Progress is never guaranteed: every choice can bring the protagonist closer to a more stable life or deepen their financial vulnerability.

## Objective

Achieve financial survival through delivery work, manage expenses and debts, and build a path toward economic advancement. The player must find ways to improve working conditions and quality of life without compromising their financial stability along the way.

## Core Mechanics

- On-demand delivery system in a large city featuring traffic, congestion, and different districts.
- Career progression starting with a bicycle, allowing upgrades to motorcycles and automobiles.
- Time management and deadline fulfillment, accounting for distance, traffic, weather conditions, and delays at establishments.
- Interactions with demanding customers, restaurants, and unexpected situations during deliveries.
- Financial management involving income, recurring expenses, debts, and personal obligations.
- Vehicle maintenance and upgrade system, including equipment and accessories.
- Management of personal needs, such as food, housing, and rest.
- High-risk betting as an alternative source of income, with potential losses and financial consequences.
- Improvement of working conditions as the player acquires better vehicles and equipment.
- Dynamic urban events that can affect delivery availability, earnings, and operating costs.
- Financial consequences of decisions and unexpected events, potentially leading to debt or opportunities for recovery.
- Progression based on the ability to balance work, investments, expenses, and quality of life.

## References

- Crazy Taxi: urban transportation, deliveries, and time pressure.
- Death Stranding: package transportation and route planning.
- Euro Truck Simulator 2: career progression, vehicles, and operating cost management.
- The Sims: management of personal needs and everyday expenses.
- Papers, Please: economic pressure and difficult decisions in a daily work routine.
