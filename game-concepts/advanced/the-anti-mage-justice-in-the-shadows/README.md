# The Anti-Mage: Justice in the Shadows

## Concept

An action RPG featuring dark fantasy and narrative investigation, following the journey of Alden, a man born on the fringes of the magical elite who carves out his place in a society dominated by those who possess supernatural powers.

The story begins with Alden as a young man, living in a rural region where his family works for one of the great magical families. Despite belonging to the majority of the population, non-mages have fewer rights and opportunities, living under the influence of an elite that concentrates political, economic, and institutional power.

When his magic awakens late in life, Alden is given the opportunity to change his fate. However, his humble origins and lack of connections make it difficult for him to integrate into magical society. Before enrolling in the academy, he travels along roads, explores dungeons, and develops combat and survival skills that will later become distinguishing assets throughout his journey.

During an expedition, Alden encounters a mysterious creature and accepts a pact that grants him access to anti-magic: an exceptional ability to disrupt the magical traces normally left behind by a mage's actions. This power allows him to bypass protections, conceal his presence, and perform actions that evade conventional investigative methods.

From that moment on, his journey begins to combine magical education, criminal investigation, infiltration, and political conflicts. During the day, Alden works as an investigator, using his institutional position to fight criminal organizations and investigate corrupt members of the elite. At night, he can use anti-magic to uncover secrets, gather information, and act against those who manage to escape conventional justice.

The gameplay combines action RPG, exploration, investigation, and stealth, with narrative decisions that influence relationships, reputation, institutional stability, and the protagonist's moral trajectory.

As he gains recognition, Alden moves closer to the center of power and begins to occupy increasingly important positions. However, his growing willingness to act outside the rules gradually transforms his pursuit of justice into a personal crusade, in which the distinction between investigator, vigilante, and criminal becomes increasingly difficult to establish.

The experience culminates in Alden's rise to the position of Archmage, confronting the player with a central contradiction: how can someone who has spent their entire life questioning those who wield power without accountability govern without becoming what they have always fought against?

## Objective

Develop Alden's journey from his humble origins to his rise to the position of Archmage, exploring dungeons, mastering different forms of magic, solving investigations, and confronting the political and moral conflicts of a society governed by the magical elite.

The player must build their reputation, develop their abilities, and decide how to use the powers they acquire, balancing personal interests, the pursuit of justice, the preservation of institutions, and the consequences of their own actions.

## Core Mechanics

- RPG system featuring character progression, attributes, skills, equipment, and specializations.
- Real-time combat system combining conventional weapons, martial techniques, and different schools of magic.
- Anti-magic system that allows the player to disrupt magical traces, bypass protections, neutralize spells, and carry out infiltrations.
- Exploration of open environments, cities, rural regions, academies, dungeons, and magically protected facilities.
- Criminal investigation system based on crime scene analysis, evidence collection, interrogations, reconstruction of events, and suspect identification.
- Stealth and infiltration system, allowing the player to break into properties, access restricted areas, and obtain information without alerting those responsible.
- Dual investigation mechanic, in which the player can use information obtained clandestinely to guide official investigations.
- Professional progression system, allowing the player to evolve from adventurer and magic student to investigator and member of the ruling elite.
- Decision and consequence system, with impacts on characters, factions, institutions, reputation, and future events.
- Reputation system with different social groups, including non-mages, traditional families, investigators, criminal organizations, and magical authorities.
- Relationship and rivalry system, featuring characters who can become allies, adversaries, informants, or threats to Alden's identity.
- Resource management system, including money, equipment, consumables, exploration materials, and mission preparation.
- Identity secrecy system, in which the player must protect their secret while using powers that could compromise their own safety.
- Moral consequence system, in which seemingly justifiable decisions can produce unexpected outcomes and compromise the protagonist's integrity.
- Political and institutional progression, allowing the player to gain influence, participate in important decisions, and eventually assume positions of authority.
- Events and missions with multiple approaches, offering alternatives such as formal investigation, negotiation, manipulation, infiltration, and direct confrontation.
- External threats and social stability system, in which the removal of powerful individuals can generate consequences that extend beyond the protagonist's immediate objectives.
