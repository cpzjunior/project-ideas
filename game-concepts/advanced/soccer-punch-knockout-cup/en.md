# Soccer Punch: Knockout Cup

## Concept

A competitive sports game that combines soccer and physical combat in an arena with no rules, referees, or stoppages. In this brutal sport, winning depends not only on ball-handling skills but also on the ability to control space, overpower opponents physically, and execute physical actions at the right moment.

Each match is a clash of controlled chaos, where movement, positioning, and combat are constantly intertwined. Players must decide when to protect teammates, force their way through opponents, fight for possession, or sacrifice their own position to create a strategic advantage.

With consistent physics and real consequences for every action, the game transforms violence, movement, and athletic skill into a new form of competition. The result is a version of soccer where strategy is not limited to plays involving the ball: physical confrontation is an integral part of the game.

## Objective

Win matches and claim the Knockout Cup by combining soccer skills, territorial control, and physical superiority to overcome opposing teams.

## Core Mechanics

- Soccer matches in arenas without referees, conventional rules, or stoppages.
- Combination of traditional soccer fundamentals and real-time physical combat.
- Movement and collision system based on consistent physics.
- Physical confrontations requiring positioning, strength, reflexes, and timing.
- Space control and territorial competition to create attacking opportunities.
- Teammate protection and coordinated actions between team members.
- Combat and movement strategies that emerge dynamically during matches.
- Physical and positional consequences for every action, influencing the course of each confrontation.
- Tournament competition with the goal of winning the Knockout Cup.

- Blood Bowl: fictional sport combining strategy, football, and brutal combat.
