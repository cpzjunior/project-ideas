# Junior Episode Director: 5 Minutes to Glory, One Frame to Lame

## Concept

A comedy-driven simulation and management game in which the player takes on the role of a junior anime director who dreams of directing entire series. For each episode, they must coordinate scene production in five-minute cycles, managing dialogue, actions, characters, and visual sequences, while following the original script or improvising solutions to deal with limitations and unexpected problems.

While trying to turn their ideas into successful episodes, the protagonist must face impossible deadlines, creative crises, production issues, and an online community made up of reclusive Reddit teenagers who follow, criticize, and discuss every episode. Between criticism, absurd theories, trolls, and memes, viewers' opinions can directly influence their reputation and professional opportunities.

Every creative decision can result in a memorable episode or a disaster that goes viral. The goal is to earn recognition as a director and prove they can survive both the anime industry and the internet's merciless judgment.

## Objective

Produce anime episodes, build a career as a director, and earn recognition from the industry and audience while balancing creative quality, production deadlines, and online reputation.

## Core Mechanics

- Episode direction and management divided into five-minute production cycles.
- Organization of dialogue, actions, characters, and scene sequences.
- Execution of the original script or improvisation of scenes and events.
- Management of deadlines, resources, and unexpected issues during production.
- Resolution of creative crises and execution problems.
- Episode reviews by the online community, including criticism, theories, memes, and unpredictable reactions.
- Interaction with different groups of viewers, fans, critics, and trolls.
- Reputation system that influences professional opportunities and career progression.
- Narrative and professional consequences resulting from creative choices and audience reception.

## References

- Anime production industry: creative processes, episode direction, and production constraints.
- Reddit culture and online communities: discussions, criticism, theories, memes, and unpredictable viewer behavior.
- Simulation and management games: resource allocation, deadlines, and operational decisions.
