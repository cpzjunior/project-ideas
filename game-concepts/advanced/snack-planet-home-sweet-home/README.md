# Snack Planet: Home Sweet Home

## Concept

A strategy, building, and management game in which the player governs a planet made entirely of food. In this peculiar world, continents, mountains, rivers, forests, and oceans are formed from different ingredients and dishes, creating edible ecosystems that sustain a variety of creatures and civilizations.

The player must develop and preserve the planet by balancing its ecosystems, managing resources, and protecting its inhabitants from predators and natural threats. Each region has its own characteristics, with different types of food, environmental conditions, and relationships between species.

However, living on a food planet presents some rather unusual challenges. Chocolate rain, melted cheese eruptions, sugar storms, and other culinary disasters can drastically alter the environment, destroying habitats, reshaping territories, and threatening ecological balance.

The player must anticipate these events, prepare their civilization, and find ways to turn potentially destructive phenomena into opportunities for development. Uncontrolled expansion can also damage ecosystems, cause resource shortages, and disrupt the food chain.

The experience combines world-building, strategy, and ecological management with absurd and humorous situations, exploring the possibilities of a planet where practically everything can be eaten.

## Objective

Develop and preserve an edible planet, ensuring the survival of its inhabitants, maintaining ecosystem balance, and protecting the world from predators and culinary disasters.

## Core Mechanics

- Construction and management of a planet composed of different foods, ingredients, and dishes.
- Development of edible ecosystems, including food-based forests, oceans, mountains, and plains.
- Management of natural resources and food, balancing production, consumption, and availability.
- Administration of populations and communities inhabiting different regions of the planet.
- Dynamic food chain featuring herbivorous creatures, carnivores, and specialized predators.
- Protection of inhabitants against natural threats and predatory creatures.
- Culinary disasters, including chocolate rain, melted cheese eruptions, and sugar storms.
- Environmental changes caused by extreme events, altering habitats and resource availability.
- Territorial expansion and development of new regions, taking into account the impact on ecological balance.
- Dynamic events requiring preparation, adaptation, and strategic decisions to preserve the planet.

## References

- SimCity: construction, planning, and management of an inhabited environment.
- Spore: development of ecosystems, creatures, and life forms.
- Planet Zoo: management of habitats, species, and environmental balance.
- Dwarf Fortress: simulation of complex ecosystems and emergent interactions between creatures and their environment.
