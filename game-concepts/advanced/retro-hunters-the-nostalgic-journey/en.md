# Retro Hunters: The Nostalgic Journey

## Concept

An adventure, collection, and competition game inspired by the progression structure of the early Pokémon games for the Game Boy, in which the player dreams of becoming the greatest video game collector in the world.

The journey takes place in a universe dedicated to classic video game culture, where collectors compete for rare consoles, cartridges, and accessories through challenges based on retro games. Instead of traditional battles, encounters take place in mini-games inspired by different genres and platforms, requiring skill, strategy, knowledge, and adaptability.

Each challenge takes the form of a competition between collectors, who put part of their collections on the line. The winner claims the items wagered, expanding their collection and unlocking new competitive opportunities.

The consoles acquired are not merely collectible items: each platform introduces new mechanics, gameplay styles, and strategies, gradually expanding the variety of available challenges. Thus, winning a new video game console also means expanding the possibilities for competition and exploration.

Throughout the journey, the player faces collectors with distinct specialties, personalities, and collections, participates in tournaments, and seeks increasingly rare items to build the ultimate collection.

## Objective

Become the greatest video game collector in the world by winning consoles, cartridges, and rare items through competitive challenges and continuously expanding your collection.

## Core Mechanics

- Exploration of a universe dedicated to classic video game culture, discovering collectors, challenges, and opportunities to compete.
- Collection system featuring consoles, cartridges, and accessories from different generations.
- Mini-games inspired by video game classics, covering different genres and gameplay styles.
- Competitions between collectors, with participants wagering consoles and cartridges from their own collections.
- Progression based on acquiring new platforms, unlocking specific mechanics and challenges.
- Rival collectors with their own specialties, strategies, and collections.
- Tournaments and special competitions to win rare items and build your reputation.
- Rarity and discovery system for special video games, cartridges, and accessories.
- Development of the player's skills across different mini-game categories.
- Creation of a personal collection, with the ability to organize and display acquired items.

## References

- Pokémon Red/Blue: adventure structure, progression, collection, and challenges against characters in the game world.
- Retro Game Challenge: experiences and challenges inspired by classic video games.
- WarioWare: variety of mini-games and quick, skill-based challenges.
- Nintendo World Championships: competition based on video game challenges and participant performance.
