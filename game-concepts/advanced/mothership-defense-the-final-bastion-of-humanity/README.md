# Mothership Defense: The Final Bastion of Humanity

## Concept

A space strategy and defense game inspired by Homeworld, in which the player commands humanity's last mothership during a devastating alien invasion. The mothership represents the final bastion of human civilization: its destruction means the end of humanity.

To protect it, the player controls a fleet of autonomous ships that automatically attack enemy forces, functioning as mobile defense towers on a dynamic, three-dimensional space battlefield.

Waves of enemies emerge from different directions, featuring varied compositions, speeds, and strategies. The player must position units precisely, organize defensive formations, and manage resources to respond to threats. Each ship has its own characteristics and capabilities, requiring strategic combinations to protect the mothership's vulnerable points.

As the invasion progresses, attacks become more intense and unpredictable. New threats demand constant adaptation, while unit losses and resource limitations make every decision even more critical. The fleet must continuously evolve and reorganize to withstand the growing pressure.

Amid the chaos of a space war, the mission goes beyond defeating waves of enemies. The player must preserve the mothership's core at all costs, as it is humanity's last hope for survival.

## Objective

Defend the mothership against successive waves of alien invaders by using a fleet of autonomous ships, strategic positioning, and resource management to ensure humanity's survival.

## Core Mechanics

- Space defense based on waves of enemies attacking the mothership from different directions.
- Fleet of autonomous ships with automatic attack systems, functioning as mobile defense towers.
- Three-dimensional unit positioning, allowing players to create defensive lines, interceptions, and strategic formations.
- Different ship classes, each with specific attributes, weapons, range, and roles.
- Resource management system for building, repairing, upgrading, and replacing units.
- Difficulty progression, with increasingly numerous, resilient, and diverse enemy waves.
- Enemies with different attack patterns, speeds, and tactical behaviors.
- Fleet technology and mothership defense system upgrade mechanics.
- Loss and damage management, requiring decisions about which units to repair, replace, or preserve.
- Special events and attacks that require rapid defensive reorganization.
- Permanent defeat condition tied to the destruction of the mothership.

## References

- Homeworld: three-dimensional space combat, fleet control, and tactical formations.
- Tower Defense: defense against successive waves of enemies and strategic unit placement.
- They Are Billions: escalating pressure from enemy hordes and defense of an essential structure.
- Riftbreaker: combination of base defense, resource management, and wave-based attacks.
