# Rubber Hose Run: The Final Reel

## Concept

A platforming action game built around a race against time, inspired by classic 1930s animation and the structure of traditional platform games.

The protagonist is a cartoon character who becomes trapped inside their own film reel. To escape, they must traverse the animated world and reach the end of the film before the projector overheats and burns the reel, permanently erasing their existence.

The setting recreates the visual characteristics of early animation: black-and-white imagery, exaggeratedly elastic movements, gloved characters, caricatured expressions, hand-drawn backgrounds, and imperfections typical of old film, such as scratches, dust, stains, and image flickering.

The world is not merely a passive backdrop. Elements of the animation, secondary characters, and even environmental objects can come to life and attempt to prevent the escape. Environments can transform, objects can change their behavior, and obstacles can appear unpredictably, as if the cartoon itself were trying to keep the protagonist trapped.

Time pressure is constant. The player has less than a minute to traverse each sequence and reach the end of the film reel. Every obstacle requires reflexes, precision, and adaptability, while the projector continues consuming the film.

## Objective

Escape from the film reel and reach the end of the film before the projector burns it, avoiding obstacles, hostile characters, and traps within the animated world.

## Core Mechanics

- Platforming and time-based challenges, with a time limit of less than one minute to complete each sequence.
- Elastic movement inspired by Rubber Hose animation, featuring jumps, dodges, and exaggerated movements.
- Black-and-white environments with the visual style of 1930s cartoons.
- Old film visual effects, including scratches, dust, stains, shaking, and image flickering.
- Interactive environments where objects, backgrounds, and characters can come to life and interfere with the escape.
- Dynamic obstacles and traps requiring reflexes and precision.
- Progression through animated sequences, featuring increasingly complex challenges and situations.
- Constant pressure from the projector, represented by the progressive deterioration of the film.
- Visual humor and absurd situations inspired by classic animation.

## References

- Super Mario Bros.: classic platforming structure, level progression, obstacles, and precise jumping.
- Cuphead: 1930s animation aesthetic, Rubber Hose characters, and traditional animation.
- Bendy and the Ink Machine: an old animation world that transforms into a hostile environment.
- Super Meat Boy: precision platforming and reflex-based challenges.
- Rayman Origins: expressive animation, fluid movement, and interactive environments.
