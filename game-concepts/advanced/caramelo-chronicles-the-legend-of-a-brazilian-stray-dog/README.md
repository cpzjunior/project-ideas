# Caramelo Chronicles: The Legend of a Brazilian Stray Dog

## Concept

An adventure and survival game in which the player takes on the role of Caramelo, a Brazilian stray dog who turns the chaotic streets of Rio de Janeiro into an ongoing journey of survival and freedom.

Every day, Caramelo must find food, explore different parts of the city, and deal with the dangers of urban life. Along the way, he crosses paths with other stray dogs, builds relationships, and faces the challenges of an unpredictable environment.

From motorcycle chases and improvised getaways to food disputes and moments of rest along the shores of Copacabana, the player discovers both the chaos and the beauty of life on the streets of one of Brazil's most iconic cities.

## Objective

Survive and claim your place on the streets of Rio de Janeiro by exploring the city, finding food, avoiding dangers, and building relationships with other stray dogs.

## Main Mechanics

- Urban exploration: roam the streets, neighborhoods, squares, and beaches of Rio de Janeiro.
- Survival: search for food, water, and safe places to rest.
- Urban dangers: escape from motorcycles, vehicles, people, and other threats.
- Improvised getaways: use the urban environment to escape pursuits and dangerous situations.
- Interaction with other dogs: encounter, meet, and interact with other stray dogs living in the city.
- Territorial disputes: compete for food, shelter, and safe spaces.
- Daily cycle: alternate between exploration, resource gathering, dangerous encounters, and moments of rest.

## References

- Urban adventure and survival games.
- Exploration games featuring animals as protagonists.
