# Around the World in 8 Days: The Influencer Challenge

## Concept

An adventure, strategy, and time-management game in which the player takes on the role of an influencer who accepts an outrageous bet: travel around the world in just eight days to prove that she is the greatest content creator on the planet.

The journey is not simply about traveling quickly between countries. On each continent, the protagonist must create viral content in real time, take part in local challenges, and maintain her relevance on social media. Each destination represents an opportunity to gain new followers, increase engagement, and strengthen her reputation, but also requires logistical planning and quick decision-making.

The experience unfolds like a global reality show, in which the world constantly reacts to the protagonist's presence. Rivals may sabotage recordings, fans may interfere with travel plans, and unexpected events may turn a post into a worldwide success or trigger a public relations crisis.

The player must balance speed, creativity, route planning, and reputation management. The race against the clock becomes a battle for the audience's attention, where the real opponent is the algorithm.

## Objective

Complete a trip around the world in eight days, visiting the required destinations and creating sufficiently engaging content to earn the title of the greatest influencer on the planet before time runs out.

## Main Mechanics

- Route planning and travel between countries and continents.
- Time management with an overall eight-day deadline.
- Different modes of transportation, each with its own costs, speeds, and limitations.
- Local challenges requiring the creation of specific content.
- Real-time content creation and publishing system.
- Metrics for followers, reach, engagement, and virality.
- Random events that can disrupt travel plans.
- Rivals capable of sabotaging recordings and competing for the audience's attention.
- Interactions with fans that can either help or hinder the journey.
- Reputation system with positive and negative consequences, including public relations crises and online cancellations.
- Strategic decisions that influence popularity and competition progress.

## References

- Time and resource management games.
- Adventure and exploration games.
- Social media and digital influence simulators.
- Reality shows and travel competitions.
