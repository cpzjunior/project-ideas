# Multiversal Grand Prix: Beyond the Timeline

## Concept

A futuristic racing and science fiction game in which a cosmic entity, existing beyond the conventional dimensions of space and time, kidnaps the consciousnesses of the greatest drivers in history and forces them to compete in a multiversal championship.

The competitors are placed in machines capable of traveling across different eras and realities, racing on circuits that range from classic tracks inspired by 1950s motorsports to impossible futuristic environments built in dimensions where the laws of physics and the very flow of time are unstable.

Each driver carries distorted fragments of their original identity, resulting from their existence across different timelines. Personalities, memories, and characteristics from their historical careers may emerge in unexpected ways, creating alternate versions of the competitors.

The tracks feature phenomena that defy traditional racing conventions. Sections may undergo temporal alterations, gravity may shift during a lap, circuits may fragment into parallel dimensions, and events from different eras may coexist along the same course.

As the championship progresses, the drivers discover that the competition is more than just a cosmic spectacle. The tournament is part of a much larger experiment, whose results could determine the future of speed itself throughout the universe. Winning may be the only way to regain their freedom and understand why they were chosen.

## Objective

Compete in and win the multiversal championship by mastering circuits from different eras and realities, while uncovering the true intentions of the entity behind the tournament.

## Core Mechanics

- Multiversal racing championship featuring drivers from different historical periods and timelines.
- Circuits inspired by different eras of motorsports, ranging from classic tracks to futuristic environments and abstract dimensions.
- Dynamic physics, with changes to gravity, speed, inertia, and other properties during races.
- Temporal and dimensional manipulation, including distortions, track fragmentation, and the coexistence of different timelines.
- Drivers with unique characteristics, abilities, and identities influenced by their historical origins and alternate versions.
- Specialized vehicles with different configurations, attributes, and customization options.
- Unpredictable events during races that can alter trajectories, environmental conditions, and track rules.
- Competitive progression through championships, unlocking new drivers, vehicles, eras, and dimensions.
- Narrative elements gradually revealed through championship events and results.
- Special challenges that explore the unique properties of each reality.

## References

- F-Zero: high-speed futuristic racing and extreme circuits.
- Wipeout: anti-gravity vehicles, futuristic tracks, and science fiction environments.
- Mario Kart: racing with unpredictable elements and circuits featuring distinctive mechanics.
- Trackmania: extreme circuits, precision challenges, and exploration of the limits of physics.
- Multiverse and science fiction: alternate realities, timelines, and entities beyond space and time.
