# Mecha Idols Brawl: Glitter, Gears & Guns

## Concept

A competitive combat game that combines mecha battles, musical performances, and idol spectacles in futuristic arenas. Participants control specially designed idol mechs built to fight, dance, and perform synchronized routines, turning every confrontation into a stylized spectacle of destruction.

Battles take place to the rhythm of music, combining explosive attacks, choreographies, missile barrages, and special abilities. Vibrant arenas host alien audiences, theatrical lighting, spotlights, and chaotic stage effects, creating an environment where combat is also a performance.

Each confrontation requires a combination of musical timing, strategy, and execution precision. Players can chain movements, attacks, and abilities into choreographed sequences, creating performances that impress the audience and put pressure on opponents.

The spectacle is not merely visual: charisma, rhythm, and firepower are all essential elements for dominating the arena. The result is an over-the-top competition in which victory depends as much on combat prowess as on performance quality.

## Objective

Compete in musical battles between idol mechs, defeating opponents and winning over the audience through combinations of combat, rhythm, and performance.

## Core Mechanics

- Mecha combat featuring melee attacks, firearms, missiles, and special abilities.
- Musical rhythm system that influences the timing and execution of movements.
- Choreographies and attack sequences that can be chained into performances.
- Mecha customization, including weapons, abilities, appearance, and idol-themed visual elements.
- Charisma and audience reaction system that influences performance scores.
- Themed arenas featuring lighting, stage effects, obstacles, and interactive show elements.
- Competitions against different opponents, musical styles, and performance challenges.
- Competitive progression based on victories, performance, and audience recognition.

## References

- Mecha: giant robot combat and futuristic weaponry.
- Idol culture: choreographed performances, charisma, and audience interaction.
- Music rhythm games: synchronization of actions with music.
- Competitive fighting games: combos, precision, strategy, and mastery of abilities.
