# Galactic Burnout: Lovecraftian Customer Support

## Concept

A simulation and survival game with dark humor, set in a decaying intergalactic society. The player takes on the role of an ordinary worker at an intergalactic call center, responsible for assisting cosmic creatures every day and solving problems as absurd as the customers themselves.

Life outside working hours is no easier. Living in a run-down hostel station, the protagonist must deal with chaotic transportation, surreal bills, and the challenges of meeting basic needs in an unwelcoming society.

Every decision affects their money, health, and sanity. Between impossible customers, poor working conditions, and events that defy human comprehension, simply surviving the daily grind is already an achievement.

Amid all this cosmic and bureaucratic chaos, the goal is surprisingly simple: build a minimally decent life and manage to retire in one piece.

## Objective

Manage work, finances, health, and sanity throughout the character's life, seeking enough stability to survive the daily grind and achieve a peaceful retirement.

## Core Mechanics

- Handle calls from cosmic creatures and entities with absurd problems.
- Resolve requests by following corporate procedures and policies.
- Manage money, expenses, and bills.
- Maintain physical health and mental sanity.
- Travel through chaotic intergalactic transportation systems.
- Manage basic needs and living conditions.
- Make decisions with consequences for personal and professional life.
- Progress through the character's life in pursuit of retirement.
