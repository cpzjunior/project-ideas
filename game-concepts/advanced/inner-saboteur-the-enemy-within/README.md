# Inner Saboteur: The Enemy Within

## Concept

A narrative comedy adventure inspired by the idea of emotions and personality traits manifesting as characters within the human mind. The player follows the life of a protagonist as different personalities compete for influence over their decisions, creating internal conflicts that frequently turn everyday situations into complete disasters.

Anxiety, impulsiveness, overconfidence, procrastination, perfectionism, and paranoia each have their own personalities, opinions, and interests. Each interprets situations differently and tries to convince the others that its approach is the right one. Their arguments, alliances, and rivalries directly affect the protagonist's choices and behavior.

The narrative unfolds through episodes featuring situations such as job interviews, romantic dates, trips, professional presentations, and family gatherings. What seems simple to an outside observer can turn into complete chaos inside the protagonist's mind.

The humor comes from the contrast between everyday situations and the absurd disputes among the characters responsible for managing the protagonist's mind. Not all personalities are necessarily harmful: depending on the situation, traits that usually get in the way can actually prove useful.

## Objective

Follow the protagonist through different everyday situations, making decisions and managing conflicts among their personalities to deal with challenges, unexpected consequences, and frequently hilarious situations.

## Core Mechanics

- Episodic narrative featuring everyday situations and social, professional, and personal challenges.
- Inner characters with their own personalities, opinions, motivations, and behaviors.
- Dialogues and arguments between different personalities, featuring alliances, rivalries, and conflicts.
- Narrative choices that allow players to influence the protagonist's decisions and behavior.
- Influence system in which different personalities compete for control over decisions.
- Unexpected consequences resulting from interactions and conflicts between characters.
- Relationships between personalities that evolve throughout the narrative.
- Comedic situations built around human contradictions and attempts to manage one's own mind.

## References

- Inside Out: emotions represented as characters with distinct personalities within the human mind.
- Narrative adventures and comedy games built around dialogue, choices, and consequences.
