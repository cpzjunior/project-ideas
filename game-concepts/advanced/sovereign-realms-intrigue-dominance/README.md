# Sovereign Realms: Intrigue & Dominance

## Concept

A strategy, management, and political intrigue game in which the player rules a kingdom they never see directly. Rather than personally witnessing events or controlling every aspect of the territory, the sovereign depends entirely on their advisors to understand what is happening within and beyond the kingdom's borders.

Every piece of information arrives through reports, recommendations, documents, and testimonies that may be incomplete, contradictory, or deliberately manipulated. A war may never have happened, a crisis may have been exaggerated to justify extraordinary measures, and an apparently loyal ally may be conspiring against the crown.

Advisors are not merely sources of information: they have their own interests, ambitions, alliances, and agendas. They may compete for influence, conceal events, distort reports, or offer different interpretations of the same situation. The composition of the council and the trust placed in its members directly shape the sovereign's perception of the kingdom.

Without direct access to reality, the player must delegate authority, interpret contradictions, investigate suspicions, and make decisions even when information is insufficient. Their choices can strengthen institutions, provoke conflicts, favor certain groups, or jeopardize the kingdom's stability.

The central challenge is not merely to rule, but to build a reliable understanding of a world presented to the player through the people who are supposed to serve them.

## Objective

Rule and preserve the kingdom's sovereignty by managing its institutions, political relationships, and conflicts through indirect information. The player must build a reliable network of advisors, identify manipulation, and make decisions that maintain or expand the crown's power.

## Core Mechanics

- Indirect governance, with no direct view of the kingdom's territory or events.
- Advisor system featuring distinct personalities, interests, ambitions, loyalties, and personal agendas.
- Reports, recommendations, and testimonies with varying levels of accuracy and reliability.
- Contradictory information about political, economic, military, and social events.
- Deliberate information manipulation, omissions, lies, and distortions by advisors.
- Delegation of authority and distribution of responsibilities among council members.
- Investigation of suspicions, verification of reports, and comparison of different information sources.
- Relationships of trust, influence, and rivalry between advisors and political factions.
- Strategic decision-making under uncertainty, taking immediate and long-term consequences into account.
- Emergent events in which decisions based on false or incomplete information can shift the kingdom's political balance.
- Evolution of the power structure as the sovereign strengthens, weakens, or reorganizes the council.

## References

- Yes, Your Grace: governance decisions and kingdom management.
- The Council: political intrigue, character relationships, and information manipulation.
- Crusader Kings: power dynamics, personal ambitions, alliances, and conspiracies.
- Suzerain: political governance, decisions under pressure, and conflicting information.
