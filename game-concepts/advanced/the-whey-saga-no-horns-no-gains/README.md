# The Whey Saga: No Horns, No Gains

## Concept

A comedy and sports simulation game starring Betinho, a man abandoned by his wife who, on top of losing his marriage, becomes the laughingstock of his friends. After being left with literally nothing but the rug and his self-esteem on the floor, he earns the nickname "Aladdin."

Rather than surrendering to defeat, Betinho decides to turn his humiliation into fuel for a new chapter in his life: entering the world of bodybuilding and reaching the pinnacle of the sport.

His journey begins in modest gyms, surrounded by improvised equipment, ridiculous workouts, and questionable advice from fellow gym-goers. Over time, he faces increasingly demanding challenges, extravagant competitions, and a fitness community full of caricatured characters, rivalries, and gym memes.

His physical evolution is accompanied by a personal transformation, driven by discipline, dedication, and an increasingly intense obsession with hypertrophy. With every achievement, Betinho moves closer to muscular glory and proves that he can overcome his past, even if his friends never stop reminding him how it all began.

His philosophy is simple and non-negotiable: no horns, no gains.

## Objective

Transform Betinho from a disgraced man into a bodybuilding legend, improving his physical condition, winning titles, and defeating opponents in increasingly prestigious and surreal competitions.

## Core Mechanics

- Weight training featuring different exercises, equipment, and workout routines.
- Physical progression through attributes such as strength, endurance, muscle mass, and definition.
- Management of workouts, rest, nutrition, and supplementation.
- Participation in bodybuilding challenges and competitions.
- Progression through gyms, events, and championships at different levels.
- Interactions with caricatured characters from the fitness world, including friends, trainers, and rivals.
- Comedic situations, gym memes, and absurd challenges throughout the journey.
- Development of Betinho's reputation as a bodybuilder and his journey to overcome his past.

## References

- Fitness Boxing: physical training integrated into the gaming experience.
- Punch Club: attribute progression, routine management, and competitive advancement.
- Wii Fit: physical exercises presented in an accessible and playful way.
- Muscle March: exaggerated aesthetics and humor related to the world of bodybuilding.
