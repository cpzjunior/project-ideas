# Shabby Casino: The House Always Loses

## Concept

A simulation, management, and strategy game with a humorous tone, in which the player takes control of a run-down casino in Las Vegas, inherited from their grandparents. The protagonist is an eccentric character, passionate about gambling and known for making questionable financial decisions. Their inheritance, however, is far from a fortune: the establishment is practically bankrupt, with deteriorating facilities, few customers, and a reputation forgotten even by its former regulars.

The mission is to transform this ruin into one of the most famous and busiest casinos in Las Vegas. To achieve this, the player must renovate the facilities, hire employees, attract customers, manage gaming tables, control finances, and build the establishment's reputation. Along the way, they will encounter professional gamblers capable of threatening profits, employees with peculiar personalities, unpredictable customers, and inspectors who seem to have developed a personal obsession with the casino.

The biggest problem, however, is the owner himself. In addition to running the house, he also loves to gamble. Whenever he wins a bet, the dealer solemnly announces, "Congratulations once again, boss!", after all, the money has simply moved from one pocket to the other. When he loses, the financial manager shows up to remind him that the renovation budget wasn't exactly play money.

Amid questionable renovations, dubious management decisions, absurd characters, and increasingly unlikely situations, the player must rebuild the family legacy and expand the business in an extremely competitive city. The challenge is to build an entertainment empire without letting the owner's own bets, financial problems, or regulatory inspections bring the casino down.

## Objective

Rebuild the inherited casino, transforming it from a nearly bankrupt establishment into one of Las Vegas's leading destinations. The player must expand the facilities, attract customers, maintain profitability, and build a reputation capable of sustaining the business's growth.

## Core Mechanics

- Renovation and expansion of facilities, including gaming floors, gaming tables, entertainment areas, and services.
- Financial management, including control of expenses, revenue, debts, and investments.
- Hiring, training, and managing employees with distinct personalities and characteristics.
- Management of gaming tables, operational capacity, and variety of attractions.
- Attracting and retaining customers with different profiles, behaviors, and spending power.
- Reputation and popularity system influenced by service quality and customer experiences.
- Interactions with professional gamblers who can compromise the profitability of gaming tables.
- Regulatory inspections and compliance, including audits, fines, and possible shutdowns.
- Random events and comedic situations involving customers, employees, and management problems.
- The owner's participation in gambling, with financial consequences for the casino itself.
- Business progression, allowing the player to transform a small, rundown establishment into a large-scale entertainment complex.
