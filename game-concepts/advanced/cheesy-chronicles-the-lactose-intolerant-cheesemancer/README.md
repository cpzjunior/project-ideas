# Cheesy Chronicles: The Lactose-Intolerant Cheesemancer

## Concept

A comedic fantasy RPG in which the player controls a powerful wizard specializing in an ancient form of cheese-based magic. During battles, the protagonist can restore health and mana by devouring different types of cheese, each providing distinct magical benefits and effects.

The problem is that the wizard is lactose intolerant. Every meal grants a massive power boost but also triggers unpredictable and hilarious side effects that can disrupt or even benefit combat. The player must deal with the consequences of their choices while using cheese-inspired spells, facing eccentric enemies, and exploring a world where cheese is considered one of the most powerful sources of ancient magic.

Amid magical explosions, questionable powers, and digestive crises, the protagonist must balance strategy and chaos to save the kingdom.

## Objective

Defeat the threats endangering the kingdom by mastering the ancient magic of cheese and learning to use its powers and side effects strategically.

## Main Mechanics

- Cheese-based magic: cast spells inspired by different types of cheese, with offensive, defensive, and support effects.
- Cheese consumption: devour different varieties to restore health and mana and gain temporary power boosts.
- Lactose intolerance: each type of cheese can trigger unpredictable side effects that interfere with combat.
- Dynamic side effects: negative or positive consequences that can alter battle conditions.
- Strategic combat: balance the immediate benefits of consuming cheese against the risks of its side effects.
- Eccentric enemies: face creatures and adversaries inhabiting a fantasy universe built around the magic of cheese.
- Magical progression: develop abilities and expand the repertoire of cheese-based spells and powers.

## References

- Fantasy RPGs with comedic themes.
- Games featuring random effect systems and unpredictable consequences.
- Medieval fantasy and elemental magic.
