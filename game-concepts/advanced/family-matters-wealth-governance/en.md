# Family Matters: Wealth & Governance

## Concept

A strategy and management game inspired by Crusader Kings II, bringing its dynamics of dynastic power, family intrigue, and succession into the contemporary world.

The player takes on the role of chairman of a powerful family dynasty, responsible for preserving and expanding the wealth accumulated over generations. Managing businesses, investments, and properties is only part of the challenge. The real game takes place within the family itself, where each member has ambitions, interests, alliances, and resentments that can influence the future of the family empire.

Children compete for the patriarch's favor, siblings vie for positions of influence, marriages can strengthen alliances, and dissatisfied heirs may conspire to take control of the businesses. Executives, advisors, and external allies also have their own interests, making governance a delicate exercise in negotiation and manipulation.

The player must balance financial growth, business stability, and family relationships while facing inheritance disputes, successions, betrayals, scandals, and rivalries with other dynasties. Every decision can strengthen the family legacy or trigger conflicts capable of jeopardizing everything that has been built.

## Objective

Preserve and expand the dynasty's power and wealth across generations by managing family businesses, maintaining alliances, and ensuring a succession capable of perpetuating the family's legacy.

## Core Mechanics

- Family Governance: manage the dynasty and distribute responsibilities, positions, and influence among its members.
- Business Management: control companies, investments, properties, and equity stakes.
- Family Relationships: monitor personalities, ambitions, loyalties, resentments, and rivalries among relatives.
- Intrigue and Conspiracies: deal with betrayals, internal disputes, sabotage, and attempts to seize power.
- Succession and Inheritance: prepare heirs, establish succession rules, and manage disputes over control of the family's wealth.
- Strategic Alliances: use marriages, agreements, and personal relationships to strengthen the family's position.
- External Influence: negotiate with executives, investors, politicians, and other powerful families.
- Dynastic Events: face crises, scandals, deaths, divorces, and events that alter the balance of power within the family.
- Generational Progression: follow the dynasty's evolution and transfer control to its successors.
