# The Debt Collector: Dead Assets Pay No Debts

## Concept

An action, adventure, and RPG game set in an intergalactic universe where the player works as a debt collector for the Intergalactic Bank. Their job is to travel across dangerous planets, space stations, and decaying colonies to recover credits from debtors ranging from indebted aliens and dangerous criminals to corporations and organized crime syndicates.

The protagonist holds a special license authorizing the use of extreme force during collection operations. Intimidation, physical combat, negotiation, and various forms of coercion are all part of the job. However, the bank enforces one ironclad rule: the debtor must not die. If that happens, the collector becomes fully responsible for the outstanding debt, turning a credit recovery operation into a potential personal financial disaster.

Each mission requires assessing the risks, understanding the debtor's profile, and choosing how to handle the collection. Some can be persuaded to negotiate, while others will attempt to escape, hire mercenaries, or turn the visit into an ambush. There are also debtors protected by powerful organizations, corrupt corporations, and local authorities who want no interference in their business.

Amid hostile worlds, decaying environments, and interplanetary conspiracies, the player discovers that many debts conceal stories and interests far greater than simple delinquency. The Intergalactic Bank itself may have questionable motives, while its client portfolio reveals complex relationships between economic power, organized crime, and galactic politics.

The experience combines combat, negotiation, and risk management with dark humor about the operations of a financial institution that considers virtually any problem solvable through an in-person debt collection visit.

## Objective

Recover debts across different regions of the galaxy, meet the targets established by the Intergalactic Bank, and maintain personal financial stability. The player must complete collections using force, intimidation, or negotiation, without ever killing debtors and becoming responsible for their outstanding debts.

## Core Mechanics

- Exploration of planets, space stations, and decaying colonies during debt collection missions.
- Contract system featuring debtors of different species, profiles, assets, and threat levels.
- Physical combat and specialized equipment used to intimidate or incapacitate debtors.
- Negotiation system for establishing agreements, installment plans, and alternative forms of debt recovery.
- Intimidation and coercion as tools for influencing debtor behavior.
- Non-lethal target rule: killing a debtor transfers their entire outstanding debt to the collector.
- Risk assessment before and during each operation, taking the environment, security, and potential participants into account.
- Debtor reactions, including resistance, escape attempts, ambushes, hiring mercenaries, and attempts to negotiate.
- Career progression, granting access to more valuable contracts and increasingly dangerous debtors.
- Management of personal finances, equipment, and obligations to the bank.
- Investigations into criminal organizations, corporations, and political interests connected to outstanding debts.
- Consequences of decisions affecting reputation, relationships with factions, and future collection opportunities.
