# Hamster vs. Capybara: Whisker Warfare

## Concept

A military strategy game with humor and management elements, in which hamsters and capybaras wage an absurd war for territorial dominance.

The player commands an army belonging to one of the two factions, each with its own characteristics, units, and combat styles. Hamsters are agile and resourceful, specializing in improvised constructions and creative equipment. Capybaras are resilient and organized, favoring collective strength, defense, and territorial control.

The conflict takes place in environments inspired by everyday human life, transformed into miniature battlefields. Ordinary objects are converted into weapons, vehicles, and military structures: hamster wheels become tanks, toys are transformed into aircraft, and food is used as ammunition and supplies.

In addition to fighting rival forces, the player must build bases, organize troops, manage resources, and plan military operations. Each battle combines strategy and improvisation, with chaotic and unpredictable situations that can change the course of the conflict.

## Objective

Command your faction, conquer territories, and defeat the rival army by using strategy, creativity, and improvised equipment to achieve battlefield supremacy.

## Core Mechanics

- Two playable factions, hamsters and capybaras, each with distinct units, abilities, and strategic playstyles.
- Construction and expansion of military bases using improvised materials and everyday objects.
- Recruitment, organization, and management of squads with different roles.
- Creation and deployment of improvised military vehicles, including tanks, aircraft, and other equipment.
- Development of creative weapons and ammunition using food and everyday objects.
- Strategic combat featuring offensive and defensive operations and territorial control.
- Logistics and resource management systems to keep troops operational.
- Troop morale influenced by battle conditions, leadership, and operational outcomes.
- Special units with unique abilities and specific tactical roles.
- Interactive environments with elements that can be used strategically during combat.
- Unpredictable events and comedic situations that alter battle conditions.

## References

- Military strategy and army management games.
- Base-building and territorial control games.
- Strategy games featuring asymmetric factions and specialized units.
