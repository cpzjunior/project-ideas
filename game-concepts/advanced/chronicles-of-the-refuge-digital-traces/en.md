# Chronicles of the Refuge: Digital Traces

## Concept

A contemplative experience inspired by old personal blogs, where the player finds an intimate and anonymous space to document their life without filters, an audience, or fear of judgment. There are no followers, likes, or algorithms, just a place to preserve thoughts, memories, personal reflections, and stories about people who have left their mark on their journey.

This space takes the form of an isolated ranch, serving as a physical and symbolic representation of the player's life story. Each entry can generate elements that become part of the environment: letters turn into objects, memories occupy spaces, important people leave their marks, and lived experiences bring changes to the structure and atmosphere of the house.

The construction of this refuge happens in a hybrid and organic way. Entries generate elements and transformations, but the player decides how to incorporate them, where to place them, and which ones to preserve. Over time, the ranch becomes a unique representation of their journey, allowing them to revisit memories and discover connections between different moments in their life.

On rare occasions, the refuge may also receive anonymous fragments from other people. These fragments appear as letters, photographs, or found objects, bringing silent echoes of other people's experiences that may connect with the player's own memories. Privacy remains a central principle: each person decides what remains exclusively theirs and what can be shared anonymously.

The goal is not to observe other people's stories or build a perfect house, but to create a place that represents who you are, what you have experienced, and the people who have been part of your story.

## Objective

Build and preserve a personal refuge that represents your own journey by documenting experiences, incorporating memories into the environment, and discovering connections between different moments in life.

## Main Mechanics

- Personal journaling: write thoughts, memories, personal reflections, and experiences in a private and anonymous environment.
- Symbolic construction: transform entries into objects, letters, photographs, and elements that become part of the ranch.
- Refuge organization: decide where to place the elements generated by memories and how to organize the different spaces.
- Organic transformation: modify the structure, decoration, and atmosphere of the house according to the story documented by the player.
- Revisiting memories: explore the ranch and interact with elements that represent past experiences.
- Connections between entries: discover relationships and meanings between different memories and events.
- Anonymous fragments: occasionally find entries from other people, presented as echoes of human experiences.
- Privacy controls: define which entries and elements remain private and which can be shared anonymously.
