# Venture Classes: The Essentials of Entrepreneurship

## Concept

An educational business simulator in which the player takes on the role of a budding entrepreneur looking to start and build their first company. Rather than presenting business concepts in a purely theoretical way, the game puts players in front of practical decisions involved in starting and running a business.

From choosing a business model to launching operations, players must deal with bureaucracy, paperwork, startup costs, service providers, and the organization of internal processes. They must decide which activities will be handled in-house and which will be outsourced, considering services such as accounting, human resources, legal support, and technology.

As the company begins operating, new challenges emerge involving financial management, cash flow, suppliers, customers, tax obligations, and growth. Seemingly simple decisions can have significant consequences, while planning and management mistakes can jeopardize the business's survival.

The goal is to turn entrepreneurship into an interactive learning experience, allowing players to understand the real challenges of running a company through experimentation, strategic decisions, and simulated consequences.

## Objective

Found and manage a sustainable company while learning to deal with the main challenges of entrepreneurship. Players must make decisions that keep the business financially healthy, structure its operations, and overcome the common obstacles faced by new entrepreneurs.

## Core Mechanics

- Creation and structuring of a company, including business model selection and formal registration.
- Management of bureaucracy, documentation, licenses, and legal obligations.
- Financial planning, cost control, cash flow, and working capital management.
- Decisions about outsourcing or bringing in-house services such as accounting, HR, legal support, and technology.
- Hiring and management of employees and service providers.
- Administration of suppliers, customers, contracts, and operations.
- Business risk management and identification of problems that could jeopardize the company.
- Simulation of financial and operational consequences resulting from decisions.
- Business progression, allowing players to expand operations and tackle increasingly complex challenges.
- Educational scenarios based on common situations encountered in the day-to-day operations of small businesses.
