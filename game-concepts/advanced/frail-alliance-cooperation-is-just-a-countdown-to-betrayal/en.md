# Frail Alliance: Cooperation is Just a Countdown to Betrayal

## Concept

A battle royale structured around three distinct phases, in which initial cooperation is merely a temporary truce before an inevitable fight for individual survival.

During the first few minutes of the match, all players must cooperate to survive AI-controlled threats and gather resources in a hostile environment. This period creates an opportunity to form temporary alliances, share equipment, and prepare strategies for the confrontation ahead.

In the second phase, the truce ends and PvP is enabled. Each player must fend for themselves, with the option to betray former allies, compete for resources, and eliminate opponents to gain an advantage.

In the final phase, the locations of surviving players are periodically revealed through global pings on the map. This information reduces the opportunities to remain hidden, forces encounters between players, and accelerates the confrontation until only one winner remains.

The concept explores the tension between cooperation and competition, placing players in a situation where trusting others may be essential for surviving the early stages, but potentially fatal once the alliance no longer serves a purpose.

## Objective

Survive all three phases of the match by using initial cooperation to prepare, overcoming opponents during the confrontation, and being the last player alive.

## Core Mechanics

- Cooperative Survival: face AI-controlled threats and gather resources during the initial phase.
- Temporary Alliances: cooperate with other players to increase survival and preparation chances.
- Transition to PvP: end the cooperation phase and enable combat between all players.
- Resource Gathering and Management: obtain equipment and supplies to improve survival chances.
- Betrayal and Confrontation: break alliances, compete for resources, and eliminate former teammates.
- Global Tracking: periodically reveal the locations of surviving players through map pings.
- Final Showdown: progressively reduce opportunities for escape and hiding, forcing survivors to confront one another.
