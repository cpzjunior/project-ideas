# Jack of No Trades: Professional Impostor

## Concept

A comedy and simulation game in which the player takes on the role of a jack-of-all-trades hired to replace absent professionals in emergency situations. The problem is that they never have any experience in the profession they are called in to fill.

From surgeon to air traffic controller, from sommelier to detective, each job presents a new challenge in which the protagonist must fake competence, learn to use unfamiliar tools, and deliver minimally acceptable results, all without letting clients, colleagues, or superiors discover that they are a complete impostor.

Each replacement job presents specific situations, requiring quick thinking, improvisation, and adaptability. The player must interpret instructions, handle unfamiliar equipment, and make decisions under pressure while maintaining the convincing appearance of an experienced professional.

As the protagonist takes on new jobs, they build an unusual reputation, receiving increasingly unlikely opportunities and greater responsibilities. The greatest challenge is not mastering a profession, but surviving all of them without being exposed.

## Objective

Complete emergency job assignments by improvising solutions to fulfill the tasks of each position, satisfy employers, and protect the protagonist's identity as an impostor.

## Core Mechanics

- Temporary job system featuring replacements across different professions and work environments.
- Minigames and challenges specific to each professional activity.
- Improvised learning of unfamiliar tools, equipment, and procedures.
- Time management and task execution under pressure.
- Quick decision-making in response to unexpected situations.
- Performance and employer satisfaction system based on the results achieved.
- Suspicion system in which mistakes, inappropriate behavior, and lack of knowledge can expose the protagonist's true identity.
- Career progression based on reputation and the availability of new professional opportunities.
- Gradual increase in difficulty and responsibilities, with increasingly unlikely jobs and more absurd situations.

## References

- Situation comedies: improvisation, false identities, and absurd situations.
- Professional simulation games: activities and challenges specific to different professions.
- Time management games: task execution under pressure and activity prioritization.
