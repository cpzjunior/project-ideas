# Wild Instinct: Survive the Unyielding Hunt

## Concept

An asymmetrical multiplayer PvP survival and pursuit game set in wilderness environments where hunting is an ongoing struggle between predator and prey. Players take on the role of an animal that must survive while being tracked by an relentless hunter controlled by another player.

Matches take place across vast natural maps, with limited durations and varying environmental conditions. Each animal has unique characteristics, such as speed, endurance, senses, and camouflage abilities, offering different survival strategies. The hunter, in turn, relies on knowledge of the terrain, observation, and tracking techniques to locate their prey.

The environment plays a fundamental role in the experience. Sounds, footprints, moving vegetation, scents, and weather conditions can reveal an animal's location or trail. At the same time, changing weather and terrain features can favor escape or make pursuit more difficult.

Survival depends less on physical strength and more on the ability to interpret the environment, hide, mislead opponents, and manage movement. Every decision can leave clues that bring the hunter closer or create opportunities to escape.

## Objective

Survive until the match ends without being captured by the hunter. Players can also take on the role of predator, using tracking techniques and environmental knowledge to locate and capture their prey within the allotted time.

## Core Mechanics

- Asymmetrical PvP featuring distinct prey and hunter roles.
- Different animal species, each with unique physical characteristics and abilities.
- Tracking system based on footprints, sounds, scents, and changes in vegetation.
- Dynamic environment with weather variations affecting visibility, movement, and tracking.
- Camouflage, hiding, escaping, and evasion mechanics.
- Animal perception system with different senses and capabilities depending on the species.
- Natural maps featuring vegetation, terrain, waterways, and strategic locations.
- Time-limited matches with specific victory conditions.
- Pursuit and survival strategies based on interpreting opponents' behavior and the signs they leave behind.

## References

- Dead by Daylight: asymmetrical multiplayer structure centered on pursuit and survival.
- The Isle: animal behavior, survival, and interaction with natural environments.
- theHunter: Call of the Wild: animal tracking, footprint analysis, and environmental interpretation.
- Predator: Hunting Grounds: asymmetrical gameplay between hunters and prey.
