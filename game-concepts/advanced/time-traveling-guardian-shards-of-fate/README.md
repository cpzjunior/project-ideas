# Time-Traveling Guardian: Shards of Fate

## Concept

An action-adventure game featuring science fiction and time travel, starring a temporal guardian belonging to a secret order dedicated to preserving the timeline. His mission is not to ensure justice or prevent suffering, but to maintain the stability of reality, ensuring that historical events unfold according to known records.

Through temporal devices and rituals, the guardian accesses the memories of historical figures and relives decisive moments from the past. During these incursions, he must prevent rival factions from altering events, including tragedies, revolutions, and wars that the order itself considers necessary to preserve temporal continuity.

However, as he investigates anomalies and witnesses alternate realities that remain stable despite diverging from known history, the protagonist begins to question the principles guiding his organization. The order has always claimed that any alteration could cause reality to collapse, but perhaps this justification conceals a more disturbing truth: the current world is not necessarily the best possible one, but merely the only version they have managed to keep standing.

As he uncovers fragments of suppressed timelines, the guardian realizes that preserving stability requires maintaining suffering that could have been prevented. The order itself begins treating him as a threat when his doubts put the foundations of his mission at risk.

Caught between loyalty to the organization and the possibility of creating a different reality, he must decide whether stability justifies the suffering of countless generations or whether it is worth risking everything to break the cycle.

## Objective

Investigate temporal anomalies, preserve or challenge historical events, and uncover the true nature of the secret order. Throughout the journey, the protagonist must decide whether to maintain the known timeline or challenge its foundations in pursuit of an alternative reality.

## Core Mechanics

- Time travel and exploration of different historical periods.
- Incursions into the memories of historical figures to relive decisive events.
- Investigation of anomalies and identification of interference in the timeline.
- Missions to preserve historical events or prevent alterations carried out by rival factions.
- Combat and confrontations with temporal agents, using specialized equipment and abilities.
- Exploration of alternate realities and fragments of discarded timelines.
- Discovery of information about the secret order and its true objectives.
- Narrative decisions with consequences for temporal stability and the fate of different realities.
- Story progression through choices that can alter the protagonist's relationship with the order and his own mission.

## References

- Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time: temporal manipulation and the consequences of altering the past.
- Assassin's Creed: exploration of historical memories and participation in events from different eras.
- Chrono Trigger: time travel and branching outcomes resulting from interventions in history.
- BioShock Infinite: alternate realities, parallel universes, and the consequences of different possibilities.
- The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt: moral dilemmas, difficult choices, and narrative consequences.
