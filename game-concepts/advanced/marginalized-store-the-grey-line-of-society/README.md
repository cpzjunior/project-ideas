# Marginalized Store: The Grey Line of Society

## Concept

A simulation and management game in which the player takes on the role of a small grocery store owner in an underprivileged neighborhood, trying to sustain and grow their business in a complex economic and social environment.

The shopkeeper must deal daily with high taxes, heavy bureaucracy, operating costs, suppliers, customers, and constant pressures from the surrounding environment. Corruption is presented as a structural part of this system, influencing prices, relationships, and business decisions. The protagonist frequently finds themselves caught between the demands of formal regulations and the informal pressures of local reality, facing situations in which survival requires carefully weighing risks and consequences.

The game offers social commentary on the difficulties faced by small business owners in contexts of economic and institutional inequality. It is not simply a matter of choosing between doing the right thing and resorting to corruption, but rather understanding how different forces shape the decisions of those trying to keep a business running.

Despite the adversities, prosperity is possible. The player must find ways to manage resources, adapt operations, build relationships of trust, and balance competing interests without jeopardizing the establishment's continued operation.

## Objective

Keep the grocery store running and make it prosper while facing economic, bureaucratic, and social challenges, balancing financial survival, local relationships, and business decisions.

## Core Mechanics

- Grocery store management, including inventory, pricing, suppliers, sales, and cash flow.
- Financial management, including taxes, fees, operating expenses, debts, and profit margins.
- Bureaucracy and inspection system, featuring licenses, documents, inspections, and regulatory requirements.
- Relationships with suppliers, customers, authorities, and different neighborhood groups.
- External events and pressures affecting costs, product availability, and business operations.
- Economic and social decision-making system in which each alternative carries risks and consequences.
- Reputation and trust management, influencing business relationships and opportunities.
- Business progression, allowing players to expand the store and improve its infrastructure.
- Balancing legality, financial survival, and informal pressures from the surrounding environment.
