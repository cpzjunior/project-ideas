# Rocket & Olive: Fast and Furries

## Concept

A platforming action game with adventure and humor elements, inspired by Donkey Kong and Phineas and Ferb. The player controls Rocket, a raccoon, and Olive, an otter, two charismatic characters who must stop the plans of a mad scientist threatening to put the world in danger.

The adventure unfolds across levels filled with obstacles, traps, moving platforms, and minions carrying out the villain's plans. Each environment presents its own challenges, requiring agility, reflexes, and creativity to make progress.

Rocket and Olive have distinct traits and abilities, allowing them to approach obstacles in different ways. Creative gadgets complement their skills, offering new possibilities for overcoming traps, fighting enemies, and reaching seemingly inaccessible areas.

Progression involves traversing increasingly challenging environments, fighting the scientist's minions, and reaching the top of his facilities to stop his plans. The experience combines platforming action, problem-solving challenges, and comedic situations featuring the duo.

## Objective

Control Rocket and Olive on an adventure to defeat the mad scientist, overcome his minions, and prevent his plans from threatening the world.

## Core Mechanics

- Platforming and action, featuring jumps, climbing, obstacles, and precision challenges.
- Two playable characters, Rocket (raccoon) and Olive (otter), each with their own abilities and characteristics.
- Character switching to take advantage of their different strengths in various situations.
- Creative gadgets that allow players to overcome obstacles, fight enemies, and access new areas.
- Levels featuring moving platforms, traps, mechanisms, and alternative paths.
- Combat against the mad scientist's minions, each with different behaviors and attack patterns.
- Environmental challenges and small puzzles requiring creativity and effective use of available equipment.
- Progression through themed environments, with increasing difficulty and new obstacles.
- Boss encounters and special challenges within the antagonist's facilities.

## References

- Donkey Kong Country: platforming, obstacles, environmental exploration, and characters with complementary abilities.
- Phineas and Ferb: humor, creative inventions, and absurd situations involving science and technology.
- Ratchet & Clank: gadgets, combat, and creative use of equipment.
- It Takes Two: cooperation between characters with distinct abilities and problem-solving challenges.
