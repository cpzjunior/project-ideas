# Dictator’s Clone: From Heir to Spare

## Concept

A science fiction and dystopian thriller game in which the player takes on the role of a young man raised to believe he is the perfect son of a powerful dictator. Educated from childhood to take command of the empire, he enjoys privileges and receives training to become the regime's ideal successor.

His reality collapses when he discovers that he is not the legitimate heir, but a clone created to serve as a biological backup for the true ruler. His existence has a single purpose: to provide organs and tissues to prolong the dictator's life and ensure his continued hold on power.

Following this discovery, the protagonist is no longer treated as a member of the ruling family and becomes hunted by the very system that created him. Among his pursuers are earlier versions of himself, clones who also discovered the truth and attempted to escape but now serve as agents of the regime.

In an empire where identity is property, memories can be rewritten, and individuality is considered a threat, the protagonist must survive and uncover the full extent of the cloning program. Throughout his journey, he must confront questions about his own existence, particularly whether it is possible to build an authentic identity when even his memories and characteristics have been inherited from someone else.

## Objective

Escape the system that created him, survive the regime's pursuit, and uncover the truth about his origins while attempting to build an identity of his own and deciding what to do with the destiny imposed upon him.

## Main Mechanics

- Dystopian exploration: investigate government facilities, cloning laboratories, and different regions of the empire.
- Escape and survival: evade security forces and agents tasked with capturing or eliminating escaped clones.
- Confronting previous versions: face other clones of the protagonist, each with their own experiences and motivations.
- Narrative investigation: uncover documents, records, and evidence about the cloning program and the ruler's true history.
- Manipulated memories: question the authenticity of one's own memories and uncover possible alterations to one's identity.
- Character progression: develop skills and attributes that enable survival in different situations.
- Moral choices: decide how to deal with other clones, the regime, and the legacy of the true ruler.
- Identity building: define who the protagonist wants to become, regardless of his biological origins and original purpose.

## References

- Dystopian science fiction.
- Narratives about cloning and identity.
- Themes of authoritarianism, social control, and memory manipulation.
