# Monster Reserve Tycoon: Adventurers Infestation

## Concept

A simulation, strategy, and management game inspired by the accidental ecological collapse of Ultima Online, in which the player takes on the role of the spiritual guardian of a nature reserve in a fantasy world.

The reserve is home to a complex ecosystem made up of different species of monsters, magical creatures, predators, and wildlife. Each species has its own needs, behaviors, and ecological relationships. The player's role is to preserve the natural balance by protecting habitats, populations, and food chains.

However, the greatest threat to the reserve does not necessarily come from the monsters themselves, but from the adventurers, heroes, and hunters who constantly invade the territory in search of treasure, experience, and glory. Although they often see themselves as saviors, these invaders leave destruction in their wake, killing creatures, looting resources, and disrupting entire ecosystems.

As the guardian, the player must strategically position monsters to protect the reserve and control the presence of invaders without compromising the environmental balance. A dragon placed in the wrong territory may drive away deer populations, while a poorly positioned ogre may destroy vegetation and threaten the habitats of several species.

Each map represents a unique ecosystem, with its own characteristics, resources, species, and vulnerabilities. The player must understand the relationships between creatures and adapt their strategies to preserve biodiversity in the face of increasingly numerous, resourceful, and troublesome invaders.

The challenge lies in keeping the reserve functional and balanced while facing a peculiar threat: adventurers who believe they are doing good, but who often represent the greatest destructive force in that world.

## Objective

Preserve the ecological balance of the nature reserve by protecting its species and habitats from adventurer interference, while strategically managing monster placement and the resources of each ecosystem.

## Core Mechanics

- Management of fantasy nature reserves, each with its own ecosystems, species, and environmental characteristics.
- Food chain system featuring relationships between predators, prey, and natural resources.
- Strategic monster placement to protect territories, control populations, and repel invaders.
- Creature behavior and needs system, including feeding, reproduction, territoriality, and habitat requirements.
- Ecological balance simulation, in which the presence or absence of certain species affects the entire ecosystem.
- Environmental consequences of monster actions, including vegetation destruction, habitat changes, and population displacement.
- Invasions by adventurers, hunters, and heroes with different objectives, equipment, and behaviors.
- Invader progression system, with increasingly sophisticated strategies to overcome the reserve's defenses.
- Environmental events and natural threats that require conservation strategies to be adapted.
- Unlocking of new monster species and protective abilities.
- Maps with specific ecological challenges, requiring different management strategies.
- Reserve ecological health assessment system, considering biodiversity, population stability, and habitat preservation.

## References

- Ultima Online: accidental ecological collapse caused by interactions between creature populations and players.
- Jurassic World Evolution: management of reserves, creatures, and the balance between different needs.
- Planet Zoo: ecosystem simulation, animal behavior, and habitat management.
- Dungeon Keeper: strategic control of creatures and territory defense against adventurers.
- Ecosystem: simulation of ecological relationships and balance between species.
