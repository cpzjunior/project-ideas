# Tome Seeker: Bound by Knowledge

## Concept

A fantasy adventure and RPG game starring a young man obsessed with books and knowledge, who dreams of building the greatest library ever known. In a world where knowledge can be more valuable than gold, he travels through cities, forgotten libraries, ancient ruins, and dangerous territories in search of rare, lost, and forbidden works.

His obsession goes far beyond accumulating wealth or power. Every book he finds represents a new achievement for his collection, whether it is a simple treatise on the history of a distant kingdom, an alchemy manual, or a grimoire containing knowledge forgotten for centuries.

Books are not merely collectible items: their pages hold abilities, techniques, spells, stories, and secrets capable of expanding the protagonist's possibilities and revealing unknown aspects of the world. As his collection grows larger and more valuable, so does the attention drawn to his library.

Rival collectors, rulers, religious organizations, and forces seeking to control knowledge begin competing for access to these works. Some books are considered too dangerous to circulate freely, while others may disappear forever if they are not found.

What begins as a personal quest for books becomes a journey to preserve knowledge and build a legacy that will endure for generations.

## Objective

Find, collect, and preserve the world's largest literary collection by exploring different regions in search of rare, ancient, and forbidden books. Build a legendary library and uncover the knowledge and secrets hidden within its pages.

## Core Mechanics

- Exploration of cities, libraries, ruins, dungeons, and dangerous territories in search of books.
- Collecting system featuring works across different categories, rarity levels, origins, and levels of knowledge.
- Identification, cataloging, and organization of discovered books.
- Books that grant abilities, techniques, spells, and special knowledge.
- Discovery of stories, mysteries, and secrets through reading collected works.
- Construction and expansion of a personal library to store and preserve the collection.
- Negotiation, trading, and competition for rare works with other collectors and merchants.
- Missions involving books that are lost, forbidden, or contested by different organizations.
- Protagonist progression through accumulated knowledge and the development of his library.

## References

- The Elder Scrolls: collectible books, world lore, and exploration of libraries and ruins.
- Book of Hours: library organization and exploration of hidden knowledge.
- Moonlighter: exploration, item acquisition, and collection building.
