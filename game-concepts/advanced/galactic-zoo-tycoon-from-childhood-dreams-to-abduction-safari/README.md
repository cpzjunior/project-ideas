# Galactic Zoo Tycoon: From Childhood Dreams to Abduction Safari

## Concept

A simulation and management game combining humor and strategy. Ever since childhood, an alien has dreamed of building the most incredible zoo in the universe. Finally equipped with sufficient resources and technology, they decide to make that dream come true by traveling to different planets to capture unique creatures and assemble an intergalactic collection. Among their acquisitions are even human beings, considered exotic species by other civilizations.

However, the work doesn't end with capturing the creatures. Each one requires specific care, including feeding, comfort, and interaction. The player must also manage facilities, trade rare species, and attract visitors from across the galaxy.

A single wrong decision can lead to escapes, chaos throughout the zoo, creature uprisings, or even interplanetary invasions. Amid management challenges and absurd situations, the protagonist attempts to turn their childhood dream into reality.

## Objective

Build and manage the most extraordinary zoo in the universe, collecting rare species from different planets, keeping the creatures satisfied, and attracting intergalactic visitors.

## Core Mechanics

- Explore planets and capture exotic creatures.
- Abduct rare species, including human beings.
- Build and expand zoo facilities.
- Manage the creatures' feeding, comfort, health, and interaction needs.
- Negotiate and trade species between civilizations.
- Attract visitors and manage revenue.
- Control escapes, uprisings, and incidents.
- Manage conflicts and diplomatic consequences involving other civilizations.
