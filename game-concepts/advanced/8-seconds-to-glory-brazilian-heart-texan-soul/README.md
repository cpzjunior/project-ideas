# 8 Seconds to Glory: Brazilian Heart, Texan Soul

## Concept

A sports and narrative adventure game that follows the journey of a young man from the Brazilian countryside who dreams of becoming a professional rodeo competitor in the United States.

Inspired by the stories of Brazilians who crossed borders to gain recognition in the international rodeo circuit, the player begins his journey in small regional competitions, facing the challenges of balancing rural life with the training required to become a professional cowboy.

As he gains experience and recognition, opportunities to compete in larger tournaments emerge, until a chance to join the North American circuit completely changes his path. In the United States, the protagonist must deal with cultural differences, rivalries, competitive pressure, and the need to prove his worth in a much more demanding environment.

The gameplay combines sports simulation, career management, and storytelling, with a focus on bull riding. Each competition represents a challenge of balance, reflexes, endurance, and precision, in which staying on the animal for the regulation eight seconds can mean the difference between defeat and an opportunity to rise through the ranks.

## Objective

Build a professional rodeo career, starting in Brazilian competitions and progressing to the major tournaments in the United States, earning international recognition and becoming one of the sport's biggest names.

## Main Mechanics

- Bull riding simulation with controls based on balance, reflexes, and timing.
- Scoring system based on the competitor's performance and the difficulty of the ride.
- Career progression from local rodeos to international competitions.
- Training to improve skills and increase riding performance.
- Career management, including contracts, sponsorships, and reputation.
- Rivalry system featuring CPU-controlled competitors.
- Narrative progression with decisions that influence relationships and professional opportunities.
- Adaptation to different circuits, arenas, and competition styles.
- Special events and championships with progressively greater challenges.

## References

- Professional Bull Riders (PBR).
- Sports simulation games.
- Sports career games with narrative progression.
