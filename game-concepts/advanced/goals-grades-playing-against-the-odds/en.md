# Goals & Grades: Playing Against the Odds

## Concept

A narrative sports adventure game about a young talent from an underprivileged community who dreams of building a better future through sports. Despite financial difficulties, limited infrastructure, and everyday responsibilities, they strive to develop their potential while balancing school, family, friendships, and training.

Players can choose from different sports, each with its own career path, challenges, competitions, and professional opportunities. Soccer, basketball, boxing, skateboarding, athletics, and other sports offer different paths toward pursuing a dream.

Each sport offers a unique journey, but all share the same life context: balancing dedication to sports with education and personal decisions. Academic performance, relationships, opportunities, and choices made throughout the story influence the protagonist's development.

The goal is to encourage children and young people to discover their passions, set goals, and persevere through difficulties. The game also aims to show that education and sports do not have to be competing paths, but can go hand in hand in building a better future.

## Objective

Develop the protagonist's potential in their chosen sport, overcome personal and professional obstacles, and pursue their dreams without neglecting their education or personal life.

## Core Mechanics

- Multiple sports career paths, each featuring its own discipline, progression, training, competitions, and challenges.
- Development of athletic skills through training and participation in competitions.
- Education system featuring school attendance, exams, grades, and activities that influence future opportunities.
- Time management between school, training, rest, family, and social life.
- Choice and consequence system affecting relationships, opportunities, and personal development.
- Athletic progression, starting with local activities and competitions and advancing toward higher-level opportunities.
- Events and challenges representing financial difficulties, limited infrastructure, pressure, and lack of support.
- Character development and relationships with family members, friends, teachers, coaches, and other athletes.
- Different possibilities for personal and professional fulfillment, depending on the choices made and the path built by the player.

## References

- Sports career and athlete simulation games.
- Stories of overcoming adversity and personal growth.
- Sports games featuring character progression and narrative choices.
