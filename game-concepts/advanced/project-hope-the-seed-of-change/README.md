# Project Hope: The Seed of Change

## Concept

A social simulation, management, and narrative game in which the protagonist, a young person who has just won the lottery, decides to use their fortune to transform the lives of orphans and homeless people.

Rather than simply giving away money, they establish a support network designed to provide real opportunities for rebuilding lives. The project starts with personal funds and a modest structure, but can gradually grow to include housing, educational institutions, healthcare services, and professional training programs.

Each person taken in has a unique story, with their own talents, experiences, traumas, challenges, and dreams. Some need stability and security before they can pursue education or employment. Others have abilities they have never had the opportunity to develop. The player must understand individual needs and find suitable paths for each person's journey.

Managing the project involves financial challenges, bureaucracy, social prejudice, internal conflicts, and unexpected crises. Not every initiative produces immediate results, and seemingly simple decisions can have lasting consequences for the lives of those receiving assistance.

As the support network grows, its impact extends beyond the individuals directly benefiting from it. People who regain their independence can contribute to the community, share their knowledge, and help others in similar situations, creating a cycle of social transformation.

The experience combines resource management and character development with individual human stories. The focus is not merely on building a successful organization, but on providing the conditions for each person to rebuild their life and find their own path.

## Objective

Build and develop a sustainable support network by providing housing, education, healthcare, and professional training to vulnerable individuals, helping them achieve independence and better prospects for the future.

## Core Mechanics

- Financial management of the initial fortune and resources allocated to social projects.
- Construction and management of housing facilities, educational institutions, healthcare centers, and professional training facilities.
- Providing assistance to orphans and homeless people, taking their individual needs and circumstances into account.
- Character system featuring unique stories, talents, traumas, challenges, interests, and personal goals.
- Individual development, tracking beneficiaries' educational, professional, emotional, and social progress.
- Planning of social reintegration and workforce training programs.
- Management of employees, specialized professionals, and volunteers.
- Dealing with bureaucracy, prejudice, institutional challenges, and unexpected crises.
- Decision-making with short- and long-term consequences for individuals and the community.
- Expansion of the support network through new projects, partnerships, and community initiatives.
- Social impact system, in which individual achievements can generate positive transformations throughout the community.

## References

- This War of Mine: human stories, difficult choices, and resource management in situations of vulnerability.
- The Sims: character development, individual needs, and life journeys.
- Two Point Hospital: construction, expansion, and management of care facilities.
- Frostpunk: administrative decisions, resource management, and social consequences.
