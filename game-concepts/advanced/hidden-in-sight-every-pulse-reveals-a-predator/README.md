# Hidden in Sight: Every Pulse Reveals a Predator

## Concept

An exploration, survival, and horror game set in a vast system of unexplored caves, where exploration teams venture into the depths in search of rare resources in a hostile environment with unstable lighting and extremely limited visibility.

The depths are inhabited by chameleon-like creatures that blend perfectly into rocks, walls, and shadows, making them virtually indistinguishable from their surroundings. To survive, explorers rely on scanning pulses that briefly reveal movements, shapes, and environmental distortions, as well as weapons capable of detecting or exposing hidden threats.

However, each pulse also reveals the position of whoever emits it, attracting predators highly adapted to silent hunting. The player must balance the need to gather information against the risk of revealing their presence. In the depths, seeing more clearly may mean being found by something that has been watching all along.

## Objective

Explore the depths of the cave system, locate and collect rare resources, and survive encounters with chameleon-like creatures by using detection and combat equipment without attracting too many predators.

## Core Mechanics

- Cave exploration featuring unstable lighting and limited visibility.
- Scanning pulses that temporarily reveal camouflaged creatures, movements, and environmental changes.
- Detection system in which each pulse also reveals the explorer's position to predators.
- Creatures with environmental camouflage and hunting behaviors based on perception, movement, and signal emission.
- Specialized weapons and equipment for detecting, exposing, and confronting hidden threats.
- Collection of rare resources in progressively more dangerous areas.
- Risk management between exploration, threat detection, and preserving the player's own stealth.

## References

- Survival horror and exploration games set in underground environments.
- Stealth and hunting games based on perception and threat detection.
