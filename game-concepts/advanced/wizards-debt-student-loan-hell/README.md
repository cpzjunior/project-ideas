# Wizard’s Debt: Student Loan Hell

## Concept

An RPG, simulation, and management game with satirical humor, in which players control a magic student who discovers too late that their arcane student loan comes with predatory interest rates and extremely unfavorable contractual terms.

To prevent the goblin bank from invoking the clause that allows it to claim his soul as collateral, the young wizard must accept practically any paid job that comes his way. Opportunities range from relatively simple magical services, such as cleaning sewers using light spells, to completely absurd activities, such as delivering pizzas through teleportation or performing odd jobs for supernatural creatures.

Between shifts, the student also ventures into dangerous dungeons in search of treasures, artifacts, and rewards that can accelerate loan repayment. However, equipment, potions, repairs, and unexpected expenses can push his debt even higher.

Players must balance work, exploration, magical development, and financial management. Each opportunity presents different risks, rewards, and consequences, while interest continues to accumulate and the goblin bank closely monitors the growing debt.

The experience combines medieval fantasy, RPG elements, and economic simulation, turning the struggle to repay a student loan into a financial survival adventure.

## Objective

Pay off the arcane student loan before the debt reaches the amount required to claim the student's own soul as collateral, achieving financial independence and the freedom to finally pursue a career as a wizard.

## Core Mechanics

- Student loan system featuring installments, accumulated interest, late fees, and predatory contractual terms.
- Temporary jobs and paid missions involving magical activities and absurd services for different clients.
- Teleportation deliveries, cleaning magically contaminated areas, household services, and other odd jobs.
- Dungeon exploration featuring combat, traps, creatures, and the search for treasures and artifacts.
- Magical progression system, allowing players to learn and upgrade spells for completing jobs and overcoming challenges.
- Financial management, balancing loan payments, everyday expenses, equipment, and investments.
- Goblin bank as the lending institution, featuring debt collection, contracts, negotiations, and threats related to default.
- Unexpected events that can create income opportunities or generate additional expenses.
- Choices between conventional jobs, dangerous activities, and expeditions offering greater rewards.
- Progression based on debt reduction and the gradual achievement of financial independence.
