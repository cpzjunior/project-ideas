# Vacation Sandbox: Brazil Unplugged

## Concept

The first title in a slow gaming series that offers a virtual vacation experience, allowing players to plan and enjoy their ideal trip before taking it in real life.

The experience focuses on leisurely exploration of Brazilian destinations, ranging from beaches and coastal villages to historic cities, mountains, and highland regions. Players can travel alone or with friends and family, customizing their itinerary, choosing the weather, setting the soundtrack, and deciding how to enjoy each destination.

Rather than completing objectives or overcoming challenges, the goal is to provide the freedom to explore environments, discover landscapes, experience cultural activities, and simply enjoy the moment. Players can try out different outfits, experiment with travel itineraries, discover restaurants and local attractions, and create different versions of the same trip.

The game turns vacation planning into part of the experience itself, serving as a virtual space for relaxation, discovery, and cultural immersion. The idea is to let players temporarily disconnect from their daily routines and start traveling without ever leaving home.

## Objective

Plan and enjoy personalized virtual vacations, exploring Brazilian destinations at your own pace while experiencing local culture, landscapes, and leisure activities. The goal is to provide relaxation and inspiration for future real-life trips.

## Core Mechanics

- Free and leisurely exploration of Brazilian tourist destinations.
- Customization of itineraries, including locations, activities, and visit durations.
- Configuration of weather conditions and soundtracks to create different atmospheres.
- Customization of outfits and looks for different destinations and occasions.
- Cultural, culinary, recreational, and sightseeing activities.
- Solo travel or trips accompanied by friends and family.
- Interactions with environments, establishments, attractions, and local cultural elements.
- Travel logs documenting trips, itineraries, and experiences through photographs and keepsakes.
- No performance pressure, allowing each player to enjoy the experience at their own pace.

## References

- Animal Crossing: New Horizons: leisurely pacing, freedom, and experience customization.
- Euro Truck Simulator 2: landscape exploration and enjoyment of the journey.
- Microsoft Flight Simulator: recreation of real-world environments and destinations for exploration.
- The Sims 4: character, clothing, and social experience customization.
