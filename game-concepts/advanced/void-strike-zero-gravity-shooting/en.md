# Void Strike: Zero Gravity Shooting

## Concept

A tactical shooter and science fiction game set in space graveyards known as "Orbital Junkyards," regions filled with shipwrecks, abandoned stations, and deteriorating industrial structures. In this hostile environment, combat takes place under extreme conditions, where the absence of gravity and structural instability turn every encounter into an unpredictable situation.

Players face hostile forces on battlefields where any shot can trigger a chain reaction. Explosions can breach pressurized compartments, destroy platforms, release debris, or cause decompression events that completely transform the environment. Seemingly safe cover can disappear in seconds, while wreckage can become obstacles or lethal hazards.

The absence of gravity also changes the dynamics of combat. Movement, positioning, weapon recoil, and projectile trajectories require constant adaptation. Beyond armed opponents, the environment itself represents a permanent threat: losing contact with a structure can send players drifting into space, while a pressurization failure can turn an entire area into a deadly trap.

The experience combines tactical combat, space physics, and environmental destruction, placing players in situations where planning, precision, and the ability to react are essential for survival.

## Objective

Survive military operations in unstable space regions, eliminating hostile forces and completing tactical objectives while managing environmental hazards. Players must use the environment strategically and avoid causing the battlefield to collapse through their own actions.

## Core Mechanics

- Tactical gunplay in zero-gravity environments.
- Space movement physics, including thrust, inertia, and three-dimensional navigation.
- Projectile and weapon recoil simulation in zero gravity.
- Environmental destruction and structures with varying levels of stability.
- Explosions capable of causing decompression, structural breaches, and chain reactions.
- Pressurized and unpressurized environments, with consequences for survival.
- Use of thrusters, mobility equipment, and anchoring tools.
- Tactical use of debris, movable cover, and unstable structures during combat.
- Missions set in space graveyards, abandoned stations, and deteriorating industrial facilities.
- Tactical planning and environmental risk management during operations.

## References

- Dead Space: combat and survival in hostile space environments.
- Shattered Horizon: zero-gravity combat and three-dimensional movement.
- Rainbow Six Siege: tactical combat, positioning, and environmental destruction.
- Hardspace: Shipbreaker: space structures, physics, and interaction with wreckage.
