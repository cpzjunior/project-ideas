# World Dominators: Multiverse Wars

## Concept

A territorial strategy game inspired by Risk, set in a multiverse composed of multiple parallel versions of Earth, connected by interdimensional portals.

Different factions compete for control of these worlds, expanding their domains, conquering territories, and facing opponents in conflicts that extend beyond the borders of a single reality.

Each Earth has its own geographical features, resources, and strategic conditions. Portals connect distant territories, enabling troop movements, surprise attacks, reinforcements, and tactical retreats. Controlling these connections is just as important as conquering territories, as they determine expansion and defense opportunities across multiple dimensions.

In addition to the competing factions, there is a group of neutral vigilantes whose mission is to prevent any single power from achieving absolute dominion over the multiverse. They intervene in conflicts whenever a faction threatens to establish hegemony, introducing an unpredictable third element into the struggle for power.

Players take command of one of the factions and must balance territorial expansion, defense, diplomacy, sabotage, and control of interdimensional connections. A conquest on one Earth can open opportunities on others, but it can also expose territories that were previously protected.

## Objective

Expand your faction's influence throughout the multiverse by conquering and holding territories across different parallel Earths, while confronting rival factions and avoiding intervention by the neutral vigilantes.

## Core Mechanics

- Turn-based territorial strategy inspired by Risk, featuring regional conquest and defense.
- Multiple parallel versions of Earth, each with unique geographical and strategic characteristics.
- Interdimensional portals connecting territories and enabling troop movements between different worlds.
- Territorial expansion, troop recruitment, defensive positioning, and offensive planning.
- Surprise attacks, sabotage, reinforcements, and tactical retreats using connections between dimensions.
- Rival factions with distinct objectives, strategies, and expansion styles.
- Neutral vigilantes who intervene to prevent a single faction from achieving absolute dominion over the multiverse.
- Strategic portal control, allowing players to restrict enemy movements and protect expansion routes.
- Multidimensional consequences, where decisions made on one Earth can alter the balance of power on others.
- Victory conditions based on territorial control and the influence achieved throughout the multiverse.

## References

- Risk: territorial conquest, troop movement, and turn-based strategy.
- Axis & Allies: military planning, territorial control, and resource management.
- Civilization: territorial expansion, development, and competition between factions.
- XCOM: Enemy Unknown: strategic movement, external threats, and management of multiple conflict fronts.
