# Refuge of Beasts: The Wild Frontier

## Concept

A strategy, survival, and management game set in a dark fantasy world, in which the player leads a group of refugees seeking safety on a continent fractured by conflicts between different species and civilizations.

Each species has its own culture, territory, political organization, and interests. Constant wars, territorial disputes, and historical rivalries make coexistence between different peoples extremely complex. In this setting, the group led by the player must find a place to survive and build a community capable of withstanding external threats.

The journey involves exploring hostile territories, managing scarce resources, establishing settlements, and negotiating alliances with other civilizations. Political and strategic decisions can provide protection and trade opportunities, but may also create rivalries and attract new enemies.

The player must address the refugees' needs, preserve the community's stability, and decide how to position themselves in the conflicts shaping the continent. Not all species will be hostile, but cultural differences, political interests, and historical events can make any relationship unpredictable.

As the settlement grows, new opportunities for expansion and increasingly complex challenges emerge. Exploring unknown regions also reveals secrets about the continent, its civilizations, and the origins of the conflicts threatening its stability.

## Objective

Protect the refugees and build a prosperous, sustainable community on a divided continent, ensuring their survival through exploration, development, diplomacy, and strategic decision-making.

## Core Mechanics

- Management of essential resources, including food, water, building materials, and equipment.
- Construction and expansion of settlements, with infrastructure to house and protect refugees.
- Population management, taking into account the community's needs, security, stability, and development.
- Exploration of hostile territories, discovering resources, habitable regions, and points of interest.
- Different species and civilizations, each with their own cultures, interests, territories, and political structures.
- Diplomacy and negotiation of alliances, trade agreements, protection treaties, and relations between factions.
- Conflict and warfare system, featuring territorial disputes and consequences for settlement survival.
- Strategic decisions that influence reputation, diplomatic relations, and regional stability.
- Expeditions to explore unknown regions and uncover the continent's secrets.
- Dynamic events that can trigger crises, population displacement, opportunities, and shifts in the balance of power.

## References

- RimWorld: survival, colonist management, and community building in hostile environments.
- Songs of Syx: settlement construction, population management, and civilization development.
- Against the Storm: settlement expansion and resource management in dangerous environments.
- Mount & Blade: Warband: faction conflicts, diplomacy, and territorial disputes.
