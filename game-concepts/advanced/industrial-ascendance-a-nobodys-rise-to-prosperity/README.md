# Industrial Ascendance: A Nobody's Rise to Prosperity

## Concept

A hardcore survival RPG and simulation game set in a dark industrial fantasy universe, inspired by the height of imperialism and industrialization, between the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

The player begins as an outcast, living in absolute poverty within a brutal, unequal society dominated by imperial powers locked in constant rivalry. The protagonist is not a chosen hero, a skilled warrior, or someone destined for greatness. They are simply another wretched soul trying to survive in a social system designed to keep them at the bottom. Even those who have almost nothing can exploit them, perpetuating a chain of oppression in which the most vulnerable compete for the few resources available.

Everyday survival is extremely difficult. Finding food, securing shelter, avoiding disease, protecting one's few possessions, and finding some form of work are constant challenges. The player faces degrading conditions, exhausting labor, debt, exploitation, and the absence of any institutional protection. Even the most basic needs can become obstacles capable of jeopardizing their survival.

The world is bleak and merciless. Great industrial cities are choked with smoke, soot, and pollution, while overcrowded working-class neighborhoods coexist with luxurious districts and centers of power. Corruption, epidemics, human exploitation, and institutional decay are part of everyday life. Meanwhile, the great powers compete for territory, resources, and influence in an era defined by wars between nations, imperial expansionism, and conflicts capable of destroying entire cities.

The universe also incorporates elements of industrial and supernatural horror. Secret societies, clandestine scientific experiments, occultism, and inexplicable phenomena coexist with the machinery, factories, and technologies of a rapidly transforming civilization. Behind the promise of progress lies a world in which human life is often worth very little.

Prosperity does not come through a quick succession of heroic victories. It is a long and difficult journey built through work, opportunities, risks, alliances, economic activities, and morally ambiguous decisions. The player must acquire resources, develop skills, build relationships, and find ways to gradually escape poverty.

Wars and political transformations directly affect the protagonist's opportunities and challenges. Conflicts can destroy cities, disrupt supply chains, trigger inflation, displace populations, and create new economic opportunities. The world continues to evolve regardless of the player's individual goals.

The objective is to build a prosperous life in every sense: achieve financial stability, secure housing and safety, accumulate wealth, establish meaningful relationships, gain social recognition, and achieve independence. The journey represents a radical transformation: from someone who struggles every day to avoid starving to someone capable of determining their own destiny in a world that never offered them opportunities.

## Objective

Survive poverty and build a prosperous life in a deeply unequal society, gradually achieving stability, wealth, independence, recognition, and influence while facing the consequences of war, industrialization, and social decay.

## Core Mechanics

- Hardcore survival simulation, with management of hunger, thirst, health, fatigue, illness, shelter, and basic needs.
- Scarcity-driven economic system in which even small amounts of money and food represent valuable resources.
- Work and subsistence activities involving exhausting tasks, low wages, and frequently exploitative conditions.
- Social class system featuring different privileges, restrictions, and opportunities depending on the character's position.
- Gradual economic progression, allowing players to accumulate resources, acquire tools, develop commercial activities, and build wealth.
- Skills and professions system, enabling specialization in different activities and income-generating pursuits.
- Social relationships, reputation, alliances, and influence as tools for upward mobility.
- Debt, contracts, exploitation, and economic dependency systems, with long-term consequences.
- Dynamic world affected by wars between nations, imperial rivalries, occupations, epidemics, economic crises, and political transformations.
- Consequences of conflicts on the population, including conscription, bombings, rationing, displacement, and supply disruptions.
- Industrial horror setting featuring supernatural phenomena, secret societies, occultism, and clandestine scientific experiments.
- Moral and strategic decisions that require balancing survival, dignity, ambition, and opportunities.
- Wealth accumulation and improvements to living conditions, including housing, businesses, property, and financial security.
- Social and economic progression, allowing players to rise from outcast to skilled worker, artisan, merchant, property owner, or influential figure.
- Persistent consequences of choices, allowing missed opportunities, failures, and established relationships to shape the character's journey.

## References

- Kingdom Come: Deliverance: survival, realism, and the progression of a protagonist without extraordinary abilities.
- Kenshi: extreme difficulty, initial vulnerability, and progression in a hostile world.
- Mount & Blade: Warband: social advancement, economy, warfare, and the acquisition of influence.
- Frostpunk: extreme survival, industrialization, scarcity, and difficult decisions in a decaying world.
- Dishonored: industrial architecture, social inequality, urban decay, and supernatural elements.
- Darkest Dungeon: oppressive atmosphere, horror, and the consequences of exposure to a hostile world.
- Dark industrial fantasy: a combination of industrialization, imperialism, supernatural horror, and social decay.
