# The Saga of Eldrin: The Cursed Crown

## Concept

A narrative medieval fantasy RPG set in Velmoria, an ancient empire fragmented into seven rival kingdoms following the death of its last emperor. Decades of succession disputes have turned the continent into a land marked by wars, political instability, and noble conspiracies.

The player follows the journey of Eldrin, an orphan who begins his life as a thief on the streets of Ravencourt. His fortunes change when he is taken in by Garrick Thorne, a powerful merchant who teaches him about trade, diplomacy, and the true relationship between wealth and power. Through intelligence and ambition, Eldrin builds his own fortune, acquires a ruined domain, and rises into the aristocracy, becoming an influential figure in Velmoria's reconstruction.

His journey takes him from merchant to baron, count, military commander, and ultimately duke. Alongside Duke Armand Valmont, he participates in the reunification of the former imperial territories and the restoration of the throne. However, his rise provokes the hostility of the traditional aristocracy, which begins to see him as a threat to the established order.

Betrayed by those he helped bring to power, Eldrin loses his titles, properties, and allies, forcing him to flee into exile. During his journey back, he discovers evidence that he is the last descendant of an imperial bloodline whose family was allegedly murdered by conspirators who feared their claims to the throne. Convinced that his ancestry grants him legitimacy, he returns to Velmoria to reclaim his place and confront those who destroyed his life.

The conflict culminates in the death of Emperor Armand I and Eldrin's ascension to the throne. However, gaining power does not bring an end to the conspiracies. Ambition, betrayal, and personal losses turn him into an increasingly paranoid and ruthless ruler. As inconsistencies emerge in the evidence surrounding his ancestry and new political manipulations come to light, Eldrin's certainty about his own legitimacy begins to crumble.

Realizing that the empire itself perpetuates the cycle of wars and betrayals that has defined his existence, he makes a final decision: to dissolve Velmoria and destroy the symbols of its power.

In the ending, Eldrin dies without ever discovering whether he truly was the last legitimate heir to the imperial bloodline or whether his ancestry was merely another carefully constructed lie devised by conspirators. His journey ends with the destruction of the empire he spent his life trying to conquer and rebuild.

The narrative follows the transformation of a young man who wanted to escape poverty into someone who achieves everything he ever thought he wanted, only to be consumed by the consequences of his own rise and the uncertainty surrounding the destiny that led him to the throne.

## Objective

Follow Eldrin's journey from his childhood on the streets to his rise to imperial power, facing personal, economic, political, and military challenges. The player must navigate power struggles, build alliances, make decisions, and face the consequences of their choices throughout a story shaped by ambition, loyalty, betrayal, and uncertainty about one's own destiny.

## Core Mechanics

- Narrative progression following different stages of Eldrin's life and social ascent.
- Dialogues and decisions with consequences for relationships, alliances, and story events.
- Trade, negotiation, and resource management to build wealth and influence.
- Political intrigue, conspiracies, diplomacy, and rivalries between factions and noble families.
- Property and domain development, including city reconstruction and economic growth.
- Combat and military strategy, with participation in battles, sieges, and territorial unification campaigns.
- Development of Eldrin's skills and influence as his journey progresses.
- Relationships with key characters, including Garrick Thorne, Armand Valmont, and Elyana Valmont.
- Long-term narrative consequences reflecting the impact of decisions and relationships built throughout the journey.

## References

- The Witcher: medieval fantasy storytelling, complex characters, and consequential decisions.
- Kingdom Come: Deliverance: medieval setting, protagonist progression, and the development of his social standing.
- Mount & Blade: Warband: political ascent, faction relationships, and military conflicts.
- Crusader Kings: dynastic intrigue, alliances, and struggles for power.
- Dragon Age: Origins: epic storytelling, characters, and choices that affect the fate of different groups.
