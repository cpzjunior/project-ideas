# Apocalyptic Store: Craft, Barter & Survive

## Concept

A simulation, management, and survival game set in a post-apocalyptic world, where the player must run a business amid the collapse of society. Even after the end of the world, economic needs remain: survivors still need to trade, resources remain scarce, and even the bills still need to be paid.

The player takes on the role of a business owner running an establishment that serves as a trading and supply hub for the local population. To keep the business running, they must produce some of their own resources through farming and animal husbandry, acquire goods through bartering, and negotiate with customers whose intentions are not always trustworthy.

The business is also exposed to environmental threats. Hungry monsters may attack the establishment in search of supplies, while sabotage robots attempt to destroy its facilities and disrupt operations. These situations require the player to balance commercial activities, production, defense, and resource management.

The experience combines economic simulation, crafting, building, and survival, turning the management of a small business into a complex survival operation in a hostile world.

## Objective

Keep the business running and expand operations in a post-apocalyptic world by ensuring resource production, building trade relationships with survivors, and protecting the establishment against external threats.

## Main Mechanics

- Management of a commercial establishment in a post-apocalyptic environment.
- Buying, selling, and trading goods through a barter system.
- Economy based on scarce resources and variable product availability.
- Farming and animal husbandry for in-house production of goods.
- Crafting system for manufacturing and processing resources.
- Customers with different needs, personalities, and behaviors.
- Negotiations involving risks, opportunities, and assessment of intentions.
- Defense of the establishment against monsters and hostile creatures.
- Sabotage robots capable of attacking facilities and disrupting operations.
- Management of inventory, production, facilities, and resources.
- Business expansion and upgrades throughout the progression.
