# My Pet Companion: A Gentle Presence For Busy Days

## Concept

A digital companion in a peaceful environment, created for people with exhausting routines who don't have the time or circumstances to care for a real pet.

The player can create their own little pet by choosing its species, breed, appearance, and colors, developing a companion that matches their preferences. The experience allows players to interact freely with their pet, play indoors, offer toys, observe its behaviors, and go for walks around the neighborhood.

The pet adapts to the player's pace, responding naturally to interactions and its surroundings. Every walk or moment at home can lead to small spontaneous events, without demanding constant attention or imposing obligations.

There are no punitive mechanics, needs that must be met at specific times, or negative consequences for staying away from the game. The companion is simply there whenever the player wants to spend a few minutes with them.

The experience focuses on presence, calm, and a light emotional connection, offering a small refuge in the middle of the day. More than a traditional game, it is a space to slow down, observe, and enjoy the company of a virtual pet.

## Objective

Create and share pleasant moments with a virtual companion, enjoying walks, playtime, and everyday interactions at your own pace.

## Core Mechanics

- Pet creation and customization, with choices of species, breed, colors, and visual characteristics.
- Free interactions at home, including playing, petting, and using toys.
- Walks around the neighborhood, exploring streets, parks, and small areas of the environment.
- Spontaneous behaviors and contextual animal reactions, creating natural moments throughout the experience.
- Customization of accessories, toys, and elements of the home environment.
- Peaceful and welcoming environments, with simple activities for enjoying the pet's company.
- No punitive mechanics, daily obligations, or needs requiring constant attention.
- Flexible experience, allowing for either short sessions or longer periods of interaction.

## References

- Nintendogs: creation, customization, and interaction with virtual pets.
- Little Friends: Dogs & Cats: companionship and pet care in a digital environment.
- Animal Crossing: welcoming environments, everyday activities, and a relaxed pace.
