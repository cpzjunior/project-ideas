# The Welfare Project: Love in Action

## Concept

A narrative social simulation and management game set in the city of São Paulo, following the daily routine of a volunteer at a nonprofit organization that provides assistance to people in vulnerable situations.

During the day, the player takes part in activities at the organization's headquarters, helping prepare meals, sort clothes, organize donations, and plan assistance initiatives. With limited resources and constant demand, efforts must be coordinated, supplies organized, and priorities established.

At night, the work continues on the streets. The volunteer takes part in outreach activities, distributing food, clothing, and essential supplies, while also offering personal attention and direct support to those receiving assistance. Each encounter presents individual stories, needs, and circumstances, requiring sensitivity and an understanding that assistance goes beyond providing material resources.

The experience alternates between planning and direct action, highlighting the importance of collaboration among volunteers, donors, and the organization's staff. Throughout the journey, the player follows individual stories and discovers how small actions, when carried out collectively, can help transform lives.

The game seeks to portray the everyday work of a social assistance organization, emphasizing solidarity, human dignity, and the impact of community engagement.

## Objective

Take part in the activities of a nonprofit organization and help expand its reach and capacity to provide assistance, managing limited resources and supporting people in vulnerable situations. The experience aims to inspire engagement in volunteer work and charitable donations.

## Core Mechanics

- Preparation and organization of meals, clothing, donations, and assistance supplies.
- Resource and supply management, taking urgent needs and limited availability into account.
- Planning outreach activities and establishing service priorities.
- Distribution of food, clothing, and essential supplies on the streets of São Paulo.
- Narrative interactions with people receiving assistance, each with their own stories and needs.
- Collaborative work with other volunteers and members of the nonprofit organization.
- Monitoring the results of activities and their cumulative impact on the community.
- Expansion of the organization's assistance capacity through collective work and the engagement of volunteers and donors.

## References

- This War of Mine: scarce resource management and human decisions in situations of vulnerability.
- Life is Strange: character-driven storytelling, personal stories, and the consequences of interactions.
- Assemble with Care: caring for objects and people through small actions and meaningful interactions.
