# Gravity Golf: Event Horizon

## Concept

A futuristic sport in which golf is played among unstable celestial bodies, turning every swing into a direct negotiation with the gravity of the universe.

The player competes in the Event Horizon, the most dangerous and prestigious golf tournament in the universe. The courses are planetary systems filled with gravitational challenges: planets shift their orbits, moons cross trajectories, stars collapse, and black holes create danger zones capable of swallowing any ball that gets too close.

Gravity is not merely an obstacle, but the player's primary tool. Every shot requires evaluating power, direction, and the gravitational influence of celestial bodies. An apparently impossible trajectory can become the perfect shot when the player learns to take advantage of orbits, accelerations, and gravitational deflections.

The courses also transform during matches. Cosmic events alter playing conditions, create new routes, and can make previously safe strategies completely unviable. The player must adapt their plans with every shot, balancing precision, creativity, and a willingness to take risks.

As the competition progresses, the challenges become increasingly extreme, featuring opponents with distinct playstyles and courses that push the limits of physics. They say no one has ever won the Event Horizon. Until now.

## Objective

Win the Event Horizon championship by mastering gravitational physics and overcoming increasingly dangerous courses and opponents to become the greatest Gravity Golf player in the universe.

## Core Mechanics

- Shots with control over power, direction, and trajectory.
- Gravitational physics that allows players to use planets, moons, stars, and black holes to alter the ball's path.
- Courses featuring moving celestial bodies and variable gravitational conditions.
- Dynamic cosmic events that modify the course during matches.
- Trajectory planning and discovery of alternative routes to reach the holes.
- Risk and reward, allowing players to choose between conservative plays and extremely risky shots.
- Progression through championships and courses of increasing difficulty.
- Opponents with different playstyles and strategies.
- Scoring system based on traditional golf rules, adapted to gravitational challenges.
- Sports commentary and comedic situations arising from unpredictable cosmic events.

## References

- Traditional golf.
- Arcade golf games.
- Physics and trajectory games, with an emphasis on gravity and interactions between celestial bodies.
