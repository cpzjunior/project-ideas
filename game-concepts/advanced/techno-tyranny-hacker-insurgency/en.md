# Techno Tyranny: Hacker Insurgency

## Concept

A strategy, espionage, and political intrigue game set in a science fiction universe where a technocracy controls the galaxy through mass surveillance, information manipulation, and absolute dominance over technological infrastructure.

The player takes on the role of an invisible insurgency operative, working in the shadows and infiltrating systems under multiple identities. Rather than commanding armies or directly confronting the regime, their primary weapon is the ability to manipulate networks, institutions, and power structures.

Operations may involve triggering blackouts in strategic regions, forging official orders, altering records, manipulating communications, and spreading distorted information. However, the consequences of these actions are unpredictable. An operation intended to weaken the regime may trigger social instability, empower a rival faction, or harm the very populations the insurgency seeks to liberate.

The information network itself is unreliable. Reports contradict one another, allies conceal their intentions, and rival factions compete over different visions for the galaxy's future. The player must interpret incomplete signals, identify hidden interests, and decide which operations to carry out without fully understanding their consequences.

The insurgency is not necessarily a unified or morally superior force. Its members have their own objectives, and the fall of the technocracy may pave the way for different forms of organization and control. Every decision influences the balance of power and helps determine what will emerge from the system's ruins.

The game explores the relationship between information, power, and instability, placing the player in a position where their influence is immense, but their control over events is limited.

## Objective

Weaken or overthrow the technocracy that governs the galaxy through digital infiltration, espionage, and infrastructure manipulation. At the same time, navigate internal power struggles within the insurgency and the agendas of different factions to influence the future of civilization.

## Core Mechanics

- Infiltration of technological systems, communication networks, and critical infrastructure.
- Use of multiple digital identities to conceal operations and confuse surveillance mechanisms.
- Execution of sabotage operations, including blackouts, forged orders, and record manipulation.
- Dissemination of information and disinformation to influence institutions, populations, and factions.
- Emergent consequence systems in which small interventions can trigger unpredictable chain reactions.
- Network of informants, allies, and contacts with varying levels of reliability.
- Contradictory reports and incomplete information requiring investigation and interpretation.
- Insurgent factions with their own interests, ideologies, and objectives.
- Management of trust, influence, and suspicion within the insurgency itself.
- Adaptive responses from the technocracy through surveillance and counterintelligence systems.
- Strategic decisions that influence the balance of power and the galaxy's possible futures.

## References

- Deus Ex: conspiracies, technological control, and political conflicts.
- Watch Dogs: system hacking and manipulation of urban infrastructure.
- Uplink: hacking operations and digital infiltration.
- Orwell: surveillance, information manipulation, and political consequences.
- Cyberpunk 2077: power struggles, megacorporations, and conflicts in a technologically dominated society.
