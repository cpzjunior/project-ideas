# Gambit of Nations: Master of the Odds

## Concept

A strategy, adventure, and competitive game inspired by No Game No Life. In a world where wars and armed conflicts have been abolished, power struggles are settled through games and wagers. Money, properties, political influence, territories, and even the sovereignty of entire nations can be put at stake.

The player literally starts from nothing: a homeless beggar with no wealth, influence, or social standing, whose only possessions are the coins they manage to collect on the streets. Looking for an opportunity to change their life, they begin participating in small local games and bets, discovering a talent for strategy, bluffing, reading opponents, and manipulating probabilities.

From there, an extraordinary journey of ascension begins. From street gambler to professional player, from challenging rulers to competing against entire nations, the protagonist earns their place in a world where power belongs to those who master the rules of the game.

But their ambitions extend beyond the planet. As their reputation grows, opponents from other civilizations and interplanetary beings emerge, for whom wagering entire worlds is just another form of entertainment. The stakes begin to involve planets, star systems, and ancient technologies, until the protagonist faces the ultimate wager: the very existence of their world.

Matches feature casino games, card games, dice, board games, and original challenges, combining luck, strategy, bluffing, and manipulation. Each opponent has their own personality, specialties, and playstyle, requiring the player to learn new game types, identify patterns, and find ways to exploit their opponents' weaknesses.

Despite the increasingly absurd scale of the wagers, the essence remains the same: a former beggar trying to outplay increasingly powerful opponents, relying solely on their ability to play the game.

## Objective

Rise from an unknown beggar to become the greatest player in the universe, accumulating wealth, influence, and power through increasingly ambitious competitions, until reaching the ultimate wager for the fate of their own world.

## Core Mechanics

- Participate in casino games, card games, dice games, board games, and original challenges.
- Economic progression, starting with coins earned through begging and advancing to vast fortunes and planetary resources.
- Compete against opponents with distinct personalities, specialties, and playstyles.
- Betting system involving money, properties, influence, organizations, territories, and planets.
- Bluffing strategies, opponent reading, manipulation, and probability calculation.
- Form alliances and rivalries with players, political leaders, and extraterrestrial civilizations.
- Face challenges with special rules and varied victory conditions.
- Experience economic, political, and territorial consequences for victories and defeats.
- Reputation progression, granting access to tournaments and opponents of increasingly greater scale.
