# Air Hockey Pro: The Path to Glory

## Concept

An Air Hockey game with narrative progression, in which an amateur player begins his journey at Playcenter tables in a shopping mall and unexpectedly ends up entering the sport's professional circuit.

As his reputation grows, he faces increasingly skilled and legendary rivals in tournaments that evolve from the local underground scene to worldwide competitions.

The journey transforms seemingly simple matches into intense duels of precision, reflexes, game reading, and rivalry. Along the way, the protagonist discovers a competitive universe far greater than he had ever imagined, involving mysterious champions, obscure traditions, and hidden rules.

At the peak of his career, he discovers the existence of a secret tournament that goes beyond the known boundaries of the sport, leading him to face extraordinary opponents, including alien competitors.

## Objective

Start as an amateur player, earn recognition in the competitive scene, overcome the biggest names in Air Hockey, and win the ultimate tournament, even if it means facing opponents from other worlds.

## Main Mechanics

- Air Hockey matches focused on precision, reflexes, and positioning.
- Career progression, starting with amateur competitions and advancing to the world circuit.
- Reputation system that influences career development and access to new tournaments.
- Rivals with different playing styles, skills, and difficulty levels.
- Tournaments with specific rules, conditions, and challenges.
- Narrative progression featuring characters, rivalries, and discoveries.
- Special competitions featuring opponents and rules that go beyond traditional Air Hockey.

## References

- Traditional Air Hockey.
- Sports games featuring career progression.
- Stories of amateur athletes rising to the professional level.
