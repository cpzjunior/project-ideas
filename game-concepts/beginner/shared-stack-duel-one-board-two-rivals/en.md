# Shared Stack Duel: One Board, Two Rivals

## Concept

A competitive puzzle game inspired by classic block-stacking games, in which two players share the same board and take turns placing continuously falling pieces.

With each round, the falling speed of the pieces progressively increases, raising the pressure and reducing the time available for making decisions. In addition to organizing pieces to complete lines and keep the board under control, each player must consider how their placement will affect their opponent's options.

The shared-board mechanic transforms a traditionally single-player experience into a direct competition, where each move can benefit the current player or create difficulties for the next participant. The limited space and increasing speed make each match progressively more chaotic and competitive.

## Objective

Outlast the opponent by preventing a piece from exceeding the top of the board during your turn.

## Main Mechanics

- Single board shared by two players.
- Alternating turns for piece placement.
- Pieces that continuously fall during each turn.
- Progressively increasing falling speed with each round.
- Piece arrangement to complete and clear lines.
- Indirect interference with the opponent through piece placement.
- Defeat condition triggered when a piece exceeds the upper boundary of the board.
- Victory determined by the survival of the last remaining player.

## References

- Tetris.
- Competitive block-based puzzle games.
