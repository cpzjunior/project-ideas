# Parasite Shooter: Microscopic Menace

## Concept

An action and shooter game with a microscopic perspective, in which the player controls a parasite that invades the human body through the circulatory system and must survive the body's natural defenses.

Each level takes place inside a different organ, such as the heart, lungs, and intestines, featuring unique environments, obstacles, and challenges. During exploration, the parasite faces elements of the immune system, such as white blood cells and antibodies, that attempt to eliminate it.

To survive and progress, the player must collect resources scattered throughout the environments, strengthen the parasite, and unlock upgrades that increase its chances of facing increasingly difficult threats.

## Objective

Progress from one organ to another, evolve the parasite, and overcome the body's defenses until it manages to take over the host before being eliminated by the immune system.

## Main Mechanics

- Movement and combat in microscopic environments.
- Levels set in different organs of the human body.
- Enemies representing components of the immune system, such as white blood cells and antibodies.
- Resource collection during exploration.
- Simple parasite evolution and upgrade system.
- Level progression with increasingly challenging threats.
- Battles against more resilient enemies in later stages.

## References

- Action and shooter games with level-based progression.
- Character evolution and upgrade games.
