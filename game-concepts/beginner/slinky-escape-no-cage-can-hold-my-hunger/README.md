# Slinky Escape: No Cage Can Hold My Hunger

## Concept

A casual action and survival game inspired by Snake, in which an extremely greedy snake escapes from its aquarium and begins exploring the house, devouring everything it finds along the way.

As it grows uncontrollably, the snake must escape its angry owner, who tries to block its routes, restrict its movements, and recapture it. Exploration becomes progressively more chaotic as the player consumes objects that can alter the environment or change the snake's own behavior.

The concept combines Snake's classic growth mechanic with chase elements and environmental interactions. The player must balance the desire to consume more and more objects with the need to maintain enough space to move around and escape.

## Objective

Survive for as long as possible, consume objects throughout the house, and avoid being captured by the owner or becoming too large to escape from the environments.

## Main Mechanics

- Continuous movement inspired by Snake.
- Snake growth as it consumes objects.
- Domestic environments with obstacles and varied paths.
- Pursuit by a CPU-controlled owner.
- Route blocking and recapture attempts by the pursuer.
- Consumable objects that alter the environment or the snake's behavior.
- Progressively reduced available space as the snake grows.
- Balance between food collection, exploration, and survival.
- Scoring based on consumption, survival time, and escape performance.

## References

- Snake.
- Chase and survival games set in enclosed environments.
