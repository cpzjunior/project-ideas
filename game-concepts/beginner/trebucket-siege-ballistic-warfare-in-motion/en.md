# Trebucket Siege: Ballistic Warfare in Motion

## Concept

An action and strategy game featuring destructive physics, inspired by Angry Birds and medieval siege warfare, in which the player takes on the role of chief engineer of a king's trebuchet corps, tasked with helping an ambitious ruler conquer enemy fortresses.

Unlike traditional siege games, trebuchets are mobile machines that must be constantly repositioned to find firing angles, avoid attacks, and maintain pressure on enemy structures. The objective is to destroy walls and fortifications while the enemy army actively responds to the assaults.

Battles involve different threats, such as long-range archers, knights who advance to sabotage siege engines, and rival trebuchets that actively participate in combat. The player must balance offense and survival by coordinating movement, positioning, protection, and firing in real time.

Each encounter becomes a dynamic scenario of physical destruction and tactical decisions, where machine positioning and the timing of each shot can determine the outcome of the battle.

## Objective

Destroy enemy fortifications, neutralize their defensive forces, and conquer their strongholds while keeping your own trebuchets operational throughout the sieges.

## Main Mechanics

- Control of mobile trebuchets on battlefields.
- Real-time movement and repositioning of siege engines.
- Firing with ballistic trajectories and projectile physics.
- Physical destruction of walls, towers, and fortified structures.
- Enemies with different roles: archers, knights, and rival trebuchets.
- Enemy attacks capable of damaging or sabotaging siege engines.
- Management of trebuchet positioning and protection.
- Simultaneous combat between siege engines and defensive forces.
- Progression through increasingly difficult battles and fortresses.

## References

- Angry Birds.
- Medieval siege games.
- Physics-based destruction games.
