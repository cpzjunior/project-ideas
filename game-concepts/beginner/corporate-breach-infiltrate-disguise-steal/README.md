# Corporate Breach: Infiltrate, Disguise, Steal

## Concept

An action and stealth game inspired by Elevator Action, in which the player takes on the role of an industrial spy tasked with infiltrating rival companies' skyscrapers to steal confidential corporate technologies and information.

During infiltration, each door represents an opportunity or a risk. The player can enter rooms to obtain employee disguises, spy on enemies, or acquire gadgets through covert operations. These choices provide different advantages and influence the available options for progressing through the building.

Exploration combines movement between floors using elevators with room incursions, creating a balance between speed and preparation. The player must decide when to advance quickly and when to take risks to obtain resources that will make the mission easier.

## Objective

Infiltrate the building, locate and steal corporate data, overcome security systems, and escape with the information before being discovered or captured.

## Main Mechanics

- Horizontal and vertical movement between floors, inspired by Elevator Action.
- Elevators for fast movement throughout the building.
- Accessible rooms offering different opportunities and rewards.
- Employee disguises to reduce suspicion.
- Enemy and security system surveillance.
- Collection and acquisition of gadgets to assist with infiltration.
- Stealth system based on the risk of detection.
- Time management and route selection.
- Data theft and building escape objectives.
