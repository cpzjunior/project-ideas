# To the Moon and Beyond: One Leap For My Cheese

## Concept

A casual vertical platformer inspired by Doodle Jump, in which a clumsy explorer uses a pogo stick on the Moon to recover his lost cheese, which keeps floating higher and higher into space.

During the ascent, the player must jump between meteorites that act as platforms, each with distinct properties and behaviors. Some provide an extra boost, others break upon contact, while some have slippery surfaces, requiring constant adaptation.

The gameplay combines consecutive jumps, mastery of low-gravity physics, and precise movement control. By getting the timing of their jumps right, the player activates a special mechanic called "Super Bounce", capable of propelling them to even greater heights.

## Objective

Climb as high as possible, reaching the cheese before it is lost forever in space. To do so, the player must master low-gravity physics, take advantage of platform properties, and execute increasingly precise jumps.

## Main Mechanics

- Movement and vertical jumps using a pogo stick.
- Low-gravity physics affecting jump height, speed, and trajectory.
- Meteorites acting as platforms.
- Platforms with distinct properties: extra boost, breaking upon contact, and slippery surfaces.
- Timing system to activate "Super Bounce".
- Vertical progression with increasing difficulty.
- Scoring based on maximum height reached.
