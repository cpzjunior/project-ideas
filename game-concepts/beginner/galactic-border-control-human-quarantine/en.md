# Galactic Border Control: Human Quarantine

## Concept

A simulation and management game with satirical humor, set in a future where Earth has been placed under quarantine by the Intergalactic Command. Humanity is considered too unstable to coexist with other civilizations, posing a threat to the security and stability of the galaxy.

The player takes on the role of an officer responsible for enforcing Earth's isolation. Their mission is to prevent alien technologies from reaching Earth, ensuring they are not turned into weapons of mass destruction, and to prevent humanity's violent culture from contaminating the rest of intergalactic society.

Between secret monitoring operations, absurd inspections, incident analysis, and endless reports, the officer must keep the quarantine under control. The job requires attention to detail, compliance with protocols, and the ability to deal with increasingly unlikely situations.

## Objective

Ensure that Earth remains isolated, preventing the smuggling of alien technologies and the spread of behaviors considered dangerous by intergalactic authorities.

## Main Mechanics

- Inspection and screening of suspicious cargo, equipment, and objects.
- Identification and confiscation of alien technologies destined for Earth.
- Secret monitoring of human activities.
- Analysis of behaviors and incidents that may pose risks to the galaxy.
- Completion of reports and compliance with bureaucratic protocols.
- Management of incidents and unexpected situations.
- Administration of inspection resources and operations.
