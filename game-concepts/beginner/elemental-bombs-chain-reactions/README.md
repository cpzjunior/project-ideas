# Elemental Bombs: Chain Reactions

## Concept

An action and puzzle game inspired by Bomberman, in which the player uses elemental bombs in destructible arenas to modify the environment, trigger chain reactions, and overcome challenges.

The bombs have different elemental properties, such as fire, ice, and electricity, each capable of producing specific effects on the environment, obstacles, and enemies. Combining these elements allows players to create unexpected interactions and develop different strategies to solve puzzles and defeat opponents.

Each level functions as a small sandbox, encouraging experimentation and the discovery of creative solutions. The arrangement of elements, the sequence of explosions, and the characteristics of enemies mean that small decisions can lead to major consequences, increasing variety and replayability.

## Objective

Explore the possibilities of elemental bombs to solve each arena's challenges, defeat enemies, and reach the exit or complete the level's objective.

## Main Mechanics

- Bomb placement and detonation in arenas.
- Environments featuring destructible elements.
- Bombs with fire, ice, and electricity elemental properties.
- Specific effects of each element on the environment and enemies.
- Element combinations that produce special interactions.
- Chain reactions between explosions.
- Enemies with different behaviors and elemental vulnerabilities.
- Environmental puzzles that require experimentation and reasoning.
- Compact levels with multiple possible solutions.

## References

- Bomberman.
- Physics-based puzzle games featuring chain reactions.
