# Infinite Backrooms Manager: Entity-Proof Audit

## Concept

A simulation and management game with absurd humor, set in the Backrooms. The player is hired to manage the storeroom of an apparently infinite facility, responsible for storing, organizing, and cataloging objects that mysteriously appear throughout the corridors.

The job involves keeping inventory organized, locating lost goods, and preventing Backrooms entities from stealing or damaging stored products. Between corridors that seem to go on forever, objects that appear out of nowhere, and creatures that disregard the basic rules of a workplace, the biggest challenge is keeping the storeroom running without getting lost in the workplace itself.

## Objective

Manage the Backrooms storeroom by keeping inventory organized, cataloging discovered objects, and protecting goods against entities and other inexplicable events.

## Main Mechanics

- Inventory organization and management across interconnected corridors.
- Cataloging and classification of mysteriously appearing objects.
- Locating and transporting goods between different storeroom areas.
- Inventory control, identification of missing items, and fulfillment of requests.
- Entities that invade the storeroom and attempt to steal, hide, or damage objects.
- Exploration of infinite corridors, with the risk of losing one's bearings.
- Adaptation to unpredictable situations and Backrooms phenomena.

## References

- Backrooms: liminal spaces, endless corridors, and inexplicable entities.
- Inventory simulation and management games.
