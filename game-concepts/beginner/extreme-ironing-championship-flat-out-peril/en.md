# Extreme Ironing Championship: Flat Out Peril

## Concept

A sports and adventure game inspired by Extreme Ironing, a real-life sport that combines extreme activities with the practice of ironing clothes in unusual and dangerous locations.

The player competes in a world championship where they must take their ironing board, iron, and clothes to the most extreme environments possible. Each competition presents a different challenge, involving climbing, water crossings, urban obstacles, forests, and other unlikely situations.

The challenge is not simply to iron clothes while overcoming obstacles, but to engage in a genuine extreme activity and find the right moment and location to set up an ironing station. A climb might end at the top of a mountain, a crossing might require setting up the ironing board on a boat, and a freestyle performance allows players to create completely unconventional routines.

Competitors are judged on the difficulty and creativity of their execution, ironing quality, style, and time, turning a household chore into a sporting competition that is as absurd as it is challenging.

## Objective

Compete in the Extreme Ironing World Championship, overcome the different disciplines, and claim the champion title by demonstrating skill in both extreme activities and the art of ironing clothes.

## Main Mechanics

- Forest Mode: complete challenges in forests, trees, and hard-to-reach terrain.
- Water Mode: transport equipment and iron clothes on boats or during water crossings.
- Rocky Mode: climb walls and rocky surfaces to reach suitable ironing locations.
- Urban Mode: perform routines in urban environments and unconventional structures.
- Freestyle Mode: create custom performances by choosing extreme locations and situations.
- Extreme Activities: perform climbs, jumps, crossings, and other maneuvers required to complete each challenge.
- Station Setup: position the ironing board and prepare the equipment at the chosen location.
- Performance Evaluation: consider ironing quality, creativity, style, difficulty, and time.
- Competitive Progression: advance through the disciplines and face increasingly elaborate challenges.
