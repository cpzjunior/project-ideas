# Paddle Grand Master: The World Circuit

## Concept

A single-player arcade game inspired by the classic Pong, in which the player faces a series of CPU-controlled opponents in a global tournament to claim the title of ultimate champion.

Matches preserve the classic Pong rules but introduce subtle, progressive variations to keep the experience dynamic. These include changes in ball speed, different opponent behavior patterns, and arenas with dynamic effects.

The concept combines Pong's simplicity and straightforward gameplay with a championship structure, offering gradual difficulty progression and increasingly challenging matches.

## Objective

Win every stage of the world circuit, overcome the different opponents, and claim the Grand Master title.

## Main Mechanics

- Pong matches against CPU-controlled opponents.
- Tournament with stage-based progression and increasingly difficult opponents.
- Progressive variations in ball speed.
- Different opponent behavior patterns and difficulty levels.
- Arenas with dynamic effects and characteristics.
- Progression system based on victories throughout the circuit.
