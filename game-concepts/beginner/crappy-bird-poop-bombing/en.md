# Crappy Bird: Poop Bombing

## Concept

A casual action and skill-based game built around short matches, simple controls, and continuous progression.

Inspired by the endless runner game formula, the player controls a pigeon in constant flight through varied environments, having to master movement physics, dodge obstacles, and achieve the highest score possible.

The game combines a traditional aerial survival mechanic with an additional layer of strategy: dropping poop bombs on targets throughout the environment. This second mechanic adds depth to the gameplay, creating a constant decision between maintaining a safe flight path or risking maneuvers to increase the score.

## Objective

Beat your own high score, improve bombing accuracy, climb the global leaderboard, and unlock new pigeon customization options.

## Main Mechanics

- Continuous flight with movement controls.
- Obstacle avoidance.
- Dropping poop bombs on targets.
- Scoring system based on survival and accuracy.
- Progression and customization unlocks.

## References

- Endless runner games.
- Flappy Bird.
