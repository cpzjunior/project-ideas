# Dice Conquest: The High Roller’s War

## Concept

A turn-based strategy and conquest game based on dice rolls, in which territories have military forces represented by sets of dice. The player must expand their domain across the map, facing opposing forces in battles where combat outcomes depend on the number and results of the dice involved.

The map features different terrain types with simple combat modifiers, as well as fortified cities protected by walls that increase their defensive capabilities. These features add a strategic layer to expansion decisions, requiring the player to assess when to advance quickly and when to concentrate forces to conquer more heavily defended positions.

## Objective

Conquer enemy territories, expand your domain across the map, and overcome enemy defenses until the match's victory condition is achieved.

## Main Mechanics

- Turn-based combat system based on dice rolls.
- Territories with military forces represented by quantities of dice.
- Territorial expansion through attacks on adjacent regions.
- Terrain types with modifiers that influence combat outcomes.
- Fortified cities with walls that increase local defense.
- Requirement for a numerical advantage in dice to conquer fortified positions.
- Strategic decisions between rapid expansion and force concentration.
- Victory system based on territorial control.
