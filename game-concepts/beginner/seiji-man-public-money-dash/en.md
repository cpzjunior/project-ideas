# Seiji-Man: Public Money Dash

## Concept

An arcade game inspired by Pac-Man, featuring political satire and corruption as its central themes. The protagonist, Seiji-Man — a Japanese expression meaning "politician" — navigates mazes inspired by public bureaucracy, collecting taxes and briefcases full of money while trying to escape police cars and investigators pursuing him.

The gameplay follows Pac-Man's classic chase-and-collect formula, incorporating humorous elements related to the political world. The player must explore the mazes, accumulate money, and avoid being captured by the authorities.

The gameplay changes when collecting the "Parliamentary Immunity" power-up, which temporarily reverses the roles: Seiji-Man begins chasing the investigators, allowing him to eliminate them from his path and erase evidence of his crimes.

## Objective

Collect as much money as possible, navigate the mazes without getting captured, and strategically use special powers to escape the authorities and maximize the score.

## Main Mechanics

- Maze navigation with a structure inspired by Pac-Man.
- Collection of taxes and briefcases full of money scattered throughout the environment.
- Pursuit by CPU-controlled police cars and investigators.
- "Parliamentary Immunity" power-up, which temporarily reverses the chase.
- Ability to chase investigators while the immunity effect is active.
- Collection and destruction of evidence as an additional scoring or progression element.
- Scoring system based on money collected, evidence erased, and level performance.
