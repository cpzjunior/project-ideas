# Personal Work and Employment Rights Management Platform

## Description

A digital platform designed for employees under formal employment contracts (CLT), independent contractors (PJ), and freelancers to manage and monitor their own professional lives, allowing them to record work schedules, track hours worked, monitor vacation time, manage absences, record activities, and track amounts received or owed.

The solution works as a personal professional management and tracking environment in which workers maintain their own records, independently of the systems used by their employers or contracting companies.

For CLT employees, the platform allows them to compare their personal records of working hours, overtime, additional compensation, vacation time, and remuneration against the information provided by their employer, identifying potential discrepancies between what was actually worked and what was recorded or paid.

For PJ professionals and freelancers, it works as a service management tool, allowing them to track hours dedicated to each client, deliverables, contracts, invoicing, payments, and professional commitments.

The proposal is to provide workers with greater visibility and control over their routines, productivity, remuneration, and professional history.

## How It Works

- Professional Engagement Registration and Management: registration of one or more professional engagements, including CLT employment contracts, PJ contracts, service agreements, and freelance work. Allows users to record employers, contracting parties, clients, positions, roles, remuneration, contractual terms, working schedules, and validity periods.

- Work Schedule and Time Tracking: daily recording of start times, end times, breaks, and other working time entries. Allows users to enter times manually, use timers, or make quick entries through the application. Maintains a daily and monthly history of hours worked, allowing users to monitor their actual working hours.

- Overtime and Compensatory Time Bank Management: automatic identification of hours exceeding the contracted working schedule, monitoring of compensatory time bank balances, tracking of time off in lieu, and recording of hours pending payment. Allows users to configure working time rules and contractual conditions to calculate estimated overtime and additional compensation.

- Vacation and Absence Management: monitoring of vacation accrual periods, scheduled vacations, days taken, and estimated remaining vacation balance. Allows users to record leaves of absence, licenses, days off, holidays, absences, and compensatory arrangements, maintaining a personal calendar of professional availability.

- Professional Activity Log: recording of activities performed throughout the workday, including projects, meetings, tasks, deliverables, travel, and incidents. Allows users to associate activities with clients, contracts, teams, and projects, maintaining a history of completed activities and the time dedicated to each one.

- Timekeeping and Compensation Verification (CLT): comparison between personal working time records and information provided by the employer, such as timekeeping reports, payslips, and payment statements. Allows users to identify discrepancies in working hours, overtime, night shift premiums, breaks, deductions, and other compensation components. The platform can provide estimates of amounts owed based on applicable contractual and labor regulations.

- Contract and Deliverable Management (PJ and Freelancer): storage of contracts, proposals, scopes, deadlines, agreed amounts, and payment terms. Allows users to track completed activities, hours dedicated, deliverables submitted, approvals, and obligations undertaken with each contracting party.

- Hours and Billing Management (PJ and Freelancer): tracking of time dedicated to each client or project, allowing users to calculate billable amounts based on hourly, daily, monthly, or deliverable-based rates. Includes tracking of approved hours, services rendered, invoices issued, amounts billed, and payments received or outstanding.

- Compensation and Income Management: consolidation of amounts received and expected, including salary, additional compensation, overtime, benefits, service payments, bonuses, and other professional income. Allows users to monitor income trends and compare expected, recorded, and actually received amounts.

- Professional Expense Management: recording of expenses related to professional activities, such as transportation, meals, accommodation, tools, equipment, and courses. Allows users to associate expenses with contracts, clients, and projects, facilitating cost control and potential reimbursement claims.

- Professional Calendar and Schedule: organization of working hours, commitments, meetings, deliverables, vacations, days off, contractual deadlines, and scheduled activities. Allows users to visualize availability and time allocation across different professional engagements and projects.

- Professional Documentation and History: organized storage of contracts, payslips, timekeeping reports, payment receipts, invoices, communications, and other documents related to professional life. Maintains a chronological history of records and documents for consultation and comparison.

- Alerts and Notifications: notifications about excessive working hours, accumulated overtime, vacation periods, contractual expiration dates, outstanding payments, approaching deliverable deadlines, and discrepancies identified in work records.

- Personal Reports and Indicators: dashboards displaying hours worked, overtime, compensatory time bank balances, remuneration, income by client, professional expenses, productivity, time allocation, and earnings growth. Allows users to generate monthly and historical reports for personal monitoring.

- Intelligence and Data Analysis: use of artificial intelligence to analyze work schedule records, documents, and financial statements, identify inconsistencies, explain discrepancies, and assist in interpreting contractual and labor-related information. Can generate alerts about patterns of overwork, excessive working hours, and differences between recorded and paid amounts.

## Applications

- CLT Employees: workers who want to monitor their working hours, track overtime, vacations, compensatory time banks, and verify whether the amounts recorded and paid by their employer match their own records.

- PJ Professionals: service providers who need to track contracts, hours worked, deliverables, billing, expenses, and payments from different contracting parties.

- Freelancers: professionals who work on a project, hourly, or deliverable basis and need to track the time dedicated to each client, organize commitments, and monitor income.

- Independent Consultants and Specialists: professionals who provide services simultaneously to different companies and need to manage contracts, activities, availability, and remuneration.

- Professionals with Multiple Engagements: workers who hold different professional positions or engagements and need to consolidate working hours, income, commitments, and availability.

- Hybrid and Remote Workers: professionals who want to maintain independent records of working hours, activities, deliverables, and availability, especially when working outside the contracting company's premises.
