# Crypto Asset Market Manipulation Prevention Act

## 1. Idea

Create legislation to prevent, identify, and punish practices involving the manipulation of cryptocurrency and other crypto asset prices, supply, and demand.

The proposal will pay special attention to schemes involving meme coins, newly created tokens, and low-liquidity assets, in which creators, large holders, or promoters may trigger artificial price increases and sell their positions at a profit at the expense of other investors.

The objective is not to prohibit speculative assets, but to prevent manipulation, fraud, and deliberate information asymmetry from being used to obtain unfair advantages.

The legislation will establish mechanisms for prevention, transparency, accountability of those involved, recovery of investor losses, and financial penalties.

## 2. Rationale

The crypto asset market allows the creation and trading of assets with varying levels of transparency regarding those responsible for them, initial distribution, ownership concentration, and issuance conditions.

The proposal seeks to specifically combat:

- Artificial price appreciation campaigns.
    
- Sales of positions by the creators themselves following aggressive promotion.
    
- Dissemination of false or misleading information.
    
- Price and trading volume manipulation.
    
- Concealment of gains obtained through fraudulent schemes.
    
- Exploitation of investors' lack of information.
    

## 3. Expected Benefits

- Reduction of fraud and manipulation in the crypto asset market.
    
- Greater transparency regarding those responsible for projects.
    
- Investor protection.
    
- Financial accountability for those who obtain gains through manipulation.
    
- Greater confidence in the crypto asset market.
    
- Recovery of losses caused to investors.
    
- Increased federal revenue through punitive fines.
    
- Allocation of collected funds toward repayment of federal public debt.
    

## 4. Potential Impacts

### 4.1. Temporary Lockup of Creators' Tokens

Require tokens reserved for creators, administrators, and promoters to have lockup periods disclosed in advance.

As an initial reference, the following may be established:

- Minimum lockup period of 12 months.
    
- Gradual release of positions over 24 months.
    
- Public disclosure of unlocking conditions.
    

The rules must take the nature of each project into account and prevent unilateral changes intended to circumvent lockup periods.

### 4.2. Liability for Promotional Information

Require clear identification of advertising, sponsorship, and compensation for promoting crypto assets.

Influencers, public figures, and other promoters must disclose when they:

- Receive payment to promote a particular asset.
    
- Hold a financial stake in the project.
    
- Maintain significant positions in the promoted asset.
    
- Receive tokens as compensation or promotional incentives.
    

Liability must apply when there is materially false or misleading disclosure, or conscious participation in manipulation.

### 4.3. Tracking and Recovery of Illicit Gains

Allow the tracking, judicial freezing, and confiscation of gains resulting from proven manipulation.

When identifiable investors have suffered losses, full reimbursement of amounts owed must be provided, with monetary adjustments and applicable interest, subject to the legal criteria for compensation.

Recovery of amounts must prevent duplicate compensation for the same loss.

### 4.4. Risk Classification and Disclosure System

Require trading platforms to provide standardized information about crypto assets, including:

- Token concentration.
    
- Available liquidity.
    
- Project age.
    
- Possibility of additional issuance.
    
- Identification of those responsible, where applicable.
    
- Existence and duration of token lockups.
    
- Possibility of unilateral changes to the smart contract.
    
- Concentration risks and dependence on large holders.
    

The classification must present objective information without representing a guarantee of safety or an investment recommendation.

### 4.5. Reporting Channel and Whistleblower Protection

Create a channel for reporting market manipulation, with the possibility of protecting the whistleblower's identity.

Establish a mechanism for financial rewards proportional to the amounts effectively recovered, subject to legal criteria, proof of the report's contribution, and safeguards against fraudulent reports.

### 4.6. Fine Equivalent to Five Times the Economic Benefit Obtained

Establish a financial penalty equivalent to five times the value of the illicit economic benefit obtained through manipulation.

The penalty must:

- Apply to individuals and legal entities proven to be responsible.
    
- Not depend on the offender's total revenue or assets.
    
- Take into account direct and indirect economic benefits.
    
- Cover benefits obtained through related parties or intermediaries when their participation in the scheme is proven.
    
- Be imposed in addition to reimbursement of amounts owed to victims.
    
- Be punitive in nature, without replacing the obligation to compensate affected investors.
    

Example: if those responsible obtain R$ 10 million in illicit economic benefits:

- R$ 10 million must be allocated to reimbursing affected investors, according to their proven losses.
    
- R$ 50 million will be imposed as a punitive fine payable to the Federal Government.
    

The fine will not be allocated to victims or constitute compensation. Its purpose will be to punish those responsible and generate public revenue.

Mandatory Allocation of Collected Revenue:

- 100% of the amounts collected from these fines will be allocated to the Federal Government.
    
- The funds will be exclusively earmarked for repayment of federal public debt, including amortization and settlement of obligations.
    
- The amounts must be accounted for separately, ensuring transparency regarding the funds collected and effectively applied toward reducing public debt.
    
- The revenue may not be used to finance administrative expenses or ordinary government expenditures.
    

Enforcement must comply with due process, individualization of liability, and the prohibition against duplicate penalties on the same grounds.

## 5. Feasibility and Challenges

- Ensure compatibility with Law No. 14,478/2022 and capital markets legislation.
    
- Avoid duplication with existing crimes and violations.
    
- Define objective criteria for identifying manipulation.
    
- Establish oversight mechanisms for decentralized and international transactions.
    
- Define technical criteria for lockup periods.
    
- Identify economic benefits obtained directly and indirectly.
    
- Preserve freedom of expression and the legitimate dissemination of opinions.
    
- Establish cooperation between regulatory authorities and platforms.
    
- Create mechanisms for tracking and recovering investor losses.
    
- Ensure that all fines collected are allocated to repayment of federal public debt.
    
- Create transparency and oversight mechanisms for the collection and allocation of funds.
    

## 6. Open Issues

- Define which projects will be subject to mandatory token lockups.
    
- Establish criteria for identifying creators, administrators, and promoters.
    
- Define concentration thresholds that trigger alerts on platforms.
    
- Establish risk classification criteria.
    
- Define requirements for granting rewards to whistleblowers.
    
- Define the methodology for calculating illicit economic benefits.
    
- Assess the application of the fivefold fine alongside existing criminal and administrative penalties.
    
- Define reimbursement mechanisms when multiple investors have suffered losses.
    
- Establish procedures for accounting for and allocating all fines to federal public debt.
    
- Analyze legislative proposals currently under consideration to avoid duplication.
