# Tax Incentive Act for Responsible Payers

## 1. Idea

Create a national tax incentive program that represents an evolution and expansion of Receita Sintonia, established by Complementary Law No. 225/2026.

The proposal will build upon the Receita Sintonia model, which classifies taxpayers according to their level of compliance and offers benefits to those with the highest ratings, expanding this concept to recognize the responsible financial behavior of individuals and legal entities.

While Receita Sintonia focuses on the tax and customs compliance of legal entities, the new program will have a broader scope: it will also cover individuals and consider the regularity of financial obligations of different types.

The objective is to create an incentive system in which citizens and companies that keep their financial commitments up to date receive tangible tax benefits.

Eligibility will be based on the absence of overdue and unpaid debts and the maintenance of a consistent payment history over a minimum period of 12 months.

The existence of loans, financing arrangements, installment plans, or other financial obligations must not prevent participation, provided they are being paid regularly. The objective is to encourage timely payments, not to require individuals or companies to be debt-free.

The benefit will be granted annually through a proportional reduction in taxes owed by the beneficiary. The percentage and applicable taxes must be defined in the law's implementing regulations.

## 2. Rationale

Receita Sintonia represents progress by establishing benefits for taxpayers who demonstrate consistency in fulfilling their tax obligations.

However, its scope is limited to tax and customs compliance, without covering individuals or the regularity of private financial commitments.

The proposal aims to expand this public policy by extending the principle of recognizing responsible payers to society as a whole.

Payment delinquency affects both Brazilian companies and households, compromising access to credit, consumption, investments, and financial stability.

Public policies often concentrate their efforts on debt renegotiation and the recovery of overdue credit. It is also necessary to create incentives for those who keep their financial commitments up to date.

The program seeks to establish a tangible economic advantage for those who regularly fulfill their obligations, encouraging financial responsibility and preventing payment delinquency.

## 3. Expected Benefits

- Expand the scope of Receita Sintonia to include individuals and legal entities.
    
- Encourage consistent and timely payments.
    
- Reduce payment delinquency rates.
    
- Promote a culture of financial planning and responsibility.
    
- Economically recognize citizens and companies that fulfill their obligations.
    
- Reduce credit risks and costs associated with debt collection and recovery.
    
- Encourage the voluntary settlement of outstanding financial obligations.
    
- Make the policy of incentivizing responsible payers broader and more accessible to the population.
    

## 4. Potential Impacts

Positive Impacts:

- Greater incentives for financial regularity among households and businesses.
    
- Potential reduction in delinquency-related costs for the financial system and suppliers.
    
- Encouragement of the formalization of financial commitments.
    
- Greater predictability for creditors and economic agents.
    
- Expansion of tax incentives beyond the corporate and tax compliance landscape.
    

Fiscal and Operational Impacts:

- Reduction in tax revenue corresponding to the benefits granted.
    
- Need to establish objective eligibility criteria.
    
- Integration of information from public agencies and credit protection databases, in compliance with data protection legislation.
    
- Need to prevent improper benefit allocation due to outdated or incorrect records.
    
- Need to ensure compatibility with existing benefits under Receita Sintonia, avoiding duplication or conflicting rules.
    

## 5. Feasibility and Challenges

Implementation must consider the following criteria:

- Scope: individuals and legal entities, including individual microentrepreneurs.
    
- Compliance Period: a minimum of 12 consecutive months without payment delinquency.
    
- Debts Considered: overdue and unpaid financial obligations, regardless of their nature.
    
- Regular Financing Arrangements: loans, financing arrangements, and installment plans that are up to date must not be considered disqualifying factors.
    
- Disputes: debts subject to administrative or judicial disputes, with enforceability suspended, must not prevent participation.
    
- Incorrect Records: outstanding amounts that have already been paid or were incorrectly recorded must be disregarded, provided this can be demonstrated.
    
- Benefit: annual tax reduction, conditional on maintaining the eligibility criteria.
    
- Oversight: cross-referencing of information and the possibility of reviewing the benefit in cases of fraud or false declarations.
    
- Integration with Receita Sintonia: leveraging the existing classification, periodic assessment, and eligibility review structure, while expanding the criteria and target audiences.
    

The implementing regulations must define the taxes covered, the reduction percentage, maximum benefit limits, and mechanisms for verifying financial regularity.

## 6. Open Issues

- What will the tax reduction percentage be?
    
- Will there be an annual financial limit per individual or company?
    
- Which taxes may be reduced for individuals and legal entities?
    
- Will the reduction increase progressively according to the length of time the beneficiary maintains regular payments, or will there be a fixed percentage?
    
- Will a separate classification system be created for individuals, similar to the classification used by Receita Sintonia?
    
- How will debts across different financial institutions and databases be verified?
    
- How should time-barred debts, improper protests, and records that have not yet been updated be handled?
    
- How can the benefit be prevented from being used by individuals or companies that conceal debts through third parties?
    
- How can the new program be integrated with Receita Sintonia without duplicating benefits already granted?
