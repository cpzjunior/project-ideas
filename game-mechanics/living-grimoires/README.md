# Living Grimoires

## Description

A system of magical artifacts that automatically record and accumulate the knowledge, experiences, and discoveries of their bearers over time. Unlike conventional books, grimoires follow the development of their owners, documenting learned spells, experiments performed, personal observations, mistakes, and magical adaptations.

Each grimoire develops its own identity, resulting from the combination of knowledge and experiences accumulated by its successive bearers. Some may exhibit behaviors and personality traits, reproducing characteristics of their former masters and acquiring traits similar to those of a magical familiar.

In this way, a grimoire represents not merely a learning tool, but also a historical and intellectual heritage, capable of preserving centuries of magical knowledge.

## Functioning

Grimoires automatically record new information as their bearers interact with the magic system. Discovered spells, techniques used, experimental combinations, and modifications made to incantations can be incorporated into their pages.

These records may contain information beyond traditional spell descriptions, including personal observations, alternative execution methods, identified limitations, and experimental results. Thus, two grimoires containing the same spell may present different knowledge and instructions, depending on the journeys of their bearers.

Over successive generations, accumulated records allow the grimoire to develop its own characteristics. Its personality may influence how it presents information, reacts to certain situations, advises its bearer, or expresses opinions about magical decisions.

Transferring a grimoire to another character allows them to inherit all previously accumulated knowledge, including rare spells, experimental discoveries, and techniques that may no longer be known to contemporary society. However, some knowledge may require compatibility, skills, or specific conditions to be used.

Ancient grimoires may also resist being transferred to a new bearer, requiring some form of recognition, bond, or acceptance ritual.

The destruction of a grimoire represents the permanent loss of its contents, potentially eliminating unique knowledge that exists in no other records. This turns certain volumes into extremely valuable artifacts and creates motivations for their preservation, protection, recovery, and transmission across generations.

## Applications

Can be used in fantasy RPGs, exploration games, narrative adventures, magic simulators, and experiences featuring complex progression and discovery systems.

It is particularly suitable for games in which magical learning plays a central role, allowing character development to become a process of accumulating knowledge.

It can also be used as a narrative and worldbuilding element, introducing historical grimoires, mage lineages, lost knowledge, intelligent artifacts, and disputes over the possession of rare magical records.
