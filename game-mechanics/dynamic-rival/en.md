# Dynamic Rival

## Description

A persistent rivalry system inspired by the relationship between the protagonist and their rival in Pokémon Red & Blue. The player has a recurring adversary who lives in the same world, develops their own abilities, and competes for the same opportunities, achievements, and objectives.

Unlike traditional rivals, who appear only at specific points in the story, this character has independent progression and follows the events unfolding in the world. If the player ignores threats, quests, or important events, the rival may act first, resolving problems, defeating enemies, exploring regions, and claiming rewards.

Their actions produce tangible consequences: they can accumulate wealth, earn titles, establish businesses, form alliances, and gain public recognition. Depending on the circumstances, they may become more famous, influential, and admired than the protagonist themselves.

The rivalry is not limited to combat. Both compete across different dimensions of the world, including economics, politics, exploration, discoveries, and social influence. The rival also reacts to the player's achievements, compares results, provokes them, questions their decisions, and uses their own accomplishments to fuel the competition.

The goal is to create the feeling that the protagonist is not the only important character in that universe. There is someone following their own path, capable of achieving the same destiny or even surpassing the player.

## Functioning

- Creation of a persistent rival with an independent personality, objectives, and progression.
- Rival participation in the same events, quests, and opportunities available to the player.
- Autonomous action system, allowing the rival to resolve problems and complete activities without the protagonist's involvement.
- Competition for rewards, resources, territories, titles, and recognition.
- Independent progression of skills, equipment, wealth, and influence.
- Rival reactions to the player's achievements, failures, and decisions.
- Public reputation system that compares the accomplishments of both characters.
- Possibility of the rival achieving important objectives before the player.
- Provocative dialogue and interactions that reflect the history of the rivalry.
- Dynamic evolution of the relationship, which may involve friendly competition, resentment, respect, or hostility.
- Possibility of the rival becoming more influential and admired than the protagonist.

## Applications

- Open-world RPGs.
- Adventure and exploration games.
- RPGs featuring character progression and reputation.
- Economic and political simulation games.
- Games with persistent worlds and dynamic events.
- Games in which achievements and public recognition are part of progression.

## References

- Pokémon Red & Blue: persistent rival who follows the protagonist's progression and competes for recognition as a Pokémon Trainer.
- The Elder Scrolls: Oblivion: a world with characters and events that evolve independently of the player.
- Mount & Blade: Warband: character progression, struggles for influence, and dynamic events in the world.
- Nemesis System (Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor): persistent adversaries who evolve and react to interactions with the player.
