# Bag of Holding

## Description

A magical storage system inspired by items such as the Bag of Holding from Dungeons & Dragons. The player possesses a bag capable of accessing a small portal connected to a secure storage chest, allowing them to store a very large number of items without having to physically carry them.

The mechanic eliminates the need to constantly manage weight and inventory space, but establishes an important distinction between storage and quick access. Only items directly equipped on the character, such as weapons in scabbards, tools on belts, and objects in pouches, can be used immediately during action.

To access other items, the player must open the bag's catalog and select the desired equipment. This operation takes place in real time, without pausing the game, leaving the character vulnerable while browsing and organizing their storage.

In this way, carrying capacity is no longer the primary concern. The challenge shifts to properly preparing equipment and deciding which objects should be available for immediate use.

## Functioning

- Magical bag connected through a small portal to a secure storage chest.
- Extremely high storage capacity, without conventional weight or volume management.
- Item storage and retrieval through an organized catalog.
- Real-time access to the catalog, without pausing gameplay.
- Only items equipped on the character's body can be used immediately.
- Equipment organized into specific slots, such as belts, pouches, scabbards, and harnesses.
- Ability to reorganize equipment and transfer items between the bag and equipped slots.
- Character vulnerability while browsing the catalog, creating a situational cost for accessing storage.

## Applications

- Action-adventure RPGs.
- Exploration and survival games.
- Fantasy RPGs featuring equipment and collectible items.
- Games with a wide variety of tools, weapons, and consumables.
- Games that aim to reduce inventory management without eliminating preparation and equipment decisions.

## References

\- Dungeons & Dragons: Bag of Holding, inspiration for the concept of magical storage with a capacity far greater than the container's external dimensions.
