# Emotional Bonds

## Description

A relationship system that establishes deep bonds between the protagonist and important people in their life, such as family members and friends. These bonds directly influence their decisions, behavior, and development throughout the story.

The narrative also explores the protagonist's personal history. As the adventure progresses, events, dialogues, and choices may unlock memories from childhood and adolescence, revealing experiences that helped shape their personality, values, and worldview.

## Functioning

Relationships evolve dynamically according to the player's actions and decisions. Interactions, narrative choices, and events can strengthen or weaken bonds, changing how these people relate to the protagonist.

People close to the protagonist may offer help, advice, resources, and support during important moments. On the other hand, conflicts, disagreements, and decisions that affect these characters may lead to estrangement, confrontations, and new challenges.

Memories of the past are gradually revealed as certain narrative conditions are met. They may emerge through conversations, significant locations, objects, events, or situations that evoke the protagonist's personal history.

These memories not only complement the narrative but also help contextualize the protagonist's present reactions, motivations, and choices.

## Applications

Can be used in narrative RPGs, character-driven adventures, open-world games, and experiences with a strong emphasis on narrative development and interpersonal relationships.

It is particularly suitable for stories in which the protagonist's personal motivations and connections with other characters are as important as the adventure's central objectives.
