# Stamina Potions

## Description

A fatigue management system based on the use of potions that allow characters to continue performing physical activities even after reaching or exceeding their natural endurance limits.

Unlike actual energy recovery, these potions merely temporarily mask the signs of fatigue, allowing the body to continue functioning beyond its usual capacity.

When the effect wears off, the character experiences a rebound effect, resulting in increased exhaustion, reduced stamina regeneration, and decreased physical performance. Frequent use can worsen these consequences, making recovery progressively more difficult.

## Functioning

Stamina potions temporarily grant additional endurance or prevent the effects of fatigue from impairing the character's performance. While the effect lasts, the player can continue running, fighting, climbing, or performing other physical activities even when their natural stamina reserves are depleted.

However, consuming the potion does not eliminate the strain accumulated by the body. When the effect ends, a temporary penalty is applied, potentially including increased fatigue, reduced stamina regeneration speed, decreased physical endurance, and restrictions on certain actions.

Repeated use can intensify these penalties, creating an accumulation of physical strain that requires longer rest periods for full recovery. Depending on the frequency of use, the character may enter a state of exhaustion, significantly compromising their combat and exploration capabilities.

In this way, the player must evaluate when to use potions, considering the urgency of the situation and the consequences that may affect subsequent stages of the adventure. Their use can provide a decisive advantage in critical situations, but may also create vulnerability afterward.

## Applications

Can be used in action RPGs, survival games, open-world adventures, and experiences featuring combat and resource management systems.

It is particularly suitable for games in which physical endurance, exploration, and preparation play an important strategic role, encouraging players to balance exertion, recovery, and resource consumption.

It can also contribute to more complex survival systems, in which immediate decisions produce consequences that affect character performance in the medium and long term.
