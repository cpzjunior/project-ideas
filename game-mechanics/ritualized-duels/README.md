# Ritualized Duels

## Description

A combat system based on formalized confrontations between mages, in which participants establish in advance a set of rules and conditions that must be respected throughout the contest.

Before combat begins, those involved perform a ritual that establishes a binding magical contract. A summoning circle or another supernatural mechanism ensures compliance with the agreed-upon conditions, automatically identifying and punishing any attempt to violate them.

The rules may determine which schools of magic are permitted, which weapons can be used, which behaviors are prohibited, and which conditions must be met to declare a winner.

In this way, duels cease to represent mere contests of power and instead involve strategy, magical knowledge, adaptability, and mastery of one's own limitations.

## Functioning

Before the confrontation, participants define the conditions that will govern the duel. These conditions are formalized through a ritual that creates a magical bond between those involved and establishes the restrictions they must respect.

The rules may be chosen by the participants themselves or determined by the legal and traditional conventions of a particular region. Possible conditions include prohibiting specific schools of magic, restricting weapon use, limiting the number of spells, forbidding lies, preventing external interference, and establishing specific victory conditions.

Once the ritual is complete, the magical contract automatically monitors the participants' behavior. Any violation may trigger predetermined penalties, such as ability suppression, interruption of spellcasting, mana loss, incapacitation, or automatic defeat.

The severity of the punishment may vary according to the seriousness of the offense and the terms of the agreement. In certain situations, even an attempt to circumvent the rules may be considered a violation.

The system allows players to adapt combat conditions according to their strategies. A mage specialized in a particular school may propose banning it to neutralize their opponent's main advantage, while another may establish restrictions that favor their own combat style.

In addition to individual confrontations, ritualized duels can serve as official mechanisms for resolving disputes between magical organizations, families, and communities. In such circumstances, rituals may follow traditional codes, include witnesses, and produce legally recognized outcomes.

In this way, preparing for a duel becomes just as important as the combat itself, since the choice of rules can determine in advance which abilities and strategies will be available to each participant.

## Applications

Can be used in fantasy RPGs, tactical combat games, narrative adventures, and experiences featuring magic systems based on complex rules.

It is particularly suitable for games in which knowledge, specialization, and preparation are as important as raw power, allowing confrontations with varied conditions and different strategic possibilities.

It can also be used as a worldbuilding element, establishing legal codes, traditions, and institutions responsible for regulating magical conflicts. This makes it possible to explore political disputes, trials, competitions between academies, and formal mechanisms for resolving conflicts between practitioners of magic.
