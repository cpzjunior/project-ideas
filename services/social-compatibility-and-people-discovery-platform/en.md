

# Social Compatibility and People Discovery Platform

## Description

A dating and social networking application that allows users to find people, make friends, and discover social groups through a system of customizable and shareable compatibility filters.

The concept combines features of dating apps, social networks, and collaborative content platforms, introducing a model in which users themselves can create, publish, share, and follow filter configurations developed by other people.

The main differentiator is transforming compatibility criteria into a social element. Instead of each user configuring their filters individually and relying exclusively on the platform's algorithm, users can discover how other people define their preferences, follow their setups, and use them as a reference for finding new connections.

Each setup represents a discovery configuration composed of filters, compatibility criteria, weights, and additional modules (Mods). Users can create their own configurations, follow setups created by other users, copy existing configurations, and customize them according to their preferences.

The platform aims to provide greater control over the people discovery process while also creating a community around the sharing of preferences, interests, and compatibility criteria.

## How It Works

When creating an account, users provide their personal characteristics, interests, preferences, and interaction goals.

From there, they can configure their own compatibility filters or explore configurations created by other users.

The platform supports different types of connections:

- Dating: finding people with relationship potential.
- Friendships: meeting people with similar interests, hobbies, and lifestyles.
- Social groups: finding communities and people for activities, events, sports, travel, and other shared interests.
- Networking: establishing professional, academic, and entrepreneurial connections.

The platform may offer features such as:

- Customizable filters: creation of compatibility criteria involving age, location, interests, personality, lifestyle, education, profession, habits, and goals.
- Compatibility weights: ability to assign different levels of importance to criteria, defining how each characteristic influences search results.
- Exclusionary and ranking filters: definition of mandatory criteria for candidate selection and criteria that only influence the compatibility score.
- Mods (additional modules): extensions that introduce new evaluation criteria, such as musical, sports, cultural, intellectual, gastronomic, or travel-style compatibility.
- Setup creation and publishing: ability to develop custom configurations, assign names, descriptions, and categories, and make them available to other users.
- Following setups: users can follow configurations created by other people and receive updates whenever their creators make changes.
- Following creators: ability to follow users who develop interesting configurations and discover other setups published by them.
- Setup discovery: catalog of configurations shared by the community, with search capabilities, categories, popularity rankings, and recommendations.
- Importing and customization: ability to copy another user's setup and adapt it by modifying weights, criteria, and Mods without changing the original configuration.
- Configuration remixing: creation of new versions based on existing setups while preserving attribution to the original creator.
- Social interaction: likes, comments, ratings, and configuration sharing.
- Intelligent recommendations: suggestions of setups based on users' interests, preferences, and previously used configurations.
- Transparent compatibility: presentation of the criteria that contributed to the compatibility score between people, making it possible to understand why certain connections were recommended.

An example would be a user who creates a setup called "People to Travel the World With," prioritizing interests in travel, availability, travel style, languages, and cultural affinity.

Other users can discover this configuration, start following it, or copy it for use in their own searches.

A third user can remix this configuration by increasing the weight assigned to international travel and adding a specific Mod for backpackers.

In this way, setups become social content that can be discovered, followed, shared, rated, and collectively evolved.

It is important to note that following a setup does not necessarily mean automatically adopting all its changes. Users retain control over their own configurations and decide when to incorporate updates or versions released by the creator.

## Applications

The solution serves people interested in expanding their social circles, finding relationships, meeting people with similar interests, and joining communities.

It can be used in different contexts:

- Dating and relationships: discovering people with greater compatibility in terms of values, interests, personality, and lifestyle.
- Friendships: identifying people with shared hobbies, interests, and activities.
- Sports activities: finding partners for running, gym workouts, cycling, tennis, soccer, and other sports.
- Travel: connecting people with similar travel styles and destination preferences.
- Communities and social groups: discovering groups interested in gastronomy, music, technology, culture, gaming, and other topics.
- Professional networking: finding people with shared professional, academic, and entrepreneurial interests.
- Events: discovering people who want to attend the same concerts, festivals, meetups, and in-person activities.

The platform also presents monetization opportunities:

- Freemium: free access to basic features, with limitations on the number of setups and customization capabilities.
- Premium subscription: access to advanced filters, greater customization capabilities, compatibility analytics, and additional features.
- Mods marketplace: sale of specialized modules developed by third parties, with revenue-sharing opportunities.
- Premium communities: creation of exclusive groups with specific membership criteria.
- Commercial partnerships: brands can develop Mods related to specific lifestyles, interests, and activities.
- B2B licensing: making the technology available to clubs, communities, event organizers, and networking platforms.
