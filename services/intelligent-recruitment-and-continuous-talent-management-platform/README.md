# Intelligent Recruitment and Continuous Talent Management Platform

## Description

A digital recruitment and selection platform that uses artificial intelligence, automation, and data analytics to transform traditional hiring processes into a continuous operation for identifying, assessing, and hiring talent.

The goal is to significantly reduce recruitment time and costs through intelligent job structuring, automated resume collection, reusable technical and behavioral assessments, candidate ranking based on alignment with the desired profile, and the creation of a permanent talent pool.

Unlike traditional recruitment systems, in which each job opening typically initiates a new cycle of advertising, resume collection, and assessment, the platform maintains a structured database of previously registered and assessed professionals. As a result, when a new opportunity becomes available, the platform can immediately identify potentially suitable candidates, including those who have participated in previous recruitment processes.

One of the solution's main differentiators is the use of artificial intelligence to support the definition of job openings themselves. Hiring managers often struggle to translate their needs into objective requirements, resulting in excessively lengthy, contradictory, or incompatible job descriptions in relation to the expected seniority and offered conditions. AI would act as a recruitment consultant, helping identify the competencies that are genuinely required, establish priorities, and build a consistent technical and behavioral profile.

The platform also aims to improve the candidate experience by eliminating lengthy forms, avoiding unnecessary repetition of assessments, and proactively notifying professionals about opportunities that match their profiles.

The objective is to transform recruitment into a faster, standardized, transparent, scalable, and data-driven process, allowing HR professionals and hiring managers to focus their efforts on interviews, qualitative assessments, and final hiring decisions.

## Operation

The hiring manager accesses the platform and initiates the creation of a new job opening, providing information about the hiring needs, the number of available positions, and the general characteristics of the role.

Based on this information, artificial intelligence conducts a structured requirements-gathering process, asking the hiring manager about responsibilities, technical competencies, required experience, seniority, behavioral characteristics, and working conditions.

AI can also identify excessive requirements, inconsistencies between expectations and compensation, redundant competencies, and characteristics that do not effectively contribute to job performance.

The result is divided into two documents:

- Public job description: information about responsibilities, activities, requirements, necessary knowledge, compensation, benefits, work model, location, and other relevant conditions that allow candidates to make informed decisions.
- Internal assessment profile: a matrix of technical and behavioral competencies, disqualifying requirements, assessment weights, and alignment criteria used by the algorithm to rank candidates.

The internal profile does not need to disclose its weights and ranking criteria to candidates, but the criteria relevant to their assessment must be transparent and related to the activities of the role.

After approval by the hiring manager and HR, the job opening is published and begins using the platform's features.

The platform can offer features such as:

* Intelligent job definition: use of AI to conduct structured interviews with hiring managers, identify hiring needs, challenge excessive requirements, and transform subjective expectations into measurable competencies.
* Requirements validation: analysis of consistency between seniority, experience, required knowledge, compensation, responsibilities, and working conditions, identifying potential issues before publication.
* Public description and internal profile: generation of two distinct artifacts, one intended to advertise the opportunity and the other used as an assessment and candidate-ranking matrix.
* Simplified candidate registration: ability to upload a resume in PDF or DOCX format or import information from a LinkedIn professional profile, when authorized and technically available, avoiding lengthy forms.
* Intelligent resume extraction: use of AI and document processing to identify academic background, professional experience, technical knowledge, certifications, languages, seniority, and competencies.
* Structured talent pool: centralized storage of candidate profiles, enabling searches by competencies, experience, qualifications, location, and participation history in recruitment processes.
* Reusable behavioral assessments: administration of tests to identify relevant behavioral characteristics, recording results and allowing them to be reused in future opportunities, subject to validity and update criteria.
* Reusable technical assessments: storage of results from knowledge, programming, language, reasoning, and other competency tests, preventing candidates from having to repeatedly complete equivalent assessments.
* Assessment validity management: management of assessment dates, test versions, and validity periods, making it possible to determine when a result can be reused or when a new assessment is required.
* Internal behavioral profile: use of assessment results to build a structured representation of the candidate's behavioral characteristics, without necessarily disclosing this detailed information in the public job posting.
* Intelligent matching engine: comparison between the internal job profile and the available information about each candidate, considering technical competencies, experience, education, assessments, and behavioral characteristics.
* Matching score: calculation of a percentage score representing the compatibility between the candidate and the criteria established for the job opening, using configurable weights and separately handling disqualifying requirements.
* Automatic candidate ranking: organization of eligible candidates in descending order of alignment, allowing the most suitable professionals to be identified quickly.
* Automatic interview shortlist generation: initial forwarding of three candidates per job opening to the second stage of the recruitment process, considering their ranking and confirmation of interest and availability.
* Backup candidate list: maintenance of the remaining ranked candidates on a waiting list, allowing the next candidates to be invited in case of withdrawal, rejection, or replacement needs.
* Interview management: provision of structured resumes, assessment results, matching scores, and relevant information to help HR and hiring managers conduct interviews.
* AI-assisted interviews: ability to use conversational agents to conduct structured interviews, record responses, generate transcripts, and organize evidence related to the job criteria.
* Candidate reports: generation of individual reports containing identified competencies, strengths, gaps, assessment results, and recommended questions for further exploration during interviews.
* Human hiring decisions: presentation of results and recommendations to HR and hiring managers, while keeping the final approval and hiring decision under the responsibility of the professionals involved.
* Proactive opportunity notifications: whenever a new job opening is published, the platform automatically searches the talent pool and notifies the candidates with the highest alignment scores, allowing them to express interest in participating.
* Candidate interest management: ability to accept or decline invitations to opportunities, update availability, and report relevant changes to their professional profiles.
* Automatic application intake closure: ability to stop accepting new candidates when the number of interested candidates with an alignment score equal to or greater than 90% exceeds twenty times the number of available positions.
* Application intake reopening: ability to resume advertising when the number of qualified and available candidates falls below the established parameters.
* Recruitment process validity: maintenance of the validity of each recruitment process for six months, counted from its opening date. After this period, the process is closed, and a new selection process must be initiated if the opportunity remains available.
* Talent pool reuse: ability to use candidates from previous recruitment processes for new opportunities, even when the original process has already been closed, provided that the data and assessments remain valid and appropriate authorization exists for processing the information.
* Rule configuration: ability to customize the number of candidates forwarded to interviews, minimum matching scores, number of qualified candidates per position, process validity periods, competency weights, and other parameters.
* Criteria versioning: recording of changes made to job profiles, weights, and ranking criteria, preserving traceability and preventing improper changes during ongoing recruitment processes.
* Performance dashboard: monitoring of time-to-hire, number of registered candidates, percentage of reused assessments, conversion rates between stages, cost per hire, average matching score, and process efficiency.
* Governance and privacy: management of consent, transparency regarding AI usage, access control, data retention and disposal, human review mechanisms, and auditing of the criteria used.

### Usage Example

A company needs to hire five professionals for a specific technology department.

The hiring manager informs the platform of the company's needs and participates in an interview conducted by AI. During this interaction, the system identifies that the hiring manager initially requested knowledge of twelve different technologies, but only five are truly essential for performing the role.

AI challenges the need for the remaining requirements, identifies complementary competencies, and proposes an assessment matrix with specific weights.

After validation by HR and the hiring manager, the opportunity is published with an objective description containing responsibilities, requirements, compensation, benefits, and work model.

The platform searches its talent pool and identifies professionals who have already participated in other recruitment processes, as well as newly registered candidates.

A professional who recently completed a SQL technical assessment does not need to repeat the test, provided that the result is still valid and corresponds to the required level of knowledge.

Another candidate has compatible experience but has not yet completed a specific assessment for a particular technology. In this case, the platform requests only the necessary additional assessment.

The matching engine calculates candidate alignment based on the weights defined for the job opening and the disqualifying requirements.

For the five positions, the platform initially forwards the fifteen highest-ranked candidates to the second stage, provided they meet the eligibility criteria and confirm their interest.

The remaining candidates stay ranked as backups and may be invited as needed.

During the application intake period, the system identifies that there are already more than one hundred interested candidates with an alignment score equal to or greater than 90%, exceeding the target of twenty qualified candidates per position. Since this parameter was configured by the company, the platform automatically stops accepting new candidates.

After the interviews conducted by HR and the hiring managers, the five professionals are selected.

The remaining candidates continue to be available in the talent pool, subject to the six-month validity period of the recruitment process and the conditions governing the processing of their data.

Some time later, the company opens a new job with similar characteristics. Before even starting a new advertising campaign, the platform identifies compatible professionals in the talent pool and sends personalized notifications, allowing the recruitment process to begin with candidates whose information and assessments are already available.

## Applications

The solution serves companies of different sizes and industries that conduct recurring recruitment and selection processes.

It can be used in different contexts:

* Large enterprises: to centralize recruitment processes, standardize assessment criteria, and maintain structured talent pools for different departments.
* Financial institutions: for recruiting professionals in technology, investments, operations, risk, compliance, customer service, and other specialized areas.
* Technology companies: to accelerate the hiring of developers, solution architects, data engineers, infrastructure professionals, security professionals, and specialists in different technologies.
* Recruitment consultancies: to manage multiple job openings, reuse assessments, and increase recruiter productivity.
* High-volume hiring companies: to automate repetitive stages and organize large volumes of applications.
* Startups and small businesses: to structure recruitment processes with less need for dedicated HR teams.
* Corporate groups: to share talent pools across companies within the same group, respecting data processing permissions and purposes.
* Universities and internship programs: to organize selection processes, assess competencies, and identify candidates for different opportunities.
* Temporary staffing and outsourcing companies: to maintain previously assessed professionals who are available for new demands.
* Internal recruitment programs: to identify employees who match opportunities for internal mobility, promotion, and professional development.

From a commercial perspective, the platform can explore different monetization models:

* Subscription-based SaaS: monthly or annual fees based on the number of job openings, recruiters, registered candidates, and available features.
* Hiring volume plans: fees based on the number of recruitment processes or candidates assessed.
* Enterprise plans: licensing for large organizations with multiple departments, business units, and HR structures.
* Assessment-based pricing: sale of credits for conducting technical, behavioral, and specialized assessments.
* Corporate integrations: fees for integration with HR systems, ATS, ERP, payroll platforms, and corporate directories.
* Talent intelligence services: provision of aggregated indicators on competencies, professional availability, and market trends, subject to privacy and data usage restrictions.
* White label: provision of the platform with customized branding and configurations for recruitment consultancies and staffing companies.
* Intelligent recruitment API: provision of resume extraction, assessment, matching, and candidate management services for integration into third-party platforms.
