# Intelligent Advisory Platform for Public Sector Managers

## Description

An artificial intelligence-based platform designed to support public sector managers in interpreting public demands, analyzing legislative proposals, and formulating public policies.

The solution would function as a virtual team of advisors, composed of multiple AI agents with distinct areas of expertise and perspectives. Each agent would independently analyze the same topics, considering legal, economic, social, environmental, administrative, and political dimensions.

The purpose would be to expand managers' analytical capabilities by providing different interpretations, arguments, evidence, and potential consequences for each decision. Rather than presenting a single answer, the platform would highlight consensus, disagreements, risks, and uncertainties, while keeping the final decision under human responsibility.

## Operation

The platform would aggregate information from citizen submissions, official documents, legislative bills, news articles, research, and government databases. Content would be organized by subject, region, and responsible government agency, facilitating the identification of recurring demands and issues requiring government intervention.

AI agents would receive the same information and analyze each issue according to their respective areas of expertise. In addition to technical agents, the platform could include agents configured around classical political ideologies, such as liberalism, conservatism, social democracy, socialism, libertarianism, and environmentalism. Each would apply its own principles and criteria when evaluating proposals and decisions.

The results would be consolidated into comparative reports containing supporting and opposing arguments, areas of agreement, disagreements, and potential impacts. The platform could also research legislation, examine experiences from other jurisdictions, estimate costs, and identify implementation risks.

All analyses would retain references to the sources consulted, assumptions adopted, model versions, and identified limitations. Managers could review the results, request additional assessments, and record their decisions, ensuring traceability and human oversight.

As a communication strategy, the platform could maintain an official profile on X dedicated to analyzing legislative issues. Each post would present a proposal and the positions simulated by the different political agents, outlining arguments and consequences from distinct ideological perspectives. The content would include references to original documents, allowing the public to consult the sources and form their own opinions.

## Applications

The solution could be used by municipal governments, state governments, federal agencies, legislative bodies, parliamentary offices, specialized consulting firms, and civil society organizations.

Its main applications would include screening and consolidating public demands, analyzing bills, evaluating public policies, comparing administrative alternatives, identifying socioeconomic impacts, and monitoring government priorities.

It could also support journalists, researchers, universities, and citizens interested in understanding the different arguments involved in public decision-making. Its presence on X would expand its reach through the publication of legislative analyses, contributing to civic education and increasing the platform's visibility.

Monetization could be achieved through institutional subscriptions, licensing based on the number of users or volume of analyses, implementation and integration contracts with government systems, as well as consulting and customization services.
