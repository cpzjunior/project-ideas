# Integrated Asset, Infrastructure, and Facilities Management Platform

## Description

A B2B SaaS platform for the integrated management of an organization's physical infrastructure, assets, facilities, and services. The solution centralizes the administration of properties, equipment, spaces, contracts, suppliers, maintenance, regulatory obligations, and facilities services in a single environment.

The platform enables companies to manage the entire lifecycle of their infrastructure, from asset registration and inventory to maintenance, compliance, modernization, or replacement. It also connects managers, operational teams, service providers, and suppliers, facilitating the execution and monitoring of activities related to the organization's physical operations.

The proposal is to provide a consolidated view of corporate infrastructure, enabling organizations to control costs, monitor performance indicators, anticipate maintenance needs, manage risks, and optimize the use of physical resources.

## Operation

- Property and Facility Management: centralized registration and administration of properties, branches, offices, stores, factories, distribution centers, and other facilities. Allows buildings to be organized by organization, region, business unit, floor, room, and area, maintaining information about physical characteristics, responsible parties, occupancy, location, and operational status.

- Asset and Equipment Management: inventory and monitoring of the organization's physical assets, including machinery, equipment, electrical installations, plumbing systems, HVAC systems, elevators, generators, security systems, and IT infrastructure. Allows users to record technical specifications, location, serial number, manufacturer, supplier, warranties, intervention history, useful life, and operational status.

- Maintenance Management: management of preventive, corrective, and predictive maintenance activities for facilities and equipment. Includes maintenance scheduling, work order creation, task assignment, execution monitoring, recording of materials used, deadline tracking, and intervention history. Allows recurring maintenance plans to be established and indicators such as availability, costs, and failure recurrence to be monitored.

- Building Compliance and Safety Management: control of technical, regulatory, and safety obligations related to buildings and facilities. Covers fire prevention and protection systems (PPCI), Fire Department Inspection Certificates (AVCB and CLCB), lightning protection systems (SPDA), NR-10, NR-12, NR-13, Preventive Maintenance, Operation, and Control Plans (PMOC), elevator inspections, licenses, technical reports, certificates, and other applicable requirements. Allows users to track deadlines, mandatory inspections, documents, responsible parties, non-conformities, and remediation plans.

- Facilities Management: administration of services required for the operation of facilities, including cleaning, maintenance, landscaping, front desk and reception, physical security, pest control, catering, waste management, and general services. Allows users to organize requests, contracts, teams, schedules, service levels, and quality indicators.

- Space and Occupancy Management: control of the utilization of the organization's physical environments and spaces, including meeting rooms, offices, workstations, auditoriums, common areas, and operational spaces. Allows users to monitor occupancy, availability, reservations, internal relocations, and infrastructure utilization, supporting decisions regarding expansion, reorganization, and space optimization.

- Supplier and Service Provider Management: registration, qualification, and monitoring of companies responsible for maintenance, installations, construction projects, and facilities services. Includes documentation control, qualifications, contracts, evaluations, performance indicators, and service history. Allows operational requests to be connected to qualified suppliers capable of fulfilling them.

- Contract Management: administration of contracts related to properties, maintenance, facilities, leases, technical services, and equipment supply. Allows users to monitor contract terms, price adjustments, renewals, obligations, service levels, contracted amounts, and scheduled deliverables, with alerts for expiration dates and outstanding issues.

- Procurement and Quotation Management: management of requests for the purchase of materials, equipment, and services related to infrastructure. Allows users to request quotations, compare proposals, evaluate suppliers, submit approvals, and track purchase orders. Can integrate requirements identified in the maintenance and facilities modules directly into the procurement process.

- Construction and Project Management: planning and monitoring of renovations, expansions, technical upgrades, modernization initiatives, and infrastructure projects. Includes scope control, schedules, budgets, suppliers, progress measurements, execution stages, technical documentation, and deliverable acceptance. Allows users to monitor the physical and financial progress of projects.

- Ticket and Request Management: provision of a portal where employees and facility users can submit requests related to infrastructure and building services. Includes ticket creation and classification, prioritization, assignment to responsible teams, SLA monitoring, communication with requesters, and service evaluations.

- Infrastructure Financial Management: control of costs associated with the operation and maintenance of physical infrastructure. Allows users to monitor expenses by property, business unit, asset, supplier, contract, and service category. Includes budget planning, CAPEX and OPEX monitoring, variance analysis, expense allocation, and future cost projections.

- Energy and Sustainability Management: monitoring of electricity, water, gas, and other resources consumed by facilities. Allows users to record consumption indicators, identify waste, compare performance across business units, monitor energy efficiency initiatives, and track environmental indicators, including waste generation and emissions.

- Operational Intelligence and Recommendations: use of operational data to identify failure patterns, predict maintenance needs, estimate future costs, detect underperforming assets, and recommend modernization or replacement actions. Allows management indicators to be consolidated and recommendations to be generated for infrastructure optimization.

- Management Dashboards and KPIs: provision of dashboards for consolidated infrastructure monitoring, including asset availability, pending maintenance, operating costs, compliance status, supplier performance, SLA compliance, resource consumption, budget execution, and indicators by business unit or region.

- Field Operations Mobile Application: provision of a mobile interface for in-house teams and service providers to perform activities directly at facilities. Allows users to view work orders, access asset information, record inspections, attach photographs, collect signatures, update statuses, and document interventions, including support for offline operations.

## Applications

- Banks and Financial Institutions: management of branches, offices, administrative centers, critical infrastructure, equipment, building security, maintenance, and geographically distributed suppliers.

- Manufacturing Companies: management of industrial facilities, machinery, equipment, utility systems, maintenance, operational safety, compliance, and modernization projects.

- Hospitals and Healthcare Institutions: administration of hospital infrastructure, critical facilities, HVAC systems, generators, elevators, equipment, maintenance, and regulatory obligations.

- Shopping Centers: management of common areas, building systems, retail stores, contracts, suppliers, security, maintenance, and facilities services.

- Retail Chains: centralized administration of stores, kiosks, distribution centers, and offices, allowing processes to be standardized and costs and operating conditions across multiple locations to be monitored.

- Commercial and Corporate Buildings: management of buildings, common areas, maintenance contracts, service providers, services, inspections, and condominium expenses.

- Logistics Companies: management of distribution centers, warehouses, material handling equipment, refrigeration systems, infrastructure, and operational maintenance.

- Universities and Educational Institutions: administration of campuses, buildings, laboratories, classrooms, equipment, shared spaces, and infrastructure services.

- Facilities and Maintenance Companies: use of the platform to manage client contracts, operational teams, work orders, assets under their responsibility, and service performance indicators.
