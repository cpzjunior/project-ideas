# Physical and Digital Art Marketplace

## Description

A marketplace for the sale of physical and digital artworks, connecting independent artists with people interested in purchasing artwork or commissioning custom creations.

The proposal is to provide an environment where artists can showcase their work, build a catalog, sell their creations, and receive commissions from customers. The marketplace would function as both a showcase and a sales channel, covering everything from digital illustrations and paintings to sculptures, photographs, and other artistic forms.

## Operation

Artists can create profiles, publish their artwork, and define prices and sales terms. Customers can browse the catalog, discover artists, and purchase available works.

In addition to selling existing artwork, the platform allows customers to request custom creations. Customers describe what they want, provide references, and define characteristics such as style, dimensions, and budget. Artists can accept the request and submit a commercial proposal.

The platform may support different categories:

- Physical Art: paintings, drawings, sculptures, photographs, handicrafts, and other tangible artworks.
- Digital Art: illustrations, concept art, animations, digital photography, and other electronic creations.
- Custom Commissions: requests for exclusive artworks developed according to customer specifications.
- Collectible Artworks: sale of limited-edition, numbered pieces or artworks accompanied by certificates of authenticity.
- Digital Ownership: possibility of using technologies such as NFTs to register the authenticity, ownership, and sales history of specific artworks.

The platform may also offer artist rating features, order tracking, payment processing through an intermediary, and protection mechanisms for buyers and sellers.

## Applications

The marketplace serves independent artists who want to expand their commercial exposure and find buyers without relying exclusively on social media or traditional galleries.

For customers, it provides centralized access to different artistic styles, allowing them to purchase existing artworks or commission artists for customized projects.

It can also serve companies and professionals interested in commissioning illustrations, visual identities, sculptures, decorative pieces, and other artistic productions.
