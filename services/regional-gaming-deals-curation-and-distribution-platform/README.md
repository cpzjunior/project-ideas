# Regional Gaming Deals Curation and Distribution Platform

## Description

A service for discovering, curating, and distributing promotions on video games and digital games, focusing on prices, commercial terms, and relevant deals for each regional market.

The proposal aims to make it easier to discover purchasing opportunities by bringing together promotions from different stores and digital distribution platforms, while taking into account the specific characteristics of each country, such as currency, regional pricing, product availability, taxes, and local promotional campaigns.

Unlike traditional price aggregators, the service aims to provide a localized experience by presenting deals contextualized for the user's market, rather than simply converting international prices into their currency.

The product's primary channel is a periodic newsletter featuring curated relevant deals, while also potentially leveraging distribution channels such as WhatsApp, Telegram, applications, and notifications.

The initiative aims to transform deal discovery into a simple, recurring experience, helping consumers find great opportunities without having to individually monitor multiple stores and platforms.

## How It Works

The service collects, organizes, and analyzes information about prices and promotions available across different stores and digital game distribution platforms.

Based on this information, it identifies relevant opportunities for each regional market and distributes curated selections of deals through communication channels.

The platform may offer features such as:

- **Localized Pricing:** Displaying prices in the local currency, taking into account the actual prices charged in each region, rather than relying exclusively on currency conversion.
- **Regional Promotions:** Identifying campaigns, discounts, and deals specific to each country or market, including local commercial events.
- **Cross-Regional Price Comparison:** Comparing international prices to provide context for pricing differences, using currency conversion as supplementary information.
- **Price History:** Tracking price trends and identifying historical lows or the best recent opportunities in each market.
- **Deal Curation:** Selecting games with significant discounts, considering final price, quality, popularity, ratings, and price history.
- **Periodic Newsletter:** Sending newsletters featuring the best deals, highlights, opportunities, and recommendations, organized by frequency and relevance.
- **Multichannel Distribution:** Making deals available through email, WhatsApp, Telegram, applications, and other communication channels.
- **Regional Personalization:** Adapting content based on country, currency, language, available stores, and consumer preferences.
- **Cross-Platform Deals:** Supporting games for PC, consoles, and other digital platforms, including Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, and Nintendo eShop.
- **Additional Opportunities:** Promoting free games, bundles, DLC promotions, subscriptions, and special promotional campaigns.
- **Deal Alerts:** Notifications about significant discounts, historically low prices, and limited-time promotions.
- **Affiliate Monetization:** Using affiliate links and commercial partnerships with stores, publishers, and distributors, where available.

The service can initially operate with a focus on a single market, such as Brazil, and subsequently expand its coverage to other countries, leveraging a collection, analysis, and distribution infrastructure that can be adapted to different regions.

## Applications

The platform serves digital gaming consumers who want to find great purchasing opportunities without having to individually track promotions across different stores.

It can be used by PC and console gamers, consumers interested in indie games, technology enthusiasts, and people who frequently follow new releases and promotional campaigns.

The newsletter can serve as the primary engagement product, providing a periodic selection of relevant deals and editorial content about purchasing opportunities.

Messaging channels, such as WhatsApp and Telegram, can complement the experience by enabling the distribution of urgent deals, price alerts, and promotional summaries.

The model can also serve different geographic markets, allowing each region to have its own selection of deals, currency, language, stores, and promotional calendar.

As the operation grows, the service can evolve into a deals portal, price comparison tool, application, or platform for discovering commercial opportunities for digital products.
