# Newsletter-Based Content Aggregator

## Description

A service that allows users to keep track of new content published across different platforms and websites through newsletters delivered directly to their email inbox.

The goal is to centralize the monitoring of content creators, YouTube channels, Substack publications, blogs, and technical websites in a single place, eliminating the need to access each platform individually to check for new publications.

## How It Works

Users register the content sources they want to follow by providing the channels, profiles, websites, or RSS feeds they are interested in.

The service monitors these sources and identifies new publications, such as videos, articles, newsletters, and other content.

Whenever new publications are identified, the system gathers the corresponding content and sends the user an email newsletter containing titles, descriptions, publication dates, and links to access the original material.

The service may offer different configuration options:

- Registration of YouTube channels, Substack publications, blogs, and technical websites.
- Support for RSS and Atom feeds.
- Organization of sources by category or topic.
- Configuration of newsletter delivery frequency.
- Consolidation of multiple publications into a single email.
- Customization of delivery times and topics of interest.

## Applications

The service is particularly useful for people who follow many content creators, professionals who need to stay up to date in a specific field, and users who want to consume information without relying on social media recommendation algorithms.

It can be used to follow news and updates about technology, software architecture, programming, artificial intelligence, financial markets, science, and other topics.

It can also function as a personal curation tool, allowing each user to build their own information hub from selected sources.

## References

- YouTube: a video platform that provides channels and mechanisms for identifying new publications.
- Substack: a platform for publishing and distributing newsletters.
- RSS: a content distribution technology that allows users to follow updates from websites and blogs.
- Feedly: a content aggregator that allows users to follow publications from different sources in one place.
- Inoreader: a platform for aggregating and monitoring RSS feeds and other content sources.
