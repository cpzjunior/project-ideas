

# Social Commerce Platform with Collective Cashback and Gamification

## Description

An e-commerce platform based on collective cashback and gamification, in which the financial benefit received by consumers increases as the total purchase volume of a given product grows over a specified period.

The concept combines e-commerce, social interaction, and financial incentives, creating a model in which consumers themselves help increase sales and, consequently, maximize the benefits received by all participants.

Unlike traditional group-buying models, consumers do not need to wait for a group to form or for a minimum number of participants to be reached before making a purchase. They purchase the product at the advertised price and receive cashback proportional to the total sales volume achieved by the campaign.

The greater the number of buyers, the higher the cashback percentage distributed among participants.

The platform also incorporates gamification and social sharing mechanisms, encouraging consumers to promote products, invite friends, and participate in collective campaigns.

The goal is to create a commercial ecosystem in which consumers are incentivized to purchase and share offers, while sellers and manufacturers can expand their products' reach, increase sales volume, and reduce customer acquisition costs.

## How It Works

Sellers and manufacturers list their products on the platform, defining prices, commercial terms, campaign periods, and the budget allocated to collective cashback.

Each product may have its own campaign, with cashback tiers linked to the total sales volume achieved during the campaign period.

Consumers access the platform, find products of interest, and view campaign terms, including the current cashback percentage and the targets required to unlock higher rewards.

After making a purchase, consumers automatically become participants in the campaign and receive the cashback corresponding to the collective result at the end of the established period.

The platform may offer features such as:

- Integrated marketplace: product catalog covering different categories, allowing sellers and manufacturers to sell directly to consumers.
- Collective cashback campaigns: campaigns in which the cashback percentage progressively increases as the number of units sold grows.
- Collective targets: sales objectives that unlock new cashback tiers for all participants.
- Progress indicator: real-time visualization of the sales volume achieved, current cashback percentage, and distance to the next target.
- Offer sharing: ability to share products and campaigns through social networks, messaging apps, and personalized links.
- Invitations and referrals: mechanisms that encourage consumers to invite friends to participate in campaigns, potentially offering additional rewards for successful referrals.
- Gamification: use of challenges, achievements, rankings, targets, and special campaigns to encourage participation and sharing.
- Individual cashback: digital wallet for tracking accumulated, available, and received amounts from each campaign.
- Campaign history: tracking of purchases made, collective results, and rewards earned.
- Flash campaigns: limited-time offers that encourage purchases to be concentrated within specific time windows.
- Targeted campaigns: offers directed at specific audiences based on interests, location, purchase history, and preferences.
- Participant rankings: consumer rankings based on their contributions to campaign promotion and new customer acquisition.
- Seller dashboard: environment for tracking sales, participants, conversions, campaign results, and amounts allocated to cashback.
- Commercial intelligence: analysis of consumer behavior, campaign performance, effectiveness of promotional channels, and identification of business opportunities.
- Personalized recommendations: use of artificial intelligence to recommend products and campaigns based on users' interests and purchasing habits.

An example would be a campaign to sell a smartphone advertised at a price of R$ 2,000.

The campaign establishes the following cashback tiers:

| Total sales volume | Cashback |
| ---------------------- | -------- |
| Up to 99 units        | 2%       |
| 100 to 249 units     | 4%       |
| 250 to 499 units     | 6%       |
| 500 units or more   | 10%      |

A consumer who purchases the product automatically participates in the campaign.

If 500 units are sold by the end of the campaign period, all eligible participants receive 10% cashback, regardless of when they made their purchases.

In this scenario, each consumer receives R$ 200 in cashback.

This dynamic creates an incentive for buyers themselves to promote the campaign, since every new purchase contributes to increasing the collective benefit.

## Applications

The solution serves consumers interested in obtaining better commercial terms through collective purchasing, sellers looking to increase sales, and manufacturers seeking alternative distribution and customer acquisition strategies.

It can be used across different segments:

- Electronics and technology: smartphones, computers, accessories, and home appliances.
- Fashion and apparel: clothing, footwear, accessories, and sporting goods.
- Home and decoration: furniture, household items, decorative products, and equipment.
- Beauty and personal care: cosmetics, hygiene products, and personal care products.
- Supermarkets and food: frequently purchased consumer goods, food, and non-alcoholic beverages.
- Sports and leisure: sporting equipment, accessories, and recreational products.
- Manufacturer products: specific campaigns for product launches, distribution expansion, and inventory clearance.

From a commercial perspective, the platform can explore different monetization models:

- Sales commissions: percentage charged to sellers on completed transactions.
- Advertising and sponsored campaigns: manufacturers and brands can pay to feature products and campaigns.
- Funded promotional campaigns: suppliers can allocate specific budgets to increase cashback and stimulate demand.
- Commercial plans for sellers: subscriptions providing access to advanced campaign management tools, commercial intelligence, and performance analytics.
- Market intelligence services: provision of aggregated analyses of purchasing behavior, conversion rates, and campaign performance.
