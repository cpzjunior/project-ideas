# Renovation as a Service Platform

## Description

A platform that provides integrated management of residential and commercial renovations, supporting clients from initial planning through project completion.

The goal is to centralize renovation management in a single environment, coordinating professionals, suppliers, material procurement, budgets, and execution schedules.

The objective is to reduce the financial and operational risks involved in renovation projects by providing greater predictability in costs, timelines, and execution quality.

Instead of having to individually hire and manage each professional and supplier, clients can use the platform as an intermediary and project coordinator, providing an integrated hiring and monitoring experience.

## How It Works

The client provides information about the desired renovation, including the property, the areas involved, the initial scope, the available budget, and expected timeline.

The platform assists with planning, budget structuring, and defining the stages required for execution.

Once the project is approved, suitable professionals and suppliers are selected, activities are organized, and execution is coordinated.

The platform may offer features such as:

- Renovation planning: assessment of requirements, definition of scope, stages, and priorities.
- Integrated budgeting: cost estimates for labor, materials, services, and additional expenses.
- Professional hiring: selection and coordination of architects, engineers, builders, electricians, plumbers, and other specialists.
- Supplier management: quotation requests, comparison, and procurement of materials and equipment.
- Execution schedule: planning of activities, dependencies, and expected dates.
- Financial management: tracking of expenses, payments, approved budgets, and scope changes.
- Project monitoring: recording of photographs, progress updates, and validation of completed stages.
- Quality control: monitoring of services and verification of expected deliverables.
- Issue management: identification of problems, recording of changes, and assessment of their financial and timeline impacts.
- Renovation handover: final checklist, documentation, warranties, and project closure.

The platform can support different contracting models, ranging from the individual management of professionals to integrated renovation packages with previously agreed budgets and schedules.

## Applications

The solution serves individuals and businesses that want to carry out renovations without having to directly manage the complexity of coordinating professionals, suppliers, and activities.

It can be used for renovations of apartments, houses, commercial properties, offices, and business establishments.

It can also serve real estate investors who purchase properties for renovation and resale, providing greater control over project costs, timelines, and profitability.

Another potential audience consists of property owners who want to renovate properties for rental purposes, seeking standardization, cost control, and faster execution.
