# Physical Consignment Management and Connection Platform

## Description

A B2B SaaS platform that connects consignors and consignees, facilitating the integrated management of physical product consignment operations.

The solution allows consignees to request products for sale and consignors to evaluate, approve, and make goods available. The platform centralizes the entire commercial and operational relationship between the parties, covering everything from consignment requests and approvals to the physical movement of products, inventory control, sales recording, returns, and financial settlements.

In addition to managing operations, the platform provides an internal business network, allowing consignors to find new distribution channels and consignees to discover suppliers and products available for sale.

## How It Works

- Participant Registration and Management: registration of consignors and consignees, including business profiles, company information, business segments, location, and commercial terms. Each participant can maintain relationships with multiple partners.

- Consignment Network: an internal environment for discovering suppliers, products, and business opportunities. Consignors can advertise products available for consignment, while consignees can search for and find partners interested in making goods available. The platform allows users to initiate partnership requests and establish new business relationships.

- Consignment Request and Approval: consignees can browse available products, select goods and quantities, and submit consignment requests. Consignors receive these requests and can approve, reject, or adjust the requested terms and quantities, retaining control over the availability of their products.

- Shipment and Receipt Management: management of goods shipment and receipt, including product identification, quantities, batches, responsible parties, and dates. Allows consignors to record dispatches and consignees to confirm receipt, identifying any discrepancies.

- Product Traceability: tracking the location and status of each product throughout the entire consignment lifecycle. Includes records of movements, transfers, sales, returns, losses, and damage, maintaining a history of responsibilities and events. QR codes and barcodes can be used to facilitate product identification and physical inventory checks.

- Consigned Inventory Management: control of products made available to each consignee, including quantities received, available, sold, returned, and pending. Allows users to monitor inventory distributed across different partners and identify replenishment or collection needs.

- Sales Recording and Management: recording of sales made by consignees, allowing automatic updates to product balances and amounts owed to consignors. Supports integration with POS systems, ERPs, and e-commerce platforms.

- Automated Financial Settlement: automatic calculation of operational results, considering products sold, commissions, margins, returns, losses, and established commercial terms. Generates settlement statements, calculates amounts due, allows reconciliation between the parties, and tracks payments, transfers, and outstanding financial obligations.

- Commercial Terms Management: definition of specific rules for each business relationship, including prices, commissions, margins, consignment periods, settlement frequency, responsibilities, and return conditions.

- Operational Dashboards and Metrics: provides consignors and consignees with performance indicators, including the volume of products distributed, sales made, inventory turnover, stagnant goods, outstanding amounts, profitability, and partners' commercial performance.

- Business Intelligence and Recommendations: use of operational data to identify replenishment opportunities, products with greater sales potential, best-performing partners, and opportunities to expand the consignment network.

## Applications

- Manufacturers and Brands: companies that make their products available to stores, resellers, and business partners, using consignment as a distribution and sales channel.

- Distributors and Wholesalers: companies that supply goods to small retailers and resellers, maintaining control over distributed products and sales results.

- Sales Representatives: professionals and companies that receive products for demonstration and sale, with inventory, sales, and commission management.

- Fashion and Accessories: brands of clothing, footwear, bags, jewelry, and semi-fine jewelry that work with multi-brand stores, boutiques, and independent resellers.

- Cosmetics and Beauty Products: manufacturers and distributors that make products available to consultants, representatives, and small retail businesses.

- Books and Publications: publishers and distributors that work with bookstores, stationery stores, and independent points of sale.

- Regional and Handmade Products: small manufacturers and producers that use partner establishments as sales channels.
